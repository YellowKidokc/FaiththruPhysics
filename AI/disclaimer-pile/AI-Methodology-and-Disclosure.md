# AI Methodology and AI Use Disclosure

## Repository-wide disclosure text

FaithThruPhysics.com and the Theophysics Registry are developed by a single researcher (David Lowe) working in theoretical unification physics, theology, and consciousness studies. This is not a funded laboratory or an institutional team. It is one person with a full-time engineering career, building this framework in the margins.

Natural Language Processing (NLP) and Large Language Models (LLMs) are used extensively throughout this project — including but not limited to: semantic analysis of articles, claim extraction and proof-mapping, domain classification, readability scoring, SEO metadata generation, structured data formatting (Schema.org/JSON-LD), and cross-referencing between series and isomorphisms.

However, every output is reviewed, verified, and approved by the human author. The AI systems function as research assistants and structural scribes, not as originators of the theoretical framework. The Theophysics axioms, the Lagrangian-Like Construct (LLC), the Master Equation dynamics, and the theological arguments are human-developed. The AI helps organize, formalize, and surface connections that might otherwise be missed in a one-person operation. It does not generate the core physics or the theological claims.

### What this means for readers
- The structure (Schema.org markup, station analysis, classification tags, metadata) is heavily AI-assisted.
- The content (arguments, derivations, evidence chains, interpretations of Scripture and physics) is human-authored and human-verified.
- The connections (cross-series isomorphisms, domain bridges, formal surface mappings) are a collaborative product: the AI surfaces candidates; the human validates and selects.

**Limitations:** Because this project operates with limited time and no dedicated research staff, some pages may carry analysis artifacts (scores, classifications, automated summaries) that reflect the AI's best interpretation rather than a peer-reviewed consensus. These are explicitly labeled where they appear. The framework itself is presented as a working hypothesis seeking rigorous engagement, not as settled doctrine.

In short: The thinking is human. The organization is human-AI collaborative. The infrastructure is AI-assisted. We believe this is the most honest and efficient way to advance a unified framework at this scale with these resources.
