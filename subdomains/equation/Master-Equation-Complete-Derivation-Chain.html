A5.1 — Observation Requirement. Possibility becomes actuality only through observation. Quantum mechanics demonstrates this empirically: the wave function does not collapse without measurement. A universe with no observers is a universe where nothing is actual.
BC4 — Three Observers Required. A single observer cannot verify itself. Two observers create a deadlock. Three observers permit triangulation — independent verification with no privileged frame. This is the minimum observer structure for stable actuality.
BC6 — Infinite Energy Source. A finite system runs down (Second Law). If the system is to be sustained, energy must enter from a source that does not deplete. The source must be inexhaustible.

A1.3 — Information Primacy. Existence + Distinction = Information. If things exist and are distinguishable, then information exists. Information is not an add-on to physics — it is what existence-with-distinction is.
T3.1 — Coherence Cannot Self-Increase. A finite closed system cannot generate final coherence from within itself. This is the moral analogue of the Second Law: entropy increases in closed systems, coherence does not. A system that is falling apart cannot put itself back together using only its own resources.

BC7 — Information Conservation. Information is never truly destroyed. It may be scattered, encrypted, made inaccessible — but it is not annihilated. This is required by unitarity in quantum mechanics and by the structure of the Master Equation.
BC8 — Voluntary Coupling. Agents must couple to external repair voluntarily. Forced repair violates the observer structure — it collapses the agent's measurement freedom, which destroys the observation axiom (A5.1). Grace that overrides free will is self-defeating.

The irreducible spine: Existence → Distinction → Information → Substrate → Self-grounding → Observation → Triadic closure → Infinite source → External grace → Voluntary coupling.
