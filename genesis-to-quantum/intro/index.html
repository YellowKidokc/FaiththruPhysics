The Argument

"Information is ontologically primitive — not matter, not energy, not spacetime."

Ten articles. Ten super-factors. One master equation. The series reads Genesis 2–3 as the first quantum measurement protocol and tracks the consequences through thermodynamics, decoherence, the Born Rule, retrocausal atonement, and the structure of time itself. Every article is rated Publication Grade (A) by the framework's own audit pipeline. Every article is knowledge-dominant. Every claim is falsifiable or marked as speculative.

A Note on What This Series Claims

Throughout these ten articles, structures from quantum mechanics, thermodynamics, and information theory are mapped onto structures from Scripture with a precision that we believe is not coincidental. But structural parallel is not identity. Isomorphism is not proof of mechanism.

Where a mapping is demonstrated, we say so. Where identity is proposed, we say that too — and we mark it. Where we don’t yet know whether the correspondence is identity, causation, or coincidence, we hold the tension honestly rather than collapsing it for rhetorical force.

Every article contains its own audit. Every overclaim we caught is flagged. The kill conditions are real — five concrete observations that would falsify the framework. We put the gun on the table. That is the standard this series holds itself to.
