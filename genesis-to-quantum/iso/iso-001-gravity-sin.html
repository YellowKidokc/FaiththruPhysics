⚠ Falsification Criteria

This claim carries its own kill condition. If any of the following are demonstrated, the isomorphism is demoted to Correspondence (Level 2) or Analogy (Level 1):

  • › Sin as repulsive force: If empirical data shows sin "pushes" moral agents toward God rather than pulling them away, the gravitational mapping collapses.
  • › Linear decay pattern: If moral decay follows a linear (not inverse-square) distance dependence, the core structural claim fails.
  • › Gradual conversion: If grace operates through gradual accumulation rather than threshold exceedance of binding energy, the escape-velocity analogy breaks.
