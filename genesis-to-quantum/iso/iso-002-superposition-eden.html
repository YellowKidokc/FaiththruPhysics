Section 1: The Five-Property Mapping
01

The Five-Property Mapping

Each of five quantum superposition properties maps to five Genesis properties. All five must hold — 4/5 is analogy, not isomorphism.

# Quantum Superposition (Physics) Pre-Fall Moral State (Genesis 2-3)
1 Multiple eigenstates coexist simultaneously The particle exists in all allowed states at once ↔ Obedience and transgression coexist as genuine possibilities Genesis 2:9 — Tree of Life and Tree of Knowledge both present
2 No definite outcome without measurement The system has no determined value until observed ↔ The moral state is structurally undetermined Genesis 2:16-17 — Death/entropy exist only as boundary conditions ("in the day you eat..."), not actualized states
3 Collapse is irreversible Once measured, the superposition is destroyed ↔ The Fall is irreversible by human means Genesis 3:22-24 — Cherubim guard the way back; no return to Eden
4 Observer's measurement basis determines outcome The choice of measurement selects which eigenstate collapses into reality ↔ The agent's choice determines which moral state actualizes Genesis 3:6 — "She took... she ate... she gave"
5 Post-collapse dynamics qualitatively differ from pre-collapse Classical physics governs post-measurement; quantum rules no longer apply ↔ Post-Fall dynamics qualitatively differ from pre-Fall Genesis 3:7 — "The eyes of both were opened" — new regime of shame, toil, death

Physicists call that quantum superposition. They discovered it in 1927. Werner Heisenberg, Niels Bohr, the whole Copenhagen crew — brilliant men working at the edge of what anyone had ever understood about matter. They thought they were describing atoms. They were describing Eden.
