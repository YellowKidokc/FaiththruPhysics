## The Five-Property Mapping {#the-five-property-mapping .section-title}

::: section-line
:::
::::

Each of five wavefunction collapse properties maps to five properties of the Fall. **All five must hold** --- 4/5 is analogy, not isomorphism.

  \#   Wavefunction Collapse (Physics)                                                                                                                                                                The Fall (Genesis 3)
  ---- ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ --- -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1    **Instantaneous --- no gradual transition** [Collapse is a discrete event, not a continuous process. One moment: superposition. Next moment: eigenstate.]{.verse-ref}                      ↔   **Instantaneous --- discrete state transition** [Genesis 3:7 --- \"Then\" (Hebrew: וַיִּפָּקְחוּ) marks an immediate shift. Eyes opened, not gradually adjusted.]{.verse-ref}
  2    **Irreversible --- cannot return to superposition** [Once measured, the quantum state has collapsed. No physical process restores the original superposition.]{.verse-ref}                 ↔   **Irreversible --- cherubim block return to Eden** [Genesis 3:24 --- God stations cherubim with a flaming sword. The way back is physically barred.]{.verse-ref}
  3    **Non-local --- affects entangled systems** [Collapse of one entangled particle instantaneously determines the state of its partner, regardless of distance.]{.verse-ref}                  ↔   **Non-local --- \"both\" experience it simultaneously** [Genesis 3:7 --- \"The eyes of both were opened.\" Federal headship: Adam\'s collapse is Eve\'s collapse. One act, universal effect.]{.verse-ref}
  4    **Qualitative regime change** [Pre-collapse: Schrödinger evolution (unitary, deterministic). Post-collapse: Born Rule probabilities. Different governing dynamics entirely.]{.verse-ref}   ↔   **Qualitative regime change** [Pre-Fall: direct communion with God, life trajectory. Post-Fall: mediated access, death trajectory, toil, pain (Gen 3:16-19).]{.verse-ref}
  5    **Information gained but freedom lost** [Measurement yields a definite value --- information is gained. But the superposition\'s degrees of freedom are destroyed.]{.verse-ref}            ↔   **Knowledge gained but innocence lost** [Genesis 3:7 --- \"They knew they were naked.\" Knowledge of good and evil is gained. Innocence --- the moral superposition --- is destroyed.]{.verse-ref}

:::: quote-block
\"Then the eyes of both were opened, and they knew that they were naked.\" Five words in Hebrew. Instantaneous. Irreversible. Non-local. Regime-changing. Knowledge gained, freedom lost. That is not a metaphor for collapse. That is the same event described in a different language.
