Section 1: Domains

Domain Specifications

⚛
Domain A — Physics
Lowe Coherence Equation
dC/dt = O·G(1−C) − S·C — a logistic-type ODE governing the time evolution of coherence. Five variables:

C = coherence (0 to 1)
O = openness / observer coupling (0 to 1)
G = grace field strength (external input, always > 0)
S = entropy / noise (always > 0)
(1−C) = capacity term (growth slows as coherence increases)

Stable equilibrium at Ceq = (O·G) / (O·G + S). Three phase regimes are determined by the coupling parameter s.
✝
Domain B — Theology
Salvation & Sanctification
Salvation and sanctification dynamics as described across the New Testament epistles. The process by which a human being moves from spiritual death toward spiritual coherence with God.

The theological tradition has split into two camps — Calvinism (grace is sovereign, not earned) and Arminianism (free will matters, you can resist). The Lowe Coherence Equation dissolves this debate: Calvinism identified G. Arminianism identified O. Both are real variables in a two-variable product O·G. Neither camp is wrong — they are each holding one factor of a product.
Section 2: Three Phase Regimes
02

Three Phase Regimes

The coupling parameter s determines which regime the system occupies. Each regime maps to a distinct spiritual state with its own dynamics, trajectory, and scriptural signature.

Regime 1
Autonomous Decay
s = −1  |  dC/dt = −S·C
Pure exponential collapse. No coupling to external grace. The system decays to zero with no floor.

Romans 1:24–28 — "God gave them over." Pure decay with no floor. When the observer fully decouples from the grace field, coherence collapses exponentially.
Regime 2
Performance Mode
s ≈ 0  |  Ceq ≈ 1/3
Partial coupling. Effort without surrender. The equilibrium is low and the trajectory is exhausting.

Works-based religion — effort without surrender. Burnout trajectory. The system optimizes O (openness) at a fixed, low s. You can work harder, but you cannot outrun the entropy term.
Regime 3
Full Surrender
s = +1  |  dC/dt = G(1−C) − S·C
Growth toward high equilibrium. Full coupling to the grace field. The capacity term (1−C) provides natural saturation.

Philippians 2:12–13 — "Work out your salvation with fear and trembling, for it is God who works in you." The comma in that verse IS the equation.

⚠ Falsification Criteria — 6 Kill Conditions

This claim carries its own kill conditions. If any of the following are demonstrated, the isomorphism is demoted or destroyed:

  • › 1. O and G collapse to one variable: If openness and grace are shown to be the SAME variable, the equation reduces and the Calvinist-Arminian resolution fails.
  • › 2. CBT consistently outperforms surrender-based approaches: If recovery data shows CBT consistently beating AA and similar surrender-based programs, the s-change model fails.
  • › 3. C = 0 reachable with G > 0: If a system with S > 0 and G > 0 can reach C = 0, the "grace floor" claim fails. The equation mathematically forbids this.
  • › 4. Unobserved predicted behavior: If the equation predicts a behavior not observed in any spiritual tradition, the mapping suffers empirical falsification.
  • › 5. Augustine's four states fail to map: If Augustine's four states of the will don't map to four parameter regions of the equation, historical theological validation fails.
  • › 6. Neuroimaging contradicts three-regime prediction: If brain imaging data contradicts the three predicted regimes (decay, performance, surrender), biological falsification occurs.
