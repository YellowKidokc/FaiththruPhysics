The Measurement Isomorphism

The MDA Coherence Diagnostic is not a custom scoring system. It is a formal application of information-theoretic divergence measures to the problem of societal coherence. The channel score for each law is a log-ratio of constructive to destructive regime dominance:

Lᵢ = log( (Cᵢ + ε) / (Dᵢ + ε) )   [per channel]
χ_MDA = (1/N) · Σ wᵢ · Lᵢ   [aggregate]

This is structurally identical to the Kullback-Leibler divergence in stochastic thermodynamics, which measures how far a system has been driven from equilibrium. It is also isomorphic to Shannon channel capacity C = B · log₂(1 + S/N), where the constructive signal corresponds to signal power and the destructive signal corresponds to noise. This is not metaphor — it is the same mathematical structure applied to a different measurement domain.

Why Log-Ratio, Not Linear Difference

A linear difference (C − D) would weight a society where C=100 and D=90 the same as one where C=10 and D=0. The log-ratio captures the proportion, not the absolute gap. This matches information theory: what matters is the signal-to-noise ratio, not the absolute signal power. A society with modest institutions that are internally coherent (low C, lower D) scores higher than a society with massive institutions that are deeply corrupted (high C, higher D). This is physically correct.

The Weight Problem (Honest Statement)

The current implementation uses equal weights (wᵢ = 1 for all channels). This is a deliberate methodological choice, not an oversight. The framework predicts that the weights should be non-arbitrary — that the ten laws are not interchangeable and some channels should carry more diagnostic weight than others. However, until the weights are either derived from the mathematical structure or empirically fitted and shown to be unique, equal weighting is the most conservative assumption. Any weight tuning is an additional degree of freedom that must be justified, not assumed.

The Dynamics Equation

The channel scores Lᵢ describe the state — where a society is. The dynamics equation describes the motion — how fast it is moving toward coherence or collapse:

dS_m/dt = σ − W/T

Where σ is the rate of entropy production (damage accumulation), W is restorative work (grace, reform, renewal), and T is institutional resistance (bureaucratic friction, cultural inertia, system mass). This is inherited from irreversible thermodynamics (Onsager, 1931; Prigogine, 1967) and is not original to this framework.

The critical prediction: when T is large, even significant restorative work W is divided down to near-zero effectiveness. This explains why large empires can rot internally while surface statistics remain stable, and why restoration in heavily institutionalized societies requires disproportionate force — either spiritual renewal or existential threat.

The Permutation Test (Proposed)

The decisive empirical test for this framework is whether the ten-law channel decomposition outperforms arbitrary groupings of the same proxy variables. The protocol:

StepProcedure
1Gather all proxy variables across all ten channels
2Score χ_MDA using the ten-law channel assignments
3Randomly shuffle the same variables into ten arbitrary buckets
4Score the same way, record predictions for collapse/recovery/instability
5Repeat 1,000 times to build a null distribution
6Compare: if the ten-law grouping sits outside the 95th percentile of the null distribution, the channel decomposition is doing statistically significant structural work

This test has not yet been run. The framework makes an explicit prediction: the ten-law grouping will outperform random. If it does not, the framework fails its own test and the channel decomposition should be abandoned.

METHODOLOGICAL HONESTY

This framework measures constructive-regime versus destructive-regime dominance across observable proxies. It does not measure sin, grace, the Holy Spirit, or any spiritual state directly. The channel labels reference physics and theology because the framework claims structural isomorphism between these domains — but the measurements are sociological and economic, not spiritual. A high score does not mean a society is righteous. A low score does not mean a society is damned. It means the observable proxies for constructive coordination are being outpaced by the observable proxies for destructive dissolution. The explanation for why this is happening is a separate inquiry from the measurement that it is happening.
