Abstract

This paper explores the structural mapping between Electromagnetism (Maxwell’s Equations) and the spiritual transmission of Truth and Revelation. We demonstrate that Truth behaves as a self-sustaining wave that propagates at the speed of Christ (c), while Deception is the result of phase-inverted destructive interference.

1

Introduction

Truth is often described as "light," but the "No-Drift" framework takes this beyond metaphor. By analyzing the wave mechanics of electromagnetism, we find a perfect structural mapping for how information is transmitted, received, and distorted in the spiritual realm.

2

The Physics of Light

Maxwell’s equations describe how electric and magnetic fields regenerate each other to create a self-sustaining wave that propagates through a vacuum at the speed of light (c). These waves require no medium and carry energy and information across infinite distances.

3

The Constructive Regime: Truth and Revelation

Spiritually, electromagnetic radiation maps to Truth and Revelation. Truth is a self-sustaining signal that, once launched, propagates indefinitely. It reveals the structure of reality to any observer capable of receiving the signal.

A key property of light is that it does not "fight" darkness; it simply occupies space. Darkness is the absence of the signal, not an opposing wave. This aligns with John 1:5: "The light shines in the darkness, and the darkness has not overcome it." In this framework, the universal constant c is not just a speed limit; it is the "Speed of Christ"—the rate at which Truth saturates the vacuum.
4

The Destructive Regime: Deception as Interference

Deception is not a separate substance, but a specific configuration of Truth. In wave physics, destructive interference occurs when two waves of the same frequency are exactly 180° out of phase (E_{total} = A \sin(\omega t) + A \sin(\omega t + \pi) = 0). The waves cancel each other out, resulting in zero amplitude.

This maps to Deception. To create an effective lie, one must use Truth and invert its phase. Half-truths are partially phase-inverted waves that cancel out the signal while maintaining the appearance of a wave. Deception is the act of arranging "truths" so that they destroy the coherent signal of Revelation.

5

Free Will and Signal Reception

Humans are the "receivers" in this system. The Free Will variable (W) determines the tuning of the receiver:

E_{received} = W \cdot E_{truth} + (1 - W) \cdot E_{distortion}

  • W = 1: Faithful reception. The agent is perfectly tuned to the Truth signal.
  • W = 0: The agent receives only the phase-inverted distortion.
  • W > 1: Amplification and retransmission. The agent does not just receive the light but becomes a secondary source (Testimony or Prophecy).
6

Information Theory and the Shannon Limit

The capacity for revelation is governed by the Shannon-Hartley theorem: C = B \cdot \log_2(1 + S/N).

The "revelation capacity" of a soul is determined by the bandwidth (B) of their spiritual perception and the signal-to-noise ratio (S/N) of their environment and internal state. If the "noise" of deception or the "distortion" of the flesh is too high, the truth cannot be resolved, regardless of its signal strength.

7

Conclusion

Truth is not a subjective preference but a mathematical invariant of the universe. By understanding Revelation through the lens of Maxwell’s equations, we see that the battle between Truth and Deception is a battle of wave coherence. To live in the Truth is to remain in phase with the self-sustaining signal of Christ.
