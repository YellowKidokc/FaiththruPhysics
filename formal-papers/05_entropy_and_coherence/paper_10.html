The Physics of Synchronization

In physics, synchronization occurs when separate oscillators begin to move in unison due to a coupling force. The Kuramoto Model describes this phase transition: when the coupling strength exceeds a critical threshold (K_c), the system snaps from chaos into collective coherence. The order parameter (r) measures this degree of synchronization, ranging from 0 (chaos) to 1 (full coherence).

The Master Equation: Chi ($\chi$)

The Two Poles: Entropy vs. Coherence
