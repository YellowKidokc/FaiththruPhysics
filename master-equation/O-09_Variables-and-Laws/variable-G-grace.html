§1 The Dual Projection

Science

Negentropy Source / Open Thermodynamics

dS/dt = σext − σint

In open thermodynamic systems, entropy can decrease locally when external negentropic input exceeds internal entropy production. The system cannot self-organize without external energy/information input.

Projects into: Non-equilibrium thermodynamics, dissipative structures, Prigogine entropy production theory

God

Unmerited Favor / External Salvation

"For by grace you have been saved through faith. And this is not your own doing; it is the gift of God, not a result of works, so that no one may boast."

Grace is fundamentally external to the self. It cannot be generated from within the system (BC2). It responds to need — the theological equivalent of negentropic input preventing heat death of the soul.

Key Scripture: Ephesians 2:8-9

§3 Derived Axioms

1

Grace is externally sourced — no closed system can generate G internally.

Plain English: You cannot bootstrap yourself into grace. It always comes from outside the self-system.

2

Grace responds to need — ∂G/∂t increases as S approaches Sc.

Plain English: The more broken you are, the more grace flows. Not because you earned it, but because that is how the equation works.

3

Grace is idempotent — receiving grace does not diminish the source.

Plain English: Unlike physical resources, grace is not zero-sum. Giving it away does not reduce it.

4

Grace couples exclusively to Coherence — it cannot amplify Entropy.

Plain English: True grace always builds order. If it is creating chaos, it is not grace.

5

Grace and Faith are conjugate variables — [G, Q] = iℏeff.

Plain English: You cannot precisely know both how much grace you are receiving and how much faith you are exercising.
