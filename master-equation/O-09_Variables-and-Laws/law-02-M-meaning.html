Mass-energy says: matter IS energy. Meaning says: events ARE interpretation. Just as E=mc² unifies what seemed separate (matter/energy), meaning unifies happening and significance.

The conservation of meaning: meaning cannot be destroyed, only transformed. Trauma doesn't erase meaning—it compresses it into denser forms. Redemption decompresses it.
