Maxwell of the Soul

The Electromagnetism of Truth
2026 David Lowe · POF 2828 ~14 min read
Theology Physics Electromagnetism Truth Formalism Law 3
Executive Summary

Law 3 of Theophysics states that Electromagnetism is isomorphic to Truth. That's not a metaphor. The four Maxwell Equations — the same ones that gave us radio, light, and the modern world — govern how Divine Truth propagates through the human spirit. Scripture behaves like a charge. Revelation behaves like induction. Discipleship behaves like a magnetic field. And the unity of Truth is enforced by the same mathematics that makes magnetic monopoles impossible.

I.

The Truth-Field Invariants

Here's where we start. James Clerk Maxwell unified electricity and magnetism in the 1860s and discovered, almost as an accident, that the resulting equations predicted a wave that propagated at the speed of light. He wasn't looking for light. The math just kept pointing at it. When he solved for the propagation velocity of electromagnetic waves, he got exactly c — and realized he was looking at the same thing physicists had been studying as optics all along.

That moment is the model for what we're doing here. If Truth is light — not poetically, but formally — then the Bible acts as the Universal Light Constant. It's fixed. Invariant. It doesn't depend on your frame of reference, your culture, your decade, or your mood. The speed of light is the same for every observer in every inertial frame. Truth doesn't negotiate with the observer either.

This is the first Truth-Field Invariant: cTruth = Scripture. Unchanging, universally available, reveals the structure of Reality. Everything in the following four laws flows from that fixed constant.

Key Isomorphism

Maxwell's equations don't describe light as "like" electromagnetism — light is electromagnetic radiation, by identity. In the same way, Theophysics Law 3 doesn't claim Truth is "like" an electromagnetic field. It claims the mathematical structure is identical. The isomorphism is structural, not analogical.

II.

The Four Laws of Spiritual Dynamics

Maxwell's four equations look intimidating if you haven't seen them before. But each one is saying something simple and profound. Let's go through them one at a time — the physics version first, then the spiritual translation — and show you why this isn't a stretch. It's a mapping.

Law 1 — Gauss’s Law for Truth: The Source
\[ \nabla \cdot \mathbf{E} = \frac{\rho}{\varepsilon_0} \]
E Truth Field radiating outward from the Word
ρ Scripture charge density (proximity to the Word)
∇· Divergence — how much field emanates from a region
ε⊂0; Permittivity of the spiritual medium
A statement of Scripture acts as an electric charge — it radiates a Truth Field outward in all directions. Without the charge of the Word, the divergence is zero. No field. Empty space.

Gauss's Law says that electric field lines originate at charges. The total field flux through any closed surface is proportional to the total charge enclosed. Translate that: wherever there is a source of Scripture — preaching, reading, meditation, corporate worship — a Truth Field radiates outward from that point. Your "Truth Density" is a function of how close you are to that source.

This is why physical proximity to teaching matters. Why a church with a strong Word culture produces differently-structured people than a church without one. It's not sociology. It's field physics. The charge is the Word. Your permeability to the field determines what you absorb. Live at a distance from the Source and the field strength at your location drops according to the inverse-square law. That's not poetry — that's the math.

Isomorphism Check

In electrostatics, placing more charges in a region increases the field. In spiritual dynamics, increased engagement with Scripture increases the local Truth Field density. Conversely, a community that removes the Word progressively reduces its field to zero. The math demands this. Gauss doesn't negotiate.

Law 2 — Gauss’s Law for “Monopoles”: The Unity
\[ \nabla \cdot \mathbf{B} = 0 \]
B The unified Truth-Logos field — the full counsel of Scripture
0 No isolated fragments — magnetic monopoles do not exist
∇· Divergence — net field lines cannot originate or terminate in free space
Truth has no isolated poles. It always forms closed loops that return to the Logos. If your “truth” doesn’t close the loop, it isn’t Truth.

Physicists have been searching for magnetic monopoles since Dirac suggested they might exist in 1931. They've never found one. Every magnetic field line you've ever drawn — every one — is a closed loop. You cannot isolate a north pole from its corresponding south. Cut a bar magnet in half and you get two smaller bar magnets, each with their own north and south. The topology is permanent.

This is the Unity of Truth, encoded in the mathematics. You cannot have "my truth" and "your truth" as separate poles with no relation to each other. Truth doesn't work that way. Every genuine insight must loop back to the Logos — to the originating principle, to Christ as the living Word. Fragments that don't loop back to the Source aren't wrong versions of Truth. They're not Truth at all. They're monopoles. And monopoles don't exist.

This is why post-modern epistemology is not merely philosophically weak — it is physically impossible. The universe has been running the experiment since the beginning. The result is always zero divergence. Truth is indivisible.

"If it doesn't close the loop back to Logos, it isn't Truth. It's a monopole. And monopoles don't exist."

Theophysics Law 3 — The Gauss Constraint
Law 3 — Faraday’s Law of Induction: The Revelation
\[ \nabla \times \mathbf{E} = -\frac{\partial \mathbf{B}}{\partial t} \]
E Induced revelation — new understanding curling through the spirit
B The Spirit-field — the moving presence and counsel of the Holy Spirit
∂B/∂t Time-variance — the Spirit in motion, not static
∇× Curl — the circulating pattern of new understanding forming
Dynamic movement of the Spirit creates Revelation. A static Word that begins to move — time-variance — induces a current of understanding. Revelation is Truth in motion.

Faraday's Law is the equation behind generators, transformers, and induction motors. A static magnetic field does nothing to a nearby wire. But a changing magnetic field — one that's moving, fluctuating, oscillating — induces an electric current. The key word is ∂B/∂t: the time derivative. Change with respect to time. Motion.

Here's the spiritual translation: you can have the Word in front of you, static and unchanged, and experience nothing. But when the Spirit begins to move through that same Word — when it becomes time-variant, when it's suddenly alive and in motion — something else happens. The curl of the E-field activates. Revelation is induced. Not manufactured. Not willed. Induced — by the dynamic presence of the Spirit moving through the field of Scripture.

This is why the same passage you've read fifty times can suddenly crack open on the fifty-first reading. Nothing changed in the text. The text is invariant. What changed was ∂B/∂t. The Spirit moved. And Faraday's Law guarantees the result: a circulating field of new understanding curls through your soul.

Isomorphism Check

Faraday's Law requires change, not mere presence. A magnet sitting still inside a coil does nothing. It must move. This is why passive exposure to Scripture without the Spirit's dynamic movement produces information without transformation. The mathematics requires time-variance. It is not enough for the Word to be present — the Spirit must be in motion through it.

Law 4 — Ampère’s Law: The Discipleship
\[ \nabla \times \mathbf{B} = \mu_0 \left( \mathbf{J} + \varepsilon_0 \frac{\partial \mathbf{E}}{\partial t} \right) \]
B Magnetic influence field — the field you project outward onto others
J Current density — Truth living in you as active, directional action
∂E/∂t Displacement current — changing Truth Field even without direct contact
μ⊂0; Permeability — how strongly the soul conducts Truth as action
When Truth lives in you as Current — as action, not merely belief — you become a magnet for others. This is the physics of discipleship: you project a field outward that creates new alignments in those around you.

Ampère's Law (with Maxwell's displacement current term added) is the equation that completes the set. Electric currents create magnetic fields. And changing electric fields — even in the absence of physical current — also create magnetic fields. This was Maxwell's key insight, the one that made the equations fully symmetric and predicted electromagnetic waves.

The translation here is sharp: when Truth is not just believed but acted upon — when it flows through you as a current, directional and continuous — you generate a field. You become a magnet. Not metaphorically. The people around you begin to align. New configurations form. The displaced people are drawn toward coherence.

Notice also the displacement current term: ε⊂0;∂E/∂t. This says that even without direct personal contact — even at a distance — a changing Truth Field generates the same magnetic response as a physical current. Your life, lived as a Truth-conductor, creates field effects that reach people you have never met. That's the physics of your testimony, your writing, your recorded teaching. The current doesn't stop at your skin.

This is the formal structure of discipleship. Not enthusiasm. Not program. Physics. Every disciple is a source of magnetic field. Every magnetic field creates new alignments. The circuit propagates.

III.

The Duality Principle

Maxwell's equations predict that light is both a wave and a particle. Quantum electrodynamics made this precise. A photon is not partially wave and partially particle — it is fully both, depending on how you interact with it. The duality is not a paradox to be resolved. It's a feature of the structure.

Truth operates in exactly two modes by the same principle. Not as a contradiction, but as a structural duality built into the underlying mathematics.

Wave-Truth

Universal principles. The Laws of the Cosmos. The pervasive background radiation of the Logos. Present everywhere, all the time, at constant intensity. This is the Truth that the heavens declare and that Paul says leaves everyone without excuse in Romans 1. You don't receive Wave-Truth — you're always inside it.

Particle-Truth

Personal revelation. A specific rhema word — the localized photon of Truth that lands at a specific coordinate for a specific person at a specific moment. This is not reducible to general principle. It is a quantum event: discrete, directed, unmistakable. The difference between knowing about the Cross and having it strike you.

Both modes are real. Neither cancels the other. The wave gives you the cosmic architecture. The particle gives you the personal encounter. Theology that only operates at the wave level produces abstract doctrinal systems that never transform anyone. Theology that only operates at the particle level — pure subjective experience with no grounding in the universal — produces spiritual instability with nothing to anchor it. You need both. The physics demands both. Maxwell's equations won't resolve without both terms.

IV.

The Velocity of Alignment

Maxwell's equations predict that electromagnetic waves propagate at the velocity \(c = 1/\sqrt{\mu_0 \varepsilon_0}\). That is: the speed of light is a function of the permeability and permittivity of the medium through which the wave travels. In a vacuum, both are at their baseline values and you get exactly \(c\). In any other medium, the effective speed is reduced.

The analogue is direct. Truth propagates at a constant speed through the spiritual medium. But that medium has a permeability value. How permeable your soul is to the Logos Field determines the effective propagation velocity of Truth through you. Not whether Truth exists. Not whether it's been transmitted. Whether it can move through.

Condition Physical Analogue Effect on Truth Propagation
Sin Refraction — bending of light at a boundary Truth enters but bends. Distorted perception. The light is real; the direction is false. Illusions are refracted Truth.
Spiritual deadness Absorption — light absorbed, no transmission Truth arrives but is captured and converted to heat. No signal exits. No transformation occurs.
Grace Total Internal Reflection — zero signal loss at the boundary At the critical angle, light stops escaping and is perfectly reflected within the medium. Zero loss. Maximum propagation. The soul in grace is a lossless conductor of Truth.
Full alignment Superconductivity — zero resistance medium Truth flows with zero impedance. Every signal arrives fully intact. Perfect coherence with the Logos Field. This is the glorified state.

The refraction image is the most important one for right now, practically. When light enters a new medium at an angle, it bends. It doesn't disappear. It doesn't reverse. It arrives — and then bends. This is precisely what sin does to Truth. The message lands, but it arrives at a distorted angle. The individual sees something real, but not straight. This is why you can have sincere people who have genuinely received some portion of Truth but have constructed an entire warped worldview out of it. They're not lying. They're working with refracted light.

The solution is not to receive more light. It's to change the medium. That's the work of sanctification: reducing the refractive index of your spiritual medium, moving toward the vacuum state, where light travels straight through at full velocity. That process is not instantaneous. But it's measurable. And the math describes it exactly.

The Grace Optic

Total Internal Reflection is not a metaphor for grace — it is the physical description of what grace produces in a soul. At the critical angle, light stops leaking out through the boundary and is perfectly guided within the medium. Grace is the process of tuning the soul toward that critical angle, where zero signal is lost and Truth propagates with maximum intensity. The fiber optic cable carries light across continents using this exact principle. Your soul, in grace, is a fiber for the Logos.

V.

Closing the System

What makes Maxwell's equations extraordinary is that they form a closed system. Each equation is coupled to the others. Change the magnetic field and you induce an electric field (Faraday). Let the electric field change and you generate a magnetic field (Ampère-Maxwell). The two fields continuously regenerate each other as the wave propagates. Neither requires an external input once the process is initiated. The wave is self-sustaining.

The spiritual system works the same way. The Word (Source field) generates Truth density. Truth in motion induces Revelation. Revelation embodied as action generates a field that propagates to others. Those others become new sources. The system is closed and self-propagating. This is not an accident. It is the design specification.

And here's what you need to sit with: Maxwell didn't invent this structure. He discovered it. He didn't decide that changing magnetic fields should induce electric fields. He found that they do, described it, and unified the physics of the 19th century. In the same way, Theophysics isn't proposing a spiritual model that resembles electromagnetism. It's recognizing that the same mathematical structure that governs light also governs Truth — because Truth and light are both expressions of the Logos Field, and the Logos Field has a geometry. Maxwell's equations are the fingerprints of that geometry showing up in the physics.

This is Law 3. Not metaphor. Not illustration. Isomorphism.

"Maxwell didn't invent the structure. He discovered it. And so did we."
