Usage Notes

The falsification table and kill cards go in the Rigor & Kill Conditions tab. Every article gets both. The table lists testable predictions with explicit kill conditions. The cards sort claims by confidence. This is how the framework holds itself accountable — the reader sees exactly where we're confident and where we're reaching.
