Section One

Article content goes here. This template uses the shared site shell via components/tp-inject.js, which renders the topbar with the four reading paths (Story, Plain, Test, Proof), the classification bar, the sticky audio dock, the subdomain navigation strip, and the footer.

A blockquote for emphasis — styled in gold-tinted background with left border.

Section Two

The classification bar at the top shows what percentage of this article falls into each category. The colored segments give an immediate visual sense of what domains the article covers — physics, theology, mathematics, evidence, cross-domain bridges, etc.

The reading path tabs control which version of the content is displayed. Story Path shows the narrative, human version. Plain Path shows clear explanation. Test Path shows technical claims and objections. Proof Path shows formal artifacts, Lean code, and datasets.

Equation Callout Example

When an article includes an equation, replace the old generic .equation-block with the new .mtl-callout markup. The callout is part of the article body, not a popup overlay.

Section Three

The audio dock at the bottom is fixed to the viewport. It has pills for Podcast, TTS (browser text-to-speech), and Read Aloud. When minimized, it collapses and a small floating play/pause button appears at the bottom right corner.
