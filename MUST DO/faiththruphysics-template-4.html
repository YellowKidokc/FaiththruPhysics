The Measurement That Collapsed Reality

When observation itself becomes the force that binds matter to meaning.

David Lowe · Faith thru Physics · GTQ-01 · June 2026

Section One

This template is designed to be dropped onto every page of faiththruphysics.com. The top bar carries the brand, the three main entry points (Home, MTL, Proof), and the reading-level selector. The classification bar beneath it shows the domain mix of the current article — Theology, Physics, Cross-Domain, Evidence, Consciousness, Mathematics, and Speculative — as both a colored bar and percentage tags.

Below the article, the big prev/next navigation creates a natural flow between articles in a series. The center column holds the series home (e.g., "GTQ Series Home") and the site home. Readers learn the pattern once and it works everywhere.

Section Two

The Final Audit is the signature of this site. Every article ends with the same three-part check: what we got right, what we overstated, and what we got wrong. The epistemic covenant sits above it, stating the boundary between what the evidence shows and where faith begins.

"The truth will defend itself. Your job is to say it clearly."

The footer grid organizes all subdomains into two groups: Proof & Research (the math-heavy tools) and Reference & Media (the supporting infrastructure). Hover any card and it lights up gold while the others dim — a physical metaphor for focus.

How to Use This Template

Copy the HTML between <!-- BEGIN:UNIFIED_TEMPLATE --> and <!-- END:UNIFIED_TEMPLATE --> markers. Inject it via inject_footer.py or paste it directly into new pages. The article content lives between the classification bar and the big prev/next nav.

  • Update ARTICLE_PROFILE in the script tag to match the current article's domain percentages.
  • Set the active reading level by adding class="active" to the correct button.
  • Update prev/next titles and hrefs in the big nav.
  • Fill in the Final Audit columns with per-article content.

Final Audit

Every page ends with the same three-part check: what we got right, what we overstated, and what we got wrong.

Epistemic covenant: we present these claims as openly, fairly, and truthfully as we can. The evidence can be checked, the mechanisms can be challenged, the proof burden is named. Where the work moves from how the science behaves to why it means something, the choice is stated. We think the pattern is worth believing, but we will not hide where faith begins.

What We Got Right

  • Load-bearing claims, clear definitions, and the parts that clearly survived the check.
  • The Master Equation derivation holds at 6.35σ.
  • The isomorphism between Yukawa potential and agapē is formally complete.

What We Overstated

  • Strong direction, but the language ran ahead of the evidence or the proof.
  • The consciousness bridge is suggestive but not yet rigorously closed.
  • We used "proof" where "high-confidence inference" would be more honest.

What We Got Wrong

  • Claims that need correction, tightening, or weaker formulation.
  • The initial entropy calculation was off by a factor of 2 — corrected in v4.
  • We attributed a source incorrectly; the citation has been fixed.
