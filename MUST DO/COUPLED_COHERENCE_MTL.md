## EQUATION 1: The Base Coherence Equation

### The Math
    dC/dt = O·G·(1 − C) − S·C

### Term by Term
    dC/dt    = the rate of change of coherence over time
    O        = openness — voluntary coupling to external source (0 to 1)
    G        = external ordering force (grace flux)
    (1 − C)  = remaining capacity for restoration (room left to heal)
    S        = entropy rate (decay, sin, disorder)
    C        = current coherence (0 to 1)

### Plain English
Coherence changes over time based on two competing forces.
The first force RESTORES: openness times grace times how much room
is left to heal. The second force DECAYS: entropy times how much
coherence currently exists. Whichever force is larger wins.

### Physics Side
This is a driven-dissipative equation. It appears in thermodynamics
(energy input vs heat loss), population dynamics (birth rate vs death
rate), and information theory (signal correction vs noise corruption).
In every case: external input maintains order against natural decay.
Remove the input and the system decays exponentially.

### Theology Side
Grace (G) enters through voluntary openness (O) and restores what
entropy (S) is destroying. The more broken the system (low C), the
more room there is for grace to work — (1 − C) is large when C is
small. Without openness, grace cannot enter. Without grace, decay wins.

### Shared Structure
Both domains describe a system that cannot maintain itself. External
input is required. The input must be received voluntarily. The decay
is always running. The outcome depends on which term dominates.

### Derived From
Three independent domains converge on this form:
1. Thermodynamics: driven-dissipative system (energy in vs entropy out)
2. Population dynamics: logistic equation with harvesting
3. Information theory: noisy channel with error correction
Convergence discovered after derivation, not designed.

## EQUATION 2: The Coupled Form (GPT's Extension)

### The Math
    dC_h/dt = O_h · G_Christ · (C_Christ − C_h) − S_h · C_h

### Term by Term
    dC_h/dt     = rate of change of HUMAN coherence over time
    O_h         = human openness (voluntary coupling — faith)
    G_Christ    = Christ's ordering force (grace — infinite)
    C_Christ    = Christ's coherence (= 1, perfect, the attractor)
    C_h         = human's current coherence (0 to 1)
    (C_Christ − C_h) = the GAP between perfect and current coherence
    S_h         = human entropy (physical + moral decay)

### Plain English
The human's coherence changes based on two forces. The restoration
force is: human openness times Christ's grace times THE GAP between
where you are and where Christ is. The decay force is: entropy times
your current coherence. The bigger the gap, the stronger the pull
toward restoration. The more broken you are, the harder grace pulls.

### Why This Is Different From Equation 1
Equation 1 has generic G — any ordering force. Equation 2 specifies
the source as Christ (G_Christ) and makes the restoration proportional
to the distance from the attractor (C_Christ − C_h). This means
grace is not a flat force. It is proportional to need. Where sin
abounded, grace abounds MORE — Romans 5:20 — falls directly out of
the math. The gap term (C_Christ − C_h) guarantees it.

### Physics Side
This is a coupled oscillator equation. In physics, when you couple
a damped oscillator to a driven reference source, the damped system
is pulled toward the driver's frequency. The coupling strength
determines how fast. The reference source does not lose energy by
coupling — it is externally driven. This is identical: Christ is the
externally driven reference. The human is the damped oscillator.

### Theology Side
Sanctification is not self-improvement. It is coupling. The human
does not generate coherence from within. The human opens a channel
(O_h > 0) to the only perfectly coherent source (C_Christ = 1).
The source does the work. The human does the opening.

### Derived From
Extension of Equation 1 by specifying the source term. When G is
infinite and the source coherence is fixed at 1, the generic equation
becomes a coupled attractor equation. The mathematical form is
identical to phase-locking in oscillator physics.

## EQUATION 3: The Equilibrium

### The Math
    C* = Λ / (Λ + S_h)

    where Λ = O_h · G_Christ

### Term by Term
    C*     = equilibrium coherence (where the system settles)
    Λ      = effective grace (openness times ordering force)
    S_h    = human entropy rate
    Λ + S_h = total force budget (grace plus entropy)

### Plain English
Where you end up depends on the ratio of effective grace to total
forces. If grace is large relative to entropy, you end up near 1.
If entropy is large relative to grace, you end up near 0. If they
are equal, you settle at 0.5 — unstable, pulled both ways.

### Derived From
Standard equilibrium analysis. Set dC_h/dt = 0 and solve for C.
This is the same method used to find steady-state temperature,
population carrying capacity, or signal-to-noise equilibrium.

### CASE 1: Closed System (O = 0)

### The Math
    O_h = 0
    Λ = O_h · G = 0
    C* = 0 / (0 + S_h) = 0
    dC/dt = −S_h · C

### Plain English
If the human is completely closed — no voluntary coupling to any
external ordering source — then the grace term vanishes entirely.
All that remains is decay. Coherence decreases exponentially toward
zero. Not because the person is stupid or uncultured. Because the
restoration channel is closed.

### What This Looks Like
This is the universe as the atheist reads it. And the reading is
mathematically correct UNDER THIS BOUNDARY CONDITION. Entropy is
real. Decay is real. Cost transfers but never terminates. The
ledger never closes. Mercy becomes sentiment. Justice becomes
consequence. Forgiveness becomes psychological coping.

The atheist is not wrong about the physics. The atheist is wrong
only if the closed-system assumption is wrong.

### Key Insight
The atheist's equation is not a different equation. It is THIS
equation with O set to zero. Atheism is a restricted case of
the fuller equation, not a competing theory.

### CASE 2: Finite Grace Sources (O > 0, G = finite)

### The Math
    O_h > 0
    G = finite
    S_h > 0
    C* = O_h · G / (O_h · G + S_h)
    0 < C* < 1

### Plain English
If the human opens to a finite ordering source — family, therapy,
law, education, community, discipline, art, medicine, philosophy,
religion-as-culture — coherence improves. The floor rises above
zero. But it never reaches one. Because finite grace divided by
finite grace plus any nonzero entropy is always less than one.

### What This Looks Like
The mother staying up all night with her addicted son. Her love is
G. Her openness to him is O. Her sacrifice raises his floor. He
gets better for a while. But her G is finite. Her own S is nonzero.
She cannot absorb his full cost without burning out. The payment is
real but incomplete. The floor rises. It does not reach the ceiling.

This is also every human institution. AA raises the floor. Therapy
raises the floor. Good government raises the floor. Education raises
the floor. None of them can reach C = 1 because none of them have
infinite G.

### Key Insight
Finite grace is not fake grace. It is real but bounded. The mother's
sacrifice genuinely helps. The equation honors it. It just cannot
complete what it starts.

### CASE 3: Infinite Grace (O > 0, G = infinite)

### The Math
    O_h > 0
    G_Christ = infinite
    C* = O_h · infinity / (O_h · infinity + S_h)

    Since O_h > 0, the numerator is infinite.
    S_h is finite.
    Infinite / (infinite + finite) = 1.

    C* approaches 1.

### Plain English
If the human opens — even slightly — to an infinite ordering
source, the equilibrium coherence goes to one. Not because the
human supplied enough openness. Because ANY nonzero openness times
INFINITE grace overwhelms any finite entropy.

This is the mustard seed. Tiny faith times infinite grace equals
sufficient restoration. Zero faith times infinite grace equals
nothing. The difference between salvation and decay is not the
SIZE of faith. It is whether faith is nonzero.

### What This Looks Like
The same mother — but now coupled to an infinite source. Her
sacrifice is still real. Her love is still real. But she is no
longer the source of the ordering force. She is the channel.
Grace flows through her from a source that does not deplete.
Her capacity is still finite. But the source behind her is not.

### Key Insight
    Works are not G.
    Works are downstream expressions of restored coherence.
    Faith is not the force.
    Faith is the open channel.
    Grace is the force.

Salvation is not generated by human moral energy. It is received
by voluntary coupling to an infinite ordering source.
