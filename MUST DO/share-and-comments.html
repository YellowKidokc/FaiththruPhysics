Quick reactions without the overhead of a full comment section. Good for individual sections or tangent cards.

React
Infrastructure

Backend Wiring

How to connect these components to Cloudflare Workers.

Comments endpoint:
POST /api/comments/{paper-slug} — submit a comment
GET /api/comments/{paper-slug} — load comments for an article

Reactions endpoint:
POST /api/reactions/{paper-slug}/{reaction-type} — toggle a reaction
GET /api/reactions/{paper-slug} — load reaction counts

Storage options:
1. Cloudflare KV — simplest, key-value, good for <100 comments/article
2. Cloudflare D1 — SQLite, good for full threading and search
3. Cloudflare Durable Objects — real-time, good for live discussion

Moderation: Comments go to KV with status: "pending". You approve via a simple admin page or Notion webhook. Only status: "approved" render on the public page.
