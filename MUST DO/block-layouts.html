Block Layouts

10 box types, 7 grid presets. Mix and match to build any paper layout. Same design DNA, different visual weight.

10 Box Types Grid Presets 7 Full Layouts Design Rules
3 Laws of Box Variety:
1. Same border/color tokens, different span — a box spanning 2 columns feels 3× more important with zero extra styling.
2. Vary depth, not color — use surface layers (--surface → --surface3 → gold tint) for hierarchy. No new hues.
3. One accent break per section — your gold-left-border callout is perfect. One per section max.
