Audit & kill conditions

Load-Bearing Claims

The universe is expanding. Hubble’s discovery (1929), confirmed by CMB, Type Ia supernovae, and BAO measurements. This is established physics. If cosmic expansion were disproved, the paper’s central parallel collapses — but so does all of modern cosmology.

Hebrew prophets described expansion. Isaiah 40:22, 42:5, 44:24, 51:13; Jeremiah 10:12; Zechariah 12:1 use the verb “stretches out” (nāṭāh) for the heavens. This is textual fact. The load-bearing question is whether the texts describe expansion or merely metaphorical tent-spreading.

~ Suggestive Claims

The Hebrew verb nāṭāh implies ongoing expansion. The participial form suggests continuous action, which parallels ongoing cosmic expansion. But ancient Hebrew grammar may not carry the precision of modern physics, and “stretching a tent” is a common Near Eastern metaphor for sky-dome construction.

The Hubble tension as evidence for grace-driven expansion. The tension between CMB-derived and distance-ladder H0 values is real (4–6σ), but its resolution may be mundane (systematic errors, new physics unrelated to theology).

Got Carried Away

“The Hebrew prophets described cosmic expansion 2,500 years before Hubble.” This is the paper’s headline claim and its most common overreach. The prophets used tent-stretching language in a cultural context where the sky was conceived as a solid dome (raqia’). Reading modern cosmology into ancient Hebrew poetry requires hermeneutical generosity that the paper presents as straightforward correspondence.
