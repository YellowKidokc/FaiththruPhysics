# Contributor Attribution and Credit Ledger

This project follows a practical open-source style workflow for credit:

- If you contributed something to the framework, code, media mapping, prompts, schema process, or editorial tooling, you can add an entry.
- Claims are reviewed and verified before acceptance.
- If a contribution is disputed, we keep it marked as `Review` until resolved.

## Current attribution policy

1. **Creditable contributions**: clearly bounded changes to structure, content, tooling, proofs, prompts, media/asset mapping, or editorial process.
2. **Core ownership**: the core Theophysics framework, Lean 4 formalization direction, and final interpretive responsibility remain David Lowe.
3. **AI vs human distinction**: AI may be cited as analytical collaborator; creative and authoritative ownership follows the same verification process as any external contributor.
4. **Disputes**: disputed items are marked `Review` and remain provisional until resolved.

## How to add a credit

Append a line to this section with:
- Date
- Name/handle or AI collaborator
- Role (human/AI/collaborative)
- Contribution summary
- Scope (series/page/tool)
- Status (`Accepted`, `Review`, `Removed`)
