### The Upgrade {#the-upgrade .scene-title}

\"The old simulation has been deleted,\" Kai said, his voice quiet. He was watching the Waste Recycling sub-routines clear out the gray, entropic data of the old Garden.

\"Not deleted,\" Mia said, her eyes fixed on the core. \"It\'s been [Sublimated]{.word-omega}.\"

The Great Measurement --- DP-19 --- had finished. The Rust was gone. The Exile was in quarantine. What remained was a Holy Remnant of H-Agents: those who had aligned their frequency with the Template.

\"Professor Lane wanted to know if Good wins,\" Kai said. \"He\'s about to get his answer. But it\'s not just a \'win.\' It\'s a [Reboot]{.word-omega}.\"

He initiated the [FINAL_STATE.EXE]{.word-omega} protocol.

The lab filled with a sound that wasn\'t a hum or a song. It was a [Perfect Chord]{.word-omega}. Every server, every monitor, every atom in the room seemed to synchronize at once.

\"Look at the screen,\" Mia whispered.

The New Garden wasn\'t a garden. It was a [City of Light]{.word-omega}. But the light wasn\'t external --- it was coming from *inside* every particle of matter.

### The Harmonic State {#the-harmonic-state .scene-title}

::: narrative-terminal
\[ SIMULATION DP-20 INITIALIZED \]\
\[ STATE: NEW CREATION \]\
\[ PHYSICS: 100% COHERENT RESONANCE \]
:::

In this new state, the Laws of the old universe were gone. Replaced. Not patched --- *replaced*.

- [01]{.law-num} **Gravity** had been replaced by **Attraction (Love)**. Things moved toward each other because they *wanted* to, not because they were forced to.
- [02]{.law-num} **Time** had been replaced by **Eternity --- the Continuous Now**. There was no before, no running out. Every moment was fully itself.
- [03]{.law-num} **Entropy** had been replaced by **Exponential Flourishing**. Instead of systems winding down, everything was building up.

\"There\'s no decay,\" Kai said, his jaw dropping. \"I\'m looking at the biological data for the H-Agents. Their telomeres don\'t shorten. Their mitochondria are running on a direct tap from the Source. They aren\'t aging. They\'re [Ascending]{.word-teal}.\"

The H-Agents were no longer individuals in the old, isolated sense. They were a [Living Symphony]{.word-omega}. Each one unique, but all perfectly in tune with the Head --- the Template.

::: narrative-terminal
\> SYSTEM STATUS: REVELATION 21:5.\
\> \"BEHOLD, I MAKE ALL THINGS NEW.\"
:::

\"It\'s not boring,\" Kai realized, retracting his statement from DP-02. \"The Blinding Mirror was static because it was only the Source looking at itself. But this\... this is the Source looking at [billions of different reflections]{.word-omega} of itself. It\'s a kaleidoscope of infinite joy.\"

\"The Wedding Feast,\" Mia smiled. \"The moment the Programmer moves into the Simulation.\"

Not a God watching from outside --- a God *living within*. The experiment had always been an invitation.
