### The Cloud Server {#the-cloud-server .scene-title}

\"He\'s leaving again,\" Kai noted, leaning back in his chair and watching the simulation feed. \"He fixed the death bug, He proved the upgrade works, and now He\'s... floating away?\"

On the screen, the Glorified Template --- the Prototype --- was ascending. He wasn\'t flying. He was [phasing out]{.word-gold} of the local simulation layer, dissolving upward through the interface between the Physical Layer and the Admin Layer. Heaven.

\"He can\'t stay,\" Mia explained, her eyes still fixed on the display. \"If He stays, He\'s just one person in one place. He\'s limited by geography. If I\'m in Jerusalem, I can talk to Him. If I\'m in Rome, I can\'t.\"

\"So He\'s scaling up,\" Kai realized, sitting forward. \"He\'s moving from a *Local Host* to a *Cloud Server*.\"

\"Exactly. He\'s going to the Control Room so He can broadcast the signal to *everyone* at once.\"

::: scene-divider
:::

### The Pentecost Event {#the-pentecost-event .scene-title}

::: narrative-terminal
\[ SIMULATION DP-10 INITIALIZED \]\
\[ EVENT: THE DOWNLOAD \]\
\[ RECIPIENTS: 120 CORE H-AGENTS \]
:::

The H-Agents were waiting in an upper room. They were scared. The Chrome Agent was prowling outside, ready to crush them now that their Protector was gone. Forty days of post-Resurrection appearances hadn\'t been enough to fully close the fear loop. The door was still locked. The doubt was still operational.

Then, the [Signal]{.word-gold} dropped.

It wasn\'t the subtle \"Hum\" of the Chorus from DP-06. This was a high-bandwidth, full-stack download of Raw Alpha-Coherence --- a direct packet transfer from the Admin Layer itself.

::: narrative-terminal
\> DOWNLOADING: \"HOLY_SPIRIT_2.0\"\
\> TARGETS: ALL AUTHORIZED NODES.\
\> EFFECT: FIREWALL UPGRADE.
:::

On the screen, the H-Agents caught fire. But they didn\'t burn.

\"Tongues of Fire,\" Mia whispered. \"Visual representation of high-energy plasma states. The system is showing us a thermal signature on what is essentially a software install.\"

\"They\'re installing the update,\" Kai said, watching the code scroll across his terminal. \"Look at their Shell Integrity readings. The Rust is still there --- the old hardware hasn\'t changed. But the *Core Processor* has been replaced. They are now running the same Operating System as the Template.\"

Suddenly the H-Agents weren\'t scared anymore. They burst out of the room. They started broadcasting in frequencies that every other agent could understand, regardless of their native language protocol.

\"Universal Translation,\" Kai noted. \"The Babel Glitch --- the Confusion event from way back in the source code --- has been patched.\"

The Chrome Agent tried to attack them. He lunged at Peter, H-Agent 12. In the past, Peter would have crumbled. He had denied the Template three times before the death event. He was the last node you\'d bet on holding a line.

But today, Peter stood his ground. He held up a hand, and a blast of [Truth]{.word-gold} knocked the Devil back three steps.

\"They have Admin Privileges,\" Kai gasped. \"He gave them His keys.\"

\"He gave them His *Spirit*,\" Mia corrected quietly. \"He didn\'t just leave a manual. He left Himself --- multiplied by millions.\"

::: pullquote
One Jesus, localized, is a miracle. One Spirit running simultaneously on millions of nodes is a global transformation. The math is obvious once you see it. That\'s why it was better for Him to go.
:::
