## The South Korean Recovery

South Korea in 1953 was rubble.[60]

- Colonized for 35 years (Japan)[61?]
- War-devastated
- Split from half its country
- One of poorest nations on Earth
- χ was **well below threshold**

By every measure, South Korea should have collapsed into permanent dysfunction.

Instead, it became:

- Top 10 global economy[62]
- Technology leader
- Cultural exporter (K-pop, K-drama)
- Functional democracy

**Coherence was restored from below threshold.**

How?

Churches became the coherence-generating institutions:

- Community formation
- Trust networks
- Shared meaning
- Mutual aid
- Intergenerational transmission

The same functions that American churches used to perform—before they stopped.

**

## The North Korean Warning

North Korea shows what happens when β = 0.[63]

No external input. No grace. Pure entropy.

The result is not just poverty. It's **anti-coherence**:

- Families spy on each other
- Trust is suicide
- Meaning is whatever the state says today
- Children report parents

Below threshold with no grace coefficient = civilizational death.
