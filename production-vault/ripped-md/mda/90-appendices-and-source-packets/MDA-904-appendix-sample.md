### **Critical Thresholds

1962

Engel v. Vitale — Supreme Court removes prayer from public schools.
                        The first domino. Within one generation, every FACTS metric begins declining.

1973

Roe v. Wade — Sanctity of life redefined by judicial fiat.
                        Accelerates the separation of moral consequence from personal choice.

1995

Internet Mass Adoption — Information decentralization begins.
                        Within 15 years, attention spans halve and algorithmic radicalization emerges.

2015

Social Media Saturation — Semantic collapse accelerates.
                        Words like "violence," "safety," and "justice" lose fixed meaning. Public discourse fragments.
