The Mathematics

The rigor layer is not an afterthought. It is the foundation. Every narrative claim in MDA — every drop in family stability, every collapse in shared meaning, every phase transition — is traceable to a proxy variable, a channel score, and an aggregate coherence computation.

## Channel Score

Li = log( (Ci + ε) / (Di + ε) )

Each channel (domain) receives a constructive signal C and a destructive signal D. The log-ratio measures which regime dominates. This is structurally identical to Kullback-Leibler divergence and Shannon channel capacity.

## Aggregate Coherence

χ = (1/N) · Σ wi · Li

The aggregate coherence χ is the weighted mean of all channel scores. It is a single number that classifies civilizational health: above 0.35 is sustainable, below is precarious, negative is collapse.

## Dynamics

dSm/dt = σ − W/T

The rate of change tracks whether coherence is accumulating (σ > W/T) or dissipating (σ < W/T). This is the same entropy-production formalism from stochastic thermodynamics.
