# The Canonical Axiom Foundation

Truth below the system. Primitives and presuppositions at the floor. Conceptual axioms above them. Theorems after that.

## Pre-System: Truth

PRE-SYSTEM — NOT AN AXIOM
Truth
Truth is not inside the hierarchy. The system exists because Truth is already there.

      Truth is not a primitive term, not a presupposition, and not an axiom. Any definition, proof, denial, or assertion already depends on truth being operative. The framework can point to this floor, but it cannot place Truth inside the system without circularity. This is why the canonical file says Truth is below the floor.

## Level 0: Primitive Terms

LEVEL 0 — THREE PRIMITIVES
Existence, Distinction, Information
These are undefined anchor concepts used by the framework but not defined from inside it.

      Existence means something rather than nothing. Distinction means one thing can differ from another. Information means distinguishable states exist. These are not laboratory predictions and not Lean axioms; they are the vocabulary floor required before the rest of the chain can even be stated.

## Level 1: Presuppositions

LEVEL 1 — THREE PRESUPPOSITIONS
The Floor of Reasoning
Something exists; distinguishable states are real; and the system can be reasoned about consistently.

      These are the minimum commitments needed before the framework starts. They are not presented as new discoveries. They are the operating floor under any rational system: there is something to discuss, there are distinctions to track, and reasoning is not meaningless.
