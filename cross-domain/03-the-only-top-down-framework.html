The Only Top-Down Framework

Why Theophysics Is the First Deductively Complete Theory of Social Collapse

David Lowe · December 2025 · Canonical

Abstract

Every theory of social decline in the last century has been built bottom-up: observe symptoms, propose causes, argue endlessly. This paper demonstrates that Theophysics is the first framework to derive social collapse from axiomatic first principles—the same method that produced General Relativity and Quantum Mechanics.

Moral decline is not the disease. It is the symptom. The disease is axiomatic—a violation of the logical primitives that govern all coherent systems.

When America adopted axioms that treat reality as a closed system (self-defining, self-referential, self-correcting), thermodynamics guaranteed the result: entropy increase until collapse.

This is not sociology. This is physics.

I

Section IThe Problem with Bottom-Up Theories

1.1 The Endless Debate

For 60 years, scholars have debated American decline:

  • Conservatives blame the 1960s counterculture
  • Progressives blame capitalism and racism
  • Libertarians blame government expansion
  • Communitarians blame individualism

They are all wrong—and all right.

Each identifies a symptom and mistakes it for the cause. This is like debating whether the fever or the cough is killing the patient, while ignoring the underlying infection.

1.2 Why Bottom-Up Fails

Bottom-up theories:

  • Start with observations (divorce rates, trust surveys, crime statistics)
  • Propose causal mechanisms (cultural shifts, policy changes, technology)
  • Argue about which mechanism is primary

This method cannot resolve the debate because:

  • Multiple symptoms can have the same root cause
  • Correlation does not establish causation
  • The mechanism level is too shallow

You cannot diagnose a system failure by cataloging its symptoms.

1.3 The Alternative: Top-Down Derivation

Mature physics works differently:

  • Establish axioms (principles that cannot be violated without contradiction)
  • Derive predictions (what must happen if the axioms are true)
  • Confirm empirically (do observations match predictions?)

Einstein did not observe time dilation and then propose relativity. He derived time dilation from axioms (constancy of light speed, equivalence principle) and then sought confirmation.

Theophysics applies this method to social systems for the first time.

2.2 The Unitary Trap

The master axiom violation is the Unitary Trap:

A system that attempts to define itself without external reference becomes a closed system. Closed systems cannot reduce their own entropy. Therefore, self-defining systems must collapse.

In Physics: A unitary process conserves information but cannot create order. You need a non-unitary operator (measurement, Grace) to reduce entropy.

In Society: A civilization that defines its own truth (relativism), its own morality (subjectivism), and its own value (fiat currency) becomes a closed system.

The Result: Thermodynamic inevitability. Entropy increases. Coherence decreases. Collapse follows.
