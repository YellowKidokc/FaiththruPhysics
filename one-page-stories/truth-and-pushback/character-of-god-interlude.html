01 - The Question

What kind of Person does reality keep resembling?

The page does not begin with a creed. It begins with properties: force, order, coherence, endurance, truth transmission, binding, restoration. One property can be coincidence. A few can be poetic. A whole field of properties starts to look like portrait.

The document does not argue by assertion. It accumulates until the outline becomes hard to ignore.

- Faith Through Physics

The claim is not that physics replaces theology. The claim is that physics keeps describing features theology already named.

02 - Proof by Accumulation

Read the properties together, not one at a time

Property 1
Order
Reality is not random motion. It is structured enough to be measured, repeated, and trusted.
Property 2
Coherence
What holds together is not merely efficient. It is intelligible across domains.
Property 3
Fidelity
Truth survives transmission when the underlying structure is not broken in the telling.
Property 4
Restoration
Grace is not random softness. It is repair without collapse.

Read the whole field and the portrait becomes specific enough to test against theology instead of hovering as a vague feeling.

03 - The Answer

The universe does not look empty. It looks authored.

The strongest version is not "God is like physics." It is sharper: physics keeps revealing the same character structure Scripture attributes to God.

The full portrait keeps the measured properties visible. This interlude only gives the outline enough contrast to be read.
