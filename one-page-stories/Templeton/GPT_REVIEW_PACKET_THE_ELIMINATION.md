# GPT Review Packet: The Elimination

## Request

Review this argument as a serious adversarial reader.

Do not flatter it.

Do not mock it.

Find the strongest places it can fail.

## Core Frame

The project is called:

```text
The Elimination
```

The central claim is not that raw miracle proves God.

The central claim is:

```text
God is the one whom death cannot lawfully hold,
justice cannot condemn,
and mercy can flow from without becoming false.
```

The God-marker is:

```text
perfect justice
+ perfect mercy
+ voluntary entry into death
+ no rightful jurisdiction against Him
+ death compelled by justice to release Him
+ restoration offered to the guilty without falsifying guilt
```

## Ten Gates

```text
G1  Sourcehood
G2  Voluntary death-entry
G3  No moral debt
G4  Perfect justice
G5  Perfect mercy
G6  Truth preservation
G7  Identity preservation
G8  Rightful victory over death
G9  Non-borrowed authority
G10 Communicable restoration
```

2. Identify equivocations.

5. Identify candidates that fail the gates in a more subtle way.

## Non-Negotiable Boundary

Do not claim the historical resurrection is proven by formal logic alone.

The historical resurrection remains historical.

The formal claim is narrower:

```text
Under the Christian jurisdictional premises,
resurrection is a lawful release,
not an arbitrary exception.
```

## Current Known Weak Point

The claim "sin gives death jurisdiction" must distinguish:

- biological death
- creation-wide futility/decay
- moral death
- death's rightful retentive jurisdiction over moral agents

The sharper claim is:

```text
Death can receive Christ by voluntary entry.
Death cannot retain Christ by rightful claim.
```
