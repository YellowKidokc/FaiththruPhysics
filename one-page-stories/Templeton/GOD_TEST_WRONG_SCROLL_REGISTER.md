# The God Test: Wrong Scroll Register

## Purpose

This register collects false God-markers and counterfeit death-victory claims.

The point is not to bury the reader in weak objections. The point is to make the exclusion field so clear that the clean scroll becomes unavoidable.

Each entry asks:

```text
Does this candidate defeat death by perfect justice and perfect mercy,
or by something less?
```

If it is something less, it fails the God test.

## Core Failure Rule

A candidate fails if death is overcome by:

- raw power
- evasion
- trick
- technology
- loophole
- deception
- borrowed rescue
- denial of justice
- denial of mercy
- loss of identity
- inability to restore others
- dependence on a higher source

## Category A: Power Without Right

001. **Brute force over death**  
Fails because strength may overpower a constraint without having rightful authority over it.

002. **Cosmic dominance**  
Fails because being the strongest being inside reality does not make one the source of reality.

003. **Violent conquest of death**  
Fails because conquest can prove superiority, not innocence or mercy.

004. **Destruction of the death-system**  
Fails because annihilating the judge is not the same as being acquitted by justice.

005. **Immortal biology**  
Fails because not dying is not the same as passing through death without debt.

006. **Indestructible body**  
Fails because invulnerability avoids the test rather than submitting to it.

007. **Infinite durability**  
Fails because lasting forever does not prove moral purity.

008. **Regeneration after injury**  
Fails because repair is not resurrection under jurisdiction.

009. **Superior energy source**  
Fails because energy can sustain life without establishing righteousness.

010. **Weaponized resurrection**  
Fails because using resurrection as domination contradicts mercy.

## Category B: Escape Without Examination

012. **Disappearing before death**

013. **Hiding from death**

014. **Pausing biological time**

015. **Cryogenic preservation**  
Fails because delay is not defeat.

016. **Uploading consciousness before death**

017. **Leaving the body behind as disposable shell**

018. **Transcending matter by escape**  
Fails because Christianity claims redemption of the body, not escape from embodiment.

019. **Becoming pure information to avoid death**  
Fails because information survival alone does not establish justice or mercy.

020. **Astral survival**  
Fails because disembodied continuation is not resurrection victory over death.

