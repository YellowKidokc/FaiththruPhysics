# The Elimination

## Working Subtitle

Every counterfeit God-marker tested against the final structural specification.

## Core Thesis

The question is not:

```text
Which religion has the most attractive God?
```

The question is:

```text
What must be true of any candidate who can pass through death with perfect justice and perfect mercy intact?
```

The God-marker is:

```text
perfect justice
+ perfect mercy
+ voluntary entry into death
+ no rightful jurisdiction against Him
+ death compelled by justice to release Him
+ restoration offered to the guilty without falsifying guilt
```

Everything else is eliminated by failure against that specification.

## The Two Scrolls

### Scroll 1: The Clean Scroll

Two sentences:

> God is the one whom death cannot lawfully hold, justice cannot condemn, and mercy can flow from without becoming false.
>
> The God-marker is not raw power over death, but perfect justice and perfect mercy passing through the final constraint of death and returning with authority to restore the guilty.

### Scroll 2: The Wrong Scroll

The long adversarial register.

Its function is not rhetorical bulk. Its function is exhaustive exit-closure.

Every alternative fails one or more of the same gates:

```text
sourcehood
death-entry
sinlessness
perfect justice
perfect mercy
truth-preservation
identity-preservation
rightful authority
non-borrowed victory
restorative power
```

## The Specification

Let a candidate `x` be tested by ten gates.

```text
G1 Sourcehood:
  x does not merely operate inside the system but stands in rightful relation to its final law.

G2 Death Entry:
  x enters the final jurisdiction voluntarily.

G3 No Moral Debt:
  justice has no rightful charge against x.

G4 Perfect Justice:
  x does not save by denying guilt, lowering the law, or falsifying the record.

G5 Perfect Mercy:
  x does not merely judge, but restores the guilty.

G6 Truth Preservation:
  salvation does not require a lie.

G7 Identity Preservation:
  resurrection preserves the person, not a copy, symbol, memory, archetype, or replacement.

G8 Rightful Death Victory:
  death loses because it has no lawful claim, not because it is tricked or overpowered.

G9 Non-Borrowed Authority:
  x does not depend on a higher source outside himself for victory over death.

G10 Communicable Victory:
  x can offer the same victory to others without destroying justice.
```

The candidate who fails any gate is not the God-marker.

## The Kill Conditions

The argument fails if any of these is true:

```text
1. Death is not the final universal constraint of the fallen order.
2. Death can rightly retain the innocent.
3. Sin/corruption/debt is not connected to death's moral-retentive jurisdiction.
4. Perfect justice and perfect mercy are not jointly required.
5. Mercy can save by denying justice.
6. Justice can remain perfect while preventing restoration forever.
7. Identity need not persist through death.
8. A copy/replacement/symbol is equivalent to resurrection.
9. Borrowed resurrection is enough to identify God.
10. Raw power over death is enough to identify God.
```

These are not weaknesses to hide. They are the test boundary.

## Tone Rule

The Elimination must not sound like mockery.

It should read like a court record.

Calm. Exact. Relentless.

No sneering.

No cheap shots.

No inflated claims.

Every rejection should say:

```text
This fails because it lacks one required structural property.
```


```text

Every alternative tested so far fails at least one required gate.
The historical claim remains historical.
The formal claim is the elimination field.
```
