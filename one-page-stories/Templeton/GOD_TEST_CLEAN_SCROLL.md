# The God Test: Clean Scroll

## Two-Sentence Form

God is the one whom death cannot lawfully hold, justice cannot condemn, and mercy can flow from without becoming false.

The God-marker is not raw power over death, but perfect justice and perfect mercy passing through the final constraint of death and returning with authority to restore the guilty.

## Compact Formula

```text
perfect justice
+ perfect mercy
+ voluntary entry into death
+ no rightful jurisdiction against Him
+ death forced to release Him
+ restoration offered to the guilty
= the God-marker
```

## The Core Test

```text
Death tests every creature.
Justice tests every claim.
Mercy tests every savior.

God is the one whom death cannot lawfully hold,
justice cannot condemn,
and mercy can flow from without becoming false.
```

## Why This Is Not Mere Miracle

A miracle can show power.

Power alone does not identify God.

Power may be deception, technology, sorcery, unknown physics, simulation control, brute force, or a local exception.

The God test is stronger:

```text
Who can pass through death with perfect justice and perfect mercy intact?
```

## Necessary Marks

1. Death is the final constraint of the fallen order.
2. The candidate enters death freely.
3. The candidate owes death nothing.
4. Justice finds no rightful accusation.
5. Mercy is extended to those who do owe debt.
6. Mercy does not deny guilt.
7. Justice does not destroy restoration.
8. Death loses by rightful authority, not by trick or force.
9. Personal identity is preserved through death.
10. The victory can be shared with the guilty.

## Shortest Public Line

```text
A candidate is not God because he avoids death.
A candidate is not God because he survives death.
A candidate is not God because he reverses death by power.

The God-marker is this:

He enters the final jurisdiction freely,
is examined by perfect justice,
is found without rightful debt,
extends perfect mercy to those who do have debt,
and death loses because it has no lawful claim.
```
