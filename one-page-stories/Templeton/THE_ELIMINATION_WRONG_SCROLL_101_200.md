# The Elimination Wrong Scroll: Entries 101-200

## Category K: Metaphysical Alternatives

101. **Necessary abstract object**  
Fails because an abstract necessity cannot enter death, extend mercy, or restore the guilty.

102. **Impersonal ground of being**  
Fails because sourcehood without personal agency cannot forgive.

103. **Mathematical Platonism**  
Fails because mathematical objects may be necessary but cannot bear cost or offer mercy.

104. **The universe as God**  
Fails because the universe is subject to death, entropy, and corruption within itself.

105. **The laws of physics as God**  
Fails because laws describe order but do not personally enter the final jurisdiction.

106. **Pure consciousness as ultimate**  
Fails if it cannot preserve concrete personal identity and restore guilty agents.

107. **Cosmic mind without incarnation**  
Fails because knowing death is not entering death.

108. **Absolute unity without distinction**  
Fails because mercy requires relation, giver, receiver, and truth-preserving restoration.

109. **Pure becoming**  
Fails because what is always becoming lacks final non-borrowed authority over death.

110. **Pure potentiality**  
Fails because potential that never actualizes cannot pass through death.

111. **Eternal matter**  
Fails because eternal substrate does not supply perfect justice or mercy.

112. **Moral order without personal judge**  
Fails because an impersonal order can condemn but cannot forgive.

113. **Cosmic balance**  
Fails because balance may preserve symmetry while leaving guilt unresolved.

114. **Fate**  
Fails because inevitability is not justice, mercy, or restoration.

115. **Chance as ultimate**  
Fails because chance cannot ground rightful authority.

116. **The Good as abstraction**  
Fails because abstract goodness does not voluntarily enter death.

117. **The One beyond personality**  
Fails if transcendence removes agency, mercy, and identity-preserving rescue.

118. **Infinite regress of causes**  
Fails because no member of the regress has final authority over death.

119. **Self-caused universe**  
Fails because self-causation does not establish moral jurisdiction or mercy.

120. **Emergent divinity**  
Fails because an emergent god depends on the system that produced it.
