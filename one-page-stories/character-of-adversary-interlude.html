Adversary / Broken World

The Character of the Adversary

If evil had a physics signature, it would not look creative. It would look like strategic decay.

Entropy is not a personality. But the pattern it serves can be read.

◆

Decay does the same kind of work everywhere. It separates what belonged together. It scrambles signal. It isolates systems. It makes memory less trustworthy. It turns form into noise.

If that were only one domain, we could call it accident. But the same pattern keeps appearing: in physics, in language, in identity, in community, in worship.

The adversary does not need to create a world. He only needs to fracture one.

Evil is not the opposite of creation. It is the parasitic inversion of coherence.

Proof by Signature

Despair says the future is closed. Isolation says no one can reach you. Identity theft says you are not who God named. The lie says signal is noise and noise is signal.

Those are not random temptations. They are attack vectors against coherence channels.

The Clean Answer

Physics does not merely describe decay. It shows why decay cannot be ultimate. Entropy can damage order, but it cannot author the order it damages.

The adversary can corrupt coherence. He cannot become its source.
