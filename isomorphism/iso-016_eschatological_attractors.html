The Claim

Structural isomorphism · binary terminus · no neutral equilibrium
ISO-016 · Structural Isomorphism · Dynamical Systems ↔ Theology

Logistic-type dynamical systems with two stable fixed-point attractors and one unstable saddle point are structurally isomorphic to the Christian eschatological schema. The system variable Φ ∈ [0, 1] evolves toward exactly one of two stable endpoints — Φ = 0 or Φ = 1 — with an unstable critical threshold at Φ = 0.5 that can never serve as a permanent terminus. The theological mapping is exact: Φ = 1 corresponds to Heaven (full communion with God, complete coherence); Φ = 0 corresponds to Hell (total separation from God, complete decoherence); and Φ = 0.5 corresponds to the lukewarm middle state — theologically unstable and explicitly rejected in scripture.

Both domains share the same topological structure: binary basin partitioning by a saddle point that cannot be occupied indefinitely. The dynamical systems framework predicts exactly the eschatological structure found in theology — no third stable state, no neutral terminus, and an unstable middle that must eventually resolve into one of the two attractors.

 Kill Condition

If a genuine third stable state were discovered in either domain — a stable neutral equilibrium in the dynamical system, or a scriptural third eschatological category that is neither Heaven nor Hell — the mapping breaks. Purgatory (Catholic theology) functions as a transient state, not a stable attractor; souls pass through Purgatory and converge to Φ = 1. Purgatory therefore does not falsify the mapping. The kill condition requires a permanent, scripturally grounded third terminus.

Governing Dynamics — Primary Equation
dΦ/dt = α Φ (1 − Φ) (Φ − Φc)
The logistic cubic governing the evolution of coherence variable Φ over time. α > 0 sets the convergence rate; Φc = 0.5 is the critical threshold partitioning the two basins of attraction. Φ ∈ [0, 1] throughout.
Fixed Points — Where dΦ/dt = 0
Φ* = 0 → Stable attractor [Hell / decoherence] Φ* = 0.5 → Unstable saddle [Lukewarmness / indecision] Φ* = 1 → Stable attractor [Heaven / full coherence]
The cubic has exactly three real roots in [0, 1]. Stability is determined by the sign of f′(Φ*): negative eigenvalue = stable attractor; positive eigenvalue = unstable saddle.
Stability Analysis — Linearization at Each Fixed Point

For α > 0, both outer fixed points are stable attractors. The inner fixed point is an unstable repeller. Any Φ0 ≠ 0.5 converges to exactly one attractor.

Evidence & Verification

Scriptural grounding · mathematical verification · caveats

The theological domain is grounded in explicit scriptural claims that directly encode the binary attractor structure. Three primary texts provide the evidence base, each mapping to a distinct feature of the dynamical system.

"When the Son of Man comes in his glory, and all the angels with him, he will sit on his glorious throne. All the nations will be gathered before him, and he will separate the people one from another as a shepherd separates the sheep from the goats." Matthew 25:31–32 — The canonical eschatological binary: two groups, two outcomes, no third category
"So, because you are lukewarm — neither hot nor cold — I am about to spit you out of my mouth." Revelation 3:16 — Direct rejection of the stable middle state; the saddle is not merely unstable but actively expelled
"This day I call the heavens and the earth as witnesses against you that I have set before you life and death, blessings and curses. Now choose life, so that you and your children may live." Deuteronomy 30:19 — Binary choice structure; two basins; volitional initial-condition selection
  • Matt 25:31–46 The Sheep and Goats judgment — canonical binary. Two groups, two outcomes. No third category. The text explicitly places sheep on the right (Heaven, Φ = 1 basin) and goats on the left (Hell, Φ = 0 basin). Maps directly to the two-basin partition.
  • Rev 3:16 Lukewarmness explicitly rejected — "I am about to spit you out of my mouth." This is a description of an active, structural instability, not merely a suboptimal state. The theological claim is that the middle state cannot be maintained. Maps to the positive eigenvalue at Φ = 0.5.
  • Deut 30:19 "I have set before you life and death… choose life." The binary structure is presented as the fundamental eschatological choice. The volitional element maps to initial-condition selection: which basin does Φ0 fall into? Two options, one selection, permanent consequence — mirrors the basin structure exactly.
Mathematical Verification — Rigorous Theorem

The dynamical systems claim is mathematically rigorous. A cubic polynomial f(Φ) = αΦ(1−Φ)(Φ−Φc) with 0 < Φc < 1 and α > 0 necessarily has exactly three real roots in [0, 1], with the two outer roots stable and the inner root unstable. This is not an approximation — it is an exact property of this class of equations.

No Third Attractor Found

A systematic search for a third stable eschatological state in Christian theology has not yielded a candidate satisfying the kill condition. Purgatory is a transient process state within the Heaven basin. Annihilationism preserves the binary topology by redefining Φ = 0 as non-existence rather than torment. The two-terminus partition holds across major Christian traditions.

Caveat — Location of Φc

Φc = 0.5 is chosen on grounds of maximal symmetry, not derived from first principles. Scripture indicates that a binary separation exists but does not specify the exact proportions of humanity in each basin. The existence of the saddle is well-grounded; its precise location is not yet derivable from deeper principles.

Caveat — Static Grace Model

The model treats grace as a discrete basin-shifting event (ISO-013). In reality, grace acts continuously over a lifetime. A complete model would require coupling ISO-016 to ISO-013 explicitly, producing a time-varying Φc(t) or an additional driving term. The current model captures the topology of grace but not its dynamics.

Honest Assessment — Imposed or Emergent?

The binary attractor structure is partly imposed through the choice of dynamics. The logistic cubic produces two stable fixed points because it is a product of Φ and (1−Φ), which naturally creates zeros at the endpoints. A different dynamical equation could produce the same topology with different basin geometry. The bimodal structure emerges from the dynamics, but the dynamics were chosen to produce it. This is why the ISO remains at Testing: the dynamics should be derived from first principles rather than postulated.

Implications & Connections

Grace as basin-shifting · connected ISOs · theological implications

The most significant implication of ISO-016 is its account of grace as a basin-shifting operator. Without external intervention, a trajectory with Φ0 < 0.5 will inevitably converge to Φ = 0. The only mechanism for reaching Φ = 1 from a below-threshold initial condition is an operator that moves the trajectory across the saddle boundary. This is precisely the function of divine grace in Christian soteriology.

Key Implication — Grace as Topological Intervention

The grace operator (ISO-013) acts by shifting the effective initial condition from Φ0 < 0.5 to Φ0′ > 0.5. Without this external intervention, self-evolution cannot change the basin — a result consistent with ISO-012 (self-evolution cannot alter the eigenvalue structure). Grace is not merely a force that accelerates convergence to Φ = 1; it is a topological intervention that changes which attractor the trajectory converges to. This gives a precise mathematical meaning to the theological claim that salvation cannot be self-generated.

ISO-016 also provides a precise account of why lukewarmness is not a stable spiritual state. The Revelation 3:16 passage describes an active rejection of the lukewarm state, mapping to the positive eigenvalue at Φ = 0.5: the system itself provides a force that drives the trajectory away from the saddle. This is not metaphor — the mathematical structure literally cannot sustain the middle state, just as theology cannot sustain spiritual indecision as a permanent condition.

The model also predicts critical slowing down near the saddle: trajectories near Φ = 0.5 evolve very slowly before accelerating toward one attractor. This maps to the theological intuition that conversion decisions are often preceded by extended periods of agonizing indecision — the experiential signature of saddle-point dynamics.

Weakest Link

The value Φc = 0.5 is assumed on grounds of maximal symmetry rather than derived from first principles. Additionally, the model does not account for the temporal dynamics of Φ(t) during a human lifetime — only the terminal basin is identified, not the trajectory shape. The ISO should remain at Testing until the governing dynamics are derived from deeper principles (e.g., from the coherence action functional) rather than postulated.

Strongest Link

The binary terminus structure is a rigorous mathematical theorem: the logistic cubic on [0, 1] with Φc ∈ (0, 1) has exactly two stable fixed points and one unstable fixed point. The theological claim (Matt 25:31-46) is among the most consistently attested themes in the New Testament. The structural correspondence between these two claims is direct and precise. The swap test passes without modification.

Open Questions for Future Research

(1) Can the location of Φc be derived from first principles rather than assumed at 0.5? (2) What is the distribution of human Φ0 values prior to the grace operator? (3) How does ISO-016 interact with the continuous action of grace over a lifetime, rather than a single basin-crossing event? (4) Does the model survive modification to a Langevin equation formulation (stochastic perturbations), and does noise-induced crossing of the saddle have a theological interpretation?
