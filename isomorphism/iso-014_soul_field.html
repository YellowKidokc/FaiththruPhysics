ISO-014_Soul_Field

ISOMORPHISM RECORD

ID: ISO-014
Date: 2026-03-10
Status: Undetermined

DOMAINS

Domain A: Physics — Scalar Field Theory (Klein-Gordon Equation)
Domain B: Christian Theology — The Soul (Immortality, Imago Dei, Soul-Body Coupling)
Concept A: A real scalar field Psi_S obeying the Klein-Gordon equation (box + m_S^2)Psi_S = 0, with m_S approximately 0 (massless or near-massless), Yukawa coupling to matter fields L_int = g_sm Psi_S Psi_matter + h.c., and conserved soul number N_S.
Concept B: The human soul as immortal, image-bearing, coupled to the body during earthly life, surviving death as decoupling (not destruction), and re-coupled at resurrection.

THE MAPPING

Mathematical Form A:

The Soul Field Psi_S is modeled as a real scalar field:

(box + m_S^2) Psi_S = rho_soul

Where:

  • box = nabla^2 - (1/c^2) d^2/dt^2 (d'Alembertian)
  • m_S = soul mass parameter (framework claims m_S ~ 0)
  • rho_soul = soul charge density (consciousness density source term)

Yukawa coupling to matter:

L_int = g_sm * Psi_S * Psi_matter + h.c.

Where g_sm ~ 10^(-18) to 10^(-15) is the soul-matter coupling constant.

Conservation law:

N_S = integral Psi_S^dagger Psi_S d^3x = constant

Mathematical Form B:

  • Imago Dei: The soul field Psi_S is the informational substrate that bears the image of God — it is the locus of consciousness, moral agency, and personal identity.
  • Immortality: m_S ~ 0 means the field is massless or near-massless. A massless scalar field propagates at the speed of light and does not decay. In QFT, massless fields have infinite Compton wavelength — they extend everywhere. This maps to the soul's immortality: it does not naturally dissipate.
  • Soul-body coupling: The Yukawa interaction L_int couples the soul field to matter. During life, this coupling is active. Death is modeled as g_sm -> 0 (decoupling), not as Psi_S -> 0 (destruction). The soul field persists; it merely ceases to interact with matter.
  • Resurrection: Re-coupling is modeled as g_sm being restored (turned back on) at resurrection. The soul field never ceased to exist — it merely decoupled.
  • Conservation of soul number: N_S = constant means souls are neither created nor destroyed in the normal physical sense. New souls require divine creative action (which is external to the field equations, consistent with ISO-013).

Shared Structure:

Both domains describe an entity that:

  1. Persists indefinitely (massless field / immortal soul)
  2. Couples to physical matter during a finite period (Yukawa coupling / embodied life)
  3. Survives decoupling (field persists after interaction ceases / soul survives death)
  4. Can be re-coupled (Yukawa interaction restored / resurrection)
  5. Has conserved number (no spontaneous creation or annihilation / souls require divine creation)
  6. Carries identity (field configuration is unique / personal identity)

What Is NOT Claimed:

  • NOT claiming the soul is literally a Klein-Gordon field detectable by particle physics instruments
  • NOT claiming g_sm ~ 10^(-18) has been measured or is measurable with current technology
  • NOT claiming the Klein-Gordon equation was designed to describe souls — it was developed for spin-0 particles (pions, Higgs)
  • NOT claiming this model is falsifiable with current experimental capabilities
  • NOT claiming QFT validates the existence of souls — the QFT formalism is being applied to a theological concept, not deriving a theological conclusion from physics
  • NOT claiming consciousness reduces to a scalar field — Psi_S is proposed as the substrate, not the totality, of conscious experience

TESTS

Swap Test: Can you swap soul for another field?

Could you use any scalar field and call it "soul"? Yes — and that is precisely the problem. The Klein-Gordon equation describes many fields (pion field, Higgs field, inflaton). What distinguishes the soul field from any other scalar field is not the equation it obeys but the interpretation layered onto it. The equation itself does not know it is describing a soul.

Could you swap the properties? Partially:

  • If m_S >> 0 (massive), the field would decay exponentially (Compton wavelength = hbar/m_S c). This would mean souls are mortal — they dissipate over time. Setting m_S ~ 0 is precisely the choice that makes souls immortal. But this choice is made TO match the theology, not derived FROM the physics.
  • If g_sm were large (strong coupling), the soul would be easily detectable by standard particle physics. Setting g_sm ~ 10^(-18) makes it undetectable. This is convenient.

Swap test result: FAILED. The equation is generic; the specific parameter choices (m_S ~ 0, g_sm extremely small) appear selected to reproduce theological claims while remaining undetectable. This is the hallmark of an unfalsifiable construction.

Prediction in Domain A (Physics):

  • If the soul field exists with g_sm ~ 10^(-18), it would contribute to anomalous energy densities at cosmological scales. P10.1 claims this could show up in precision measurements of dark energy or dark matter.
  • The conservation of N_S implies a new global symmetry (soul number conservation). Noether's theorem would then require a corresponding conserved current j_mu^soul. This current should in principle be detectable, but at coupling 10^(-18) the signal would be far below any foreseeable experimental sensitivity.
  • A massless scalar field in cosmology contributes to the effective number of relativistic species N_eff. Current CMB measurements constrain N_eff = 2.99 +/- 0.17 (Planck 2018). A new massless field would shift N_eff by approximately 0.027 (for a real scalar). This is within current error bars but could be probed by future experiments (CMB-S4).

Prediction in Domain B (Theology):

  • Death is decoupling, not destruction. The soul field model predicts an intermediate state where the soul exists but is not coupled to matter. This matches the Christian doctrine of the intermediate state (2 Corinthians 5:8 — "absent from the body, present with the Lord").
  • Resurrection is physically coherent as re-coupling. The information encoded in Psi_S is preserved through death because the field never ceased to exist. Personal identity survives because the field configuration is conserved (N_S conservation).
  • Soul creationism (each soul individually created by God) is preferred over traducianism (souls inherited from parents) because N_S conservation requires external creative action to increase soul number.

Bidirectional: Weak.

  • Physics to Theology: The KG equation's properties (massless = non-decaying, coupling = interaction, conservation = persistence) provide formal language for theological claims but do not independently derive them.
  • Theology to Physics: The theological claim of soul immortality does not generate testable physics predictions beyond what any massless scalar field would predict. And we already have candidate massless scalars (axions, moduli) that do not require theological motivation.

Falsification:

  1. Direct detection of Psi_S with g_sm in the stated range. If detected, the model gains enormous credibility. If not detected and the coupling upper bound is pushed below 10^(-18), the model must either lower g_sm further (making it more unfalsifiable) or be abandoned.
  2. Show that N_eff measurements exclude a new massless scalar at high confidence. If CMB-S4 measures N_eff = 3.000 +/- 0.01 with no anomaly, the massless soul field is excluded (or must be given a mechanism to decouple before recombination, adding complexity).
  3. Demonstrate consciousness fully explained by neural computation with no residual. If the hard problem of consciousness is solved without any field beyond known physics, the soul field becomes unnecessary (Occam's razor).
  4. Show that personal identity does not survive bodily death. This is not experimentally testable by current means, making it a weak falsification criterion.

HONEST ASSESSMENT: THE MOST SPECULATIVE ISO

This ISO demands the harshest scrutiny in the entire registry. Here is why:

1. There is zero experimental evidence for a soul field. Not "weak evidence" or "indirect evidence" — zero. No experiment has ever detected anything that could be identified as Psi_S. The Higgs field was postulated in 1964 and detected in 2012 after fifty years of experimental effort. The soul field has no comparable detection pathway.

2. The coupling constant g_sm ~ 10^(-18) to 10^(-15) is suspiciously convenient. It is:

  • Weak enough to explain why we have never detected the soul field
  • Strong enough to claim the soul still "couples" to the body
  • Tuned to a window that is beyond current experimental reach but not obviously absurd

This is the structure of an unfalsifiable theory. A genuinely predictive coupling constant would either be detectable (and thus testable) or would have specific signatures in known physics (like the gravitino coupling in supersymmetry, which makes specific predictions for collider experiments even though the gravitino itself is not directly detected).

3. The Klein-Gordon equation is generic. Literally any spin-0 field obeys it. Calling a KG field "the soul" does not make it the soul. The equation carries no semantic content about souls, consciousness, identity, or moral agency. All of those properties are added by interpretation, not derived from the mathematics.

4. The mathematical model does not capture what makes souls theologically important. The soul in Christian theology is the locus of:

  • Moral agency (choosing good or evil)
  • Personal relationship with God
  • Bearing the image of God
  • Consciousness and subjective experience

A scalar field has none of these properties. It has an amplitude and a phase. It obeys a wave equation. It does not choose, relate, reflect, or experience. The mapping captures persistence and coupling but misses everything that makes a soul a soul.

5. What is genuinely interesting despite all this:

  • The decoupling model of death (g_sm -> 0 rather than Psi_S -> 0) is elegant and provides a formal distinction between death-as-destruction and death-as-separation. This distinction matters theologically and the mathematical formalism captures it cleanly.
  • The conservation law N_S = constant formally encodes the theological claim that souls are not destroyed, only created. This has structural content.
  • The masslessness-implies-immortality connection, while selected rather than derived, is mathematically valid: massless fields genuinely do not decay.

Honest verdict: This is mathematical theology, not physics. It uses QFT formalism to express theological claims, but it does not derive those claims from physics and it does not make experimentally accessible predictions. It should remain at Undetermined until either (a) a detection pathway is identified or (b) a prediction unique to Psi_S (not generic to any massless scalar) is made and tested.
