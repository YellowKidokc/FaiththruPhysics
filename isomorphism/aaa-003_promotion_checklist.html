01

Level 1 → Level 2

Analogy → Structural Analogy (Correspondence)
Current State
What you currently have
  • A verbal or intuitive similarity between a physics concept and a theological concept
  • No mathematics on either side
 Required Deliverables
  • Mathematical form for Domain A (physics) Write the governing equations, define the state space, identify relevant symmetries and conservation laws. Name the standard formulation explicitly — e.g., "Landau-Ginzburg free energy", "Navier-Stokes", "Maxwell's equations". Citing "physics" without an equation is not this step.
  • Mathematical form for Domain B (theology) This is the hard part. Define a formal structure — at minimum an ordered set, a group, an algebra, or a category. "Theology says X" is not mathematical form. You need: objects, morphisms (or operations), and at least one non-trivial relation.
  • A morphism F: A → B preserving at least one operation Not just "these correspond" — show explicitly that F(x * y) = F(x) * F(y) for at least one operation *. Assertion of correspondence is not a morphism.
  • One worked example with concrete values Abstract notation alone is not sufficient. Demonstrate the preservation with actual values or elements — not just symbols. If you can't fill in the numbers, the morphism isn't ready yet.
Common Failure Mode

Treating a pictorial similarity (shape of a curve, shared terminology) as if it already defines a morphism. "Energy flows like water" is not structural until you supply the differential equations and the variable dictionary. The images are Level 1. The equations are Level 2.

Time estimate: Weeks to months. If the physics is standard, the bottleneck is formalizing Domain B. Expect this to be the hardest step — most ISOs stall here for longer than expected.

02

Level 2 → Level 3

Structural Analogy → Isomorphism
Current State
What you currently have
  • Both domains formally specified
  • A partial structure-preserving map (preserves some operations, not all)
  • Possibly a one-directional functor
 Required Deliverables — ALL seven
  • Complete functor F: A → B With explicit formulas for all objects and morphisms. "I can describe it" is not the same as "I have written it."
  • Inverse functor G: B → A With explicit formulas — not just "I could write one in principle." Writing G is where most ISOs fail. The inverse reveals every place where the mapping doesn't close.
  • Proof that GF = id_A and FG = id_B Up to natural isomorphism if working with categories. This is a proof, not an assertion. Write it out.
  • Verification that ALL operations are preserved Not just the ones you highlighted in the paper. A bijection is necessary but not sufficient. Check every algebraic and topological structure in both domains.
  • At least one non-trivial, non-arbitrary prediction The isomorphism must make a prediction in one domain that was NOT used to construct the mapping in the other domain. If every prediction is something you already knew, the mapping is unfalsifiable.
  • Testable prediction (in principle) Even if not yet tested, the prediction must be precise enough to be testable. "Theology and physics are connected" is not testable. "Legalistic communities exhibit autoimmune-like pathology at rate X" is testable.
  • Non-Arbitrariness Test — MUST pass The mapping cannot be equally well replaced by a different theological target. If gravity maps to sin, could it equally map to grace, or love, or doubt? If yes, the mapping is arbitrary and stays at Level 2. This test kills more ISOs than any other.

The Non-Arbitrariness Test is the gate most ISOs fail silently. Before claiming Level 3, ask: if I tried to map the same physics to a DIFFERENT theological concept, would the mapping be worse? If the answer is "not really," the isomorphism is arbitrary and doesn't belong at Level 3.

Common Failure Mode

Demonstrating a bijection on underlying sets but ignoring higher-level structure (topology, smooth structure, monoidal product). A bijection is necessary but not sufficient. You must check that the operations work, not just that the elements correspond.

Time estimate: Months to a year. This is where most ISOs stall. The bottleneck is usually the inverse functor G. Writing G reveals all the places where the mapping doesn't quite close — and that's useful. If G can't be written, the mapping is Level 2, and that's the honest answer.

03

Level 3 → Level 4

Isomorphism → Physical Law / Identity
Current State
What you currently have
  • A rigorous mathematical isomorphism
  • Predictions that are testable in principle
  • No empirical confirmation
 Required Deliverables — ALL five
  • Operational definitions for ALL theological entities What exactly do you measure to get "moral entropy" or "grace rate" or "sanctification level"? These must be precise enough that two independent researchers would agree on how to measure them. Without this, Level 4 is impossible by definition.
  • A novel quantitative prediction Not "this is consistent with" existing data. The form is: "this predicts X = [specific number or range] under [specific conditions]." The prediction must be specific enough to be falsified.
  • An experiment or observational study Run the test. Collect the data. The isomorphism must survive contact with measurement — not just with argument.
  • Independent replication Someone not from the Faith Through Physics program must run the test and get a comparable result. In-house confirmation is not independent confirmation.
  • Surviving falsification across the parameter space The prediction must hold up not just in one case but across the conditions where the model makes predictions. A single confirming example does not establish a physical law.
Common Failure Mode

Claiming Level 4 because the isomorphism is elegant or because it predicts things that are "basically true." Level 4 requires measurement. Elegance is not evidence. Unification is not verification. This is not a special Faith Through Physics standard — it's the standard Maxwell had to meet with electromagnetic waves and that Hertz supplied.

Time estimate: Years, not weeks. This is a research program's worth of work. It requires: operationalization → experiment design → data collection → independent replication. For the Faith Through Physics registry, Level 4 is a long-horizon goal. The framework's best current entries are at Level 3. That is not a failure. Level 3 is rigorous. Level 4 is the frontier.

04

What Promotion Is Not

Four things that feel like progress but are not promotion
None of the following count as promotion
  • Getting another AI to agree the mapping is compelling AI agreement confirms that the mapping is plausible-sounding. It does not confirm structural preservation, non-arbitrariness, or novel predictive power. Language model consensus is not mathematical proof.
  • Finding more examples that fit the pattern Accumulating examples that are consistent with a mapping does not constitute promotion. A sufficiently flexible mapping fits many things. What matters is constraint — what the mapping rules out — and whether those exclusions are verified.
  • Writing a longer paper More words, more references, more elaboration: none of these are what the checklist requires. A one-page note with an explicit inverse functor and one confirmed prediction outranks a fifty-page paper with neither.
  • Invoking the framework's internal coherence "This fits the rest of Faith Through Physics" is not evidence for a specific ISO. Each isomorphism must pass its own tests on its own merits. Internal coherence is necessary but not sufficient for promotion.

"Promotion requires external evidence — something the mapping predicts that would be surprising if true and that wasn't already known when the mapping was constructed."

The Promotion Clock

Honest time estimates at each transition
Level 1 → Level 2
Weeks
If the physics is already standard, the bottleneck is formalizing Domain B. A researcher who knows the physics can often do this alone. The theological formalization requires either mathematical training or collaboration with a systematic theologian.
Level 2 → Level 3
Months–Year
The inverse functor problem. Writing G: B → A is where the work lives. If the mapping doesn't close, the honest response is to report why and stay at Level 2. This transition typically requires collaboration between a mathematician and a theologian.
Level 3 → Level 4
Years
Operationalization, experiment design, data collection, and independent replication. This is a research program, not a paper. For Faith Through Physics, Level 4 is the long-horizon goal. The framework's best current entries are at Level 3 — and that is rigorous, defensible, and worth publishing.

The framework is currently at Level 3 for its best entries. Level 4 requires an experimental program that does not yet exist. This is not a failure — it is an accurate statement of where the research stands. Level 3 means: rigorous, non-arbitrary, bidirectional, constrained. That is a meaningful epistemic position. It is not the same as proof. It is not nothing.
