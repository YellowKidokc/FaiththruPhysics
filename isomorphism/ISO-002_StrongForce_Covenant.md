# ISO-002: Strong Force ↔ Covenant Unity (Law 02)

## DOMAINS
**Domain A:** QCD — Strong Nuclear Force
**Domain B:** Theology — Covenant Theology
**Concept A:** Quarks are bound by gluons. Pulling quarks apart INCREASES the binding force (confinement). At close range, quarks move freely (asymptotic freedom). You cannot isolate a single quark — the energy required to separate them creates new quark-antiquark pairs.
**Concept B:** Covenant bonds strengthen under pressure. Persecution deepens faith communities. Attempting to tear apart a covenant relationship increases its binding energy. The "asymptotic freedom" of covenant: within the bond, individuals are maximally free. Outside it, they are bound by the very act of separation.

## THE MAPPING
**Shared Structure:** Both exhibit confinement (can't isolate the bound unit), asymptotic freedom (freedom within the bond), and pair creation under separation stress (breaking a covenant creates new relational structures). The force INCREASES with distance — the opposite of gravity.
**Falsification:** If covenant bonds weakened under stress rather than strengthening, the mapping breaks. If persecution consistently destroyed faith communities rather than deepening them, the mapping breaks.
