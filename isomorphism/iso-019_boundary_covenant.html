The Boundary Condition Framework

The χ-field equations admit an infinite family of mathematical solutions. Boundary conditions (BCs) select the unique physical solution from this family by imposing constraints at the boundaries of the problem domain. Without sufficient BCs, the solution is underdetermined; with too many, it may be overconstrained.

The Theophysics framework identifies 8 boundary conditions BC1-BC8 as necessary to obtain a unique, physical solution for the χ-field:

02

The Eight Boundary Conditions: Analysis

The crucial distinction is between BCs derived from physical requirements versus BCs that encode theological presuppositions. The mapping is honest only if this distinction is made explicit.

Independently Derived
BC1 — Ghost-Freedom
No Negative-Norm States
Physics: Any physical theory must exclude ghost modes (negative kinetic energy states that violate unitarity and causality).
Covenant: Truth without internal contradiction — no aspect of the covenant undermines its own coherence.
Independently Derived
BC2 — Asymptotic Flatness
Field Decays at Infinity
Physics: The χ-field must vanish far from any source — otherwise it would produce infinite-range effects inconsistent with observation.
Covenant: Finite covenant scope — the covenant applies within its specified domain, not as a universal automatic mechanism.
Independently Derived
BC3 — Energy Positivity
Hamiltonian Bounded Below
Physics: The total energy must have a minimum. Unbounded below → the vacuum is unstable and reality collapses.
Covenant: Grounding in divine faithfulness — the covenant cannot fail at its foundation. God's reliability is the energy floor.
Independently Derived
BC4 — Observational Consistency
Matches CMB and LSS Data
Physics: Any proposed modification to GR/QFT must be consistent with precision cosmological observations (Planck CMB, DESI BAO, Euclid WL).
Covenant: Covenant must be consistent with experience — a covenant that contradicts observable reality fails its own claim.
Theologically Presupposed
BC5 — Personal Agency
Not an Impersonal Field
Physics: The χ-field is asserted to be more than a geometric quantity — it has agency.
Covenant: Covenant requires a person, not a mechanism. This BC encodes Christian theism; pantheism and deism fail it. Already assumes the conclusion.
Theologically Presupposed
BC6 — Historical Revelation
Logos Enters Time
Physics: The field must have a specific historical instantiation — not just a background constant.
Covenant: The Incarnation is the BC. This is distinctively Christian — the Logos "entered the channel in person" (ISO-035). Other traditions don't have this BC and would fail it by construction.
Theologically Presupposed
BC7 — Atonement Structure
Substitution Required
Physics: The field equation requires a source term that absorbs the "divergence" — sin's contribution to the stress-energy tensor must be renormalized.
Covenant: Penal substitutionary atonement. This is a specifically Reformed theological commitment. Other atonement theories would give different BCs.
Ambiguous
BC8 — Resurrection Boundary
Death Not Final
Physics: The temporal boundary condition — what happens as t → ∞? The system must have a defined end state, not diverge.
Resurrection as the final boundary condition. Ambiguous because "time has an end state" is potentially physical; "that end state is bodily resurrection" is theological.
03

The Circularity Problem: Acute

  Why This is the Registry's Most Vulnerable Isomorphism
  • 4 of 8 BCs are independent: Ghost-freedom, asymptotic flatness, energy positivity, and observational consistency are genuinely derived from physics requirements. They stand on their own.
  • 3 of 8 BCs are Christian-presuppositional: BCs 5, 6, and 7 encode theological commitments that already assume Christian theism. If the BCs are designed so only Christianity satisfies them, the "uniqueness" result is tautological.
  • The claim "Christianity satisfies 8/8" is trivially true if the BCs were designed around Christianity. The mapping gains evidential force ONLY if the BCs were derived independently and Christianity then happens to satisfy them — not if Christianity was used to choose the BCs.
  • Honest verdict from the source MD: "The circularity assessment is ACUTE." This is the most self-aware warning in the entire registry. It is preserved here in full.

The swap test fails for exactly this reason: the BCs on the physics side are legitimate independent constraints; the BCs on the theology side are partly constructed to select a predetermined outcome. The mapping is not symmetric — the physics constrains, but the theology is constrained BY the theology.

04

Paths to Strengthen This Isomorphism

The framework's source document identifies two paths. Both require significant work.

Path 1: Derive All 8 from Physics

Remove the circularity by deriving all 8 BCs from physical requirements alone — then check which theologies satisfy the result. If Christianity is the unique 8/8 satisfier when the BCs are set purely by physics, the result is non-trivial. This would be a genuine discovery. Currently, BCs 5–7 cannot be derived from physics alone. The path is open but long.

Path 2: Honest Partition

Partition the BCs into "independently derived" (BC1-BC4) and "theologically motivated" (BC5-BC7). Present the uniqueness claim only for the independently-derived subset. This is weaker — the claim becomes "Christianity satisfies the 4 physics-derived BCs plus 3 additional theological requirements" — but it is honest and non-circular for the physics portion.

Path 2 is the responsible immediate step. Path 1 is the research horizon. Until one of these is achieved, ISO-019 should remain at Candidate / Low confidence.

05
