The Claim

A falsifiable structural identity across three independently derived domains
FOUNDATIONAL META-ISOMORPHISM — all other ISOs derive from this one

Mathematical truth, moral truth, and the classical attributes of God share 24 independently verifiable ontological properties with zero exceptions. This is not analogy. It is structural identity, testable by finding a single property that belongs to one domain but not the others.

 Kill Condition

Find one property that genuinely belongs to mathematical truth but NOT moral truth (or NOT a divine attribute), or vice versa, that is not reducible to measurement domain. One genuine ontological mismatch kills the identity claim.

The Swap Test

Definition of structural identity — labels must be interchangeable
If you swap column headers — relabel "Mathematical Truth" as "Divine Attribute" and vice versa — does the table still read correctly?
YES.
Every row survives the swap. This is the definition of structural identity — the labels are interchangeable because the underlying structure is the same.
If you remove one column entirely, do the remaining two columns still hold independently?
YES.
Math ↔ God holds without the moral column. Math ↔ Morality holds without the God column. God ↔ Morality holds without the math column. Each pair is independently valid. The three-way convergence is stronger than any pair alone.

 Weakest Link — Property #14 (Good)

"Information theory has built-in oughts" is the most contested claim. If someone shows information theory is genuinely normatively neutral — no directional preference for coherence over noise — this row weakens. But Shannon's channel coding theorem contains normative prescription (R < C ⇒ ∃ code achieving reliable communication), which is an "ought" embedded in the definition of optimality.

 Strongest Link — Property #1 (Necessary)

Mathematical necessity is the most established claim in philosophy of mathematics. Even nominalists struggle with it. The necessity of mathematical truth is as close to philosophically uncontested as any claim in the field.
