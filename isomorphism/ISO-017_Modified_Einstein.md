# ISO-017: Modified Einstein Equation → Christological Mediation

## Domain A — Physics
The standard Einstein field equation is modified by adding a χ-field coupling term:

**G_μν + Λg_μν = (8πG/c⁴)T_μν + κχ_μν**

where:
- G_μν is the Einstein tensor (spacetime curvature)
- Λg_μν is the cosmological constant term
- T_μν is the stress-energy tensor (matter-energy content)
- **κχ_μν** is the χ-field contribution to spacetime geometry
- **κ ~ 10⁻⁶⁹** — extraordinarily small coupling constant

The χ-field term means spacetime geometry is not determined by matter-energy alone; the χ-field mediates an additional contribution to the curvature of spacetime.

## Domain B — Theology
Christological mediation:
- "All things were made through him, and without him was not any thing made that was made." (John 1:3)
- "He is before all things, and in him all things hold together." (Colossians 1:17)
- "He upholds the universe by the word of his power." (Hebrews 1:3)

The theological claim: the Logos/Christ is the mediating principle through which physical reality is structured and sustained.

## Mapping
| Physics | Theology |
|---|---|
| χ_μν couples to spacetime geometry | Christ mediates the structure of reality |
| κ ~ 10⁻⁶⁹ (subtle, nearly undetectable) | Divine sustaining is not empirically obvious |
| Without κχ_μν, standard GR holds approximately | Without Logos, physics appears self-sufficient |
| χ-field modifies but does not replace matter-energy | Christ works through creation, not against it |

## Swap Test: FAILED
The modified Einstein equation is **not distinguished from generic scalar-tensor gravity**. Brans-Dicke theory, f(R) gravity, quintessence models, and many other extensions all add similar terms to the Einstein equation. The χ-field coupling has the same mathematical form as any scalar-tensor modification.

There is nothing in the equation itself that requires a theological interpretation. The physics maps equally well onto any scalar field; the Christological reading is imposed, not derived.

## Falsification Condition
If the χ-field coupling could be shown to be **exactly zero** (κ = 0) — that is, if no additional scalar-tensor contribution to spacetime geometry exists beyond standard GR + Λ — the physical basis for the mapping vanishes.
