The Claim

ISO-009 · Universal Expansion ↔ Teleological History
Structural Isomorphism  ·  Z&sub2; Sign Inversion on Λ  ·  Medium Confidence  ·  Law 09
Domain A — Physical Cosmology

The universe expands from the Big Bang singularity toward a final state fixed by energy content and cosmological constant Λ. Expansion accelerates — dark energy overwhelms gravity at large scales. Final fate (heat death, Big Rip, de Sitter) is fixed by initial conditions plus Λ.

Domain B — Eschatology

Biblical history unfolds from creation toward a determined end state (eschaton). Prophetic density accelerates as history approaches its boundary. The fate of creation is fixed by divine decree (initial conditions) plus the Logos field (ΛLogos).

The universe expands from a singularity governed by the Friedmann equation: energy density, curvature, and cosmological constant Λ determine the expansion rate H at every epoch. Late-time dynamics are dominated by Λ, an inescapable background field that fixes the final state regardless of local fluctuations.

Biblical-eschatological history has the same mathematical form: expansion from a creation event, governed by moral-energy density, historical curvature, and a Logos field ΛLogos. Late-time prophetic dynamics are dominated by ΛLogos, an inescapable teleological attractor that fixes the eschaton regardless of local historical contingencies.

The isomorphism’s structural content is not mere expansion — that is generic. The content is the Z&sub2; sign inversion on Λ: the physical constant is repulsive (driving dispersion), while the Logos field is cohesive (driving convergence). Physical expansion mirrors spiritual contraction across a Z&sub2; reflection boundary.

What Is NOT Claimed

Prophecy is not literally cosmology; the eschaton is not a heat death. The claim is structural: history has the mathematical form of an expanding universe, and ΛLogos plays the role of Λ — but with inverted sign, producing convergence rather than dispersion.

K2: Λcosm were attractive (decelerating universe) → Z&sub2; anti-correlation collapses; both domains converge; isomorphism loses structural content.
