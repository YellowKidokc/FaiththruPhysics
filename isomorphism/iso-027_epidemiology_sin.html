The Claim

Sin spreads like disease — a little leaven leavens the whole lump
 Process Isomorphism · ISO-027 · Testing / High Confidence
"A little leaven leavens the whole lump." — 1 Corinthians 5:6 & Galatians 5:9 (ESV)

The SIR epidemiological model — which describes how diseases propagate through a susceptible population via contact with infected individuals — maps structurally and quantitatively onto sin propagation through communities. The isomorphism is not merely metaphorical: it is a formal process isomorphism in which every mechanistic feature of epidemic dynamics has a precise theological counterpart, and vice versa.

At its core: R&sub0; > 1 means exponential spread in both domains. Sin is contagious. It spreads through social contact, influence, imitation, and normalization. Church discipline is quarantine. The righteous remnant functions as herd immunity. Super-spreaders exist (Jeroboam son of Nebat). The Judges cycle is damped epidemic oscillation. Vaccination has a theological analog in temptation-resistance built through controlled encounter.

The key result: sin propagation follows contact-network topology, not random distribution. This is independently predicted by epidemiological theory and independently attested in Scripture. The most extensively documented transmission chain in the Bible — Jeroboam son of Nebat, referenced 21 times in Kings as "who caused Israel to sin" — is explicable in epidemiological terms: he lowered the barrier to infection (β↑) and removed recovery mechanisms (γ↓), thereby maximizing R&sub0; for northern-kingdom idolatry.

 Kill Condition

This isomorphism is falsified if any of the following are demonstrated:

(1) Sin propagation follows random rather than contact-network patterns — i.e., individuals who are socially isolated from sinning communities sin at the same rate as those embedded in them.

(2) Moral collapse in communities shows no threshold effects — i.e., there is no "herd immunity" phenomenon, no critical mass of the faithful below which societal moral collapse becomes inevitable.

(3) Isolated individuals are equally likely to sin as those in highly socially connected peer groups — which would falsify the contact-rate term βSI as the driver of spread.

Mathematical Form

SIR differential equations & the basic reproduction number
The SIR model partitions a population of size N into three compartments: Susceptible (S), Infected (I), and Recovered/Removed (R). The dynamics are governed by three coupled ordinary differential equations parameterized by the transmission rate β and the recovery rate γ.
SIR System — Epidemiological Form
dS/dt = −βSI dI/dt = βSI − γI dR/dt = γI
Population is conserved: S(t) + I(t) + R(t) = N for all t. The system is nonlinear due to the product term βSI representing the contact process.
Basic Reproduction Number
R&sub0; = β / γ
R&sub0; is the average number of secondary infections produced by one infected individual in a fully susceptible population. If R&sub0; > 1, the epidemic grows; if R&sub0; < 1, it dies out. R&sub0; is the central control parameter of the system.
Epidemic Threshold Condition
Epidemic grows iff R&sub0; = β/γ > 1 Epidemic dies out iff R&sub0; = β/γ < 1 Herd immunity threshold: p⊂c = 1 − 1/R&sub0;
The herd immunity threshold p⊂c gives the minimum fraction of the population that must be immune (recovered or resistant) to prevent epidemic growth. This maps to the righteous remnant threshold in theological terms.
Theological Mapping of Parameters
R&sub0;(sin) = β(sin) / γ(sin) where: β(sin) = contact rate × probability of moral transmission per contact γ(sin) = rate of repentance / accountability / recovery from sin S = individuals susceptible to this particular sin I = individuals actively propagating this sin in community R = individuals who have repented / become resistant
Jeroboam maximized R&sub0;(idolatry) by: (1) increasing β — institutionalizing idolatry at Dan and Bethel, making contact with idolatrous practice unavoidable; and (2) decreasing γ — removing Levitical priests and changing the festival calendar, eliminating recovery mechanisms.

Evidence & Verification

Swap test, bidirectional prediction, and empirical confirmation
TEST-01 Swap Test  Passed

The swap test requires that substituting domain-A concepts into domain-B statements (and vice versa) generates non-trivial, independently testable predictions — not mere tautologies. Three substitutions were performed:

  • "Jeroboam was a super-spreader with R&sub0; >> 1" — predicts that his influence should show exponential, not linear, propagation through the northern kingdom. The biblical record confirms: every single northern king is described as continuing his sins. This is not linear diffusion — it is the reproductive cascade pattern of a high-R&sub0; pathogen in a susceptible population with no effective quarantine.
  • "Church discipline is quarantine" — predicts that failure to practice discipline should result in infection spread. Paul explicitly argues this in 1 Cor 5:6-7: "Don't you know that a little yeast leavens the whole batch of dough? Get rid of the old yeast." Paul's reasoning is epidemiological: leaving the infected individual in the community threatens the whole community. The prediction is confirmed by explicit apostolic argument.
  • "The righteous remnant functions as herd immunity" — predicts a critical threshold below which societal moral collapse becomes inevitable. Abraham's negotiation for Sodom (Gen 18:22-33) explicitly and quantitatively explores this threshold: Would 50 righteous save it? 45? 30? 20? 10? The text treats the threshold as real, negotiable, and consequential — exactly as epidemiologists treat the herd immunity threshold.
TEST-02 Bidirectional Prediction  Passed

A genuine isomorphism should generate predictions in both directions: from Domain A into Domain B, and from Domain B into Domain A.

  • Forward (Epidemiology → Theology): SIR dynamics predict that sin propagation should follow contact-network topology, not random distribution. This is testable: sin patterns in communities should correlate with social network structure. Individuals with high-degree connectivity in social networks should show higher sin-exposure rates than socially isolated individuals — not because they are inherently weaker, but because βSI requires contact.
  • Reverse (Theology → Epidemiology): The Judges cycle (sin → oppression → cry → deliverance → sin) predicts epidemic oscillation. This is exactly what SIR models produce: damped oscillations in endemic disease around an equilibrium prevalence. The Judges cycle is not arbitrary narrative structure — it is the expected dynamical behavior of a system with R&sub0; near 1 where recovery mechanisms are imperfectly sustained.

Primary Case Study
Jeroboam Son of Nebat — The Biblical Super-Spreader
21× referenced in 1-2 Kings 19 northern kings after him 19/19 followed his sin pattern R&sub0; >> 1 estimated

The phrase "the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, who caused Israel to sin" appears 21 times in 1-2 Kings. This is the most extensively documented transmission chain in Scripture. The epidemiological framework does not merely describe what happened — it explains why the transmission was so complete and persistent.

Jeroboam did not merely sin. He created infrastructure for transmission:

  • Set up golden calves at Dan and Bethel (permanent transmission nodes at population centers)
  • Appointed non-Levitical priests (removed qualified recovery agents; γ↓)
  • Changed the festival calendar (replaced recovery rituals with transmission-amplifying ones)
  • Made idolatrous practice institutionally mandatory (β↑ for the entire population)

In epidemiological terms, he simultaneously increased β (transmission rate) by making contact with idolatrous practice unavoidable, and decreased γ (recovery rate) by dismantling the mechanisms of return. The result is the highest possible R&sub0; for northern-kingdom idolatry — and the biblical record shows 100% transmission to all subsequent northern kings, exactly as high-R&sub0; epidemic dynamics predict in a population with no effective quarantine or recovery mechanism.

The 21-times repetition of the formula in Kings is not literary redundancy — it is the author marking every node in the transmission chain back to patient zero.

 Strongest Confirmation

The Abraham / Sodom negotiation (Gen 18:22-33) independently and explicitly models the herd immunity threshold concept: a small number of righteous individuals can protect a large population from moral-epidemic collapse. Abraham negotiates the threshold downward from 50 to 10 — exactly the kind of threshold sensitivity analysis that epidemiologists perform. The text treats this threshold as mathematically real and morally consequential.

 Caveat — Non-Biological Mechanism

The biological SIR model assumes physical pathogen transmission via contact. The theological domain operates via social, psychological, and spiritual mechanisms — imitation, normalization, influence, temptation. The isomorphism is at the process level (identical mathematical structure), not at the mechanism level (the how of transmission differs). This does not weaken the isomorphism — many physical systems share SIR-type dynamics without shared mechanisms — but it must be noted.

Implications & Connections

Theological consequences, related isomorphisms, and broader POF context
ISO-027 has several significant theological and practical implications, and connects to multiple other isomorphisms in the registry.

Theological Implications

 Implication 1 — Church Discipline is Not Optional

If sin propagates via SIR dynamics with R&sub0; > 1, then failure to exercise church discipline is not merely permissive — it is epidemiologically catastrophic. The tolerant community that refuses to quarantine an actively sinning member does not thereby maintain peace; it allows the epidemic to grow. Paul's reasoning in 1 Cor 5 is not harsh; it is simply correct. The model vindicates the apostolic practice.

 Implication 2 — Community Size Matters

Below the righteous-remnant threshold p⊂c = 1 − 1/R&sub0;, even a community that is mostly faithful cannot prevent moral-epidemic spread if the faithful fraction falls below critical mass. This has direct implications for church health metrics: percentage of the congregation in active discipleship, accountability, and sacramental practice is not merely a spiritual concern — it determines whether the community is above or below its herd-immunity threshold.

 Implication 3 — Network Structure Matters

SIR dynamics on networks show that hub nodes (highly connected individuals) are both higher-risk for infection and disproportionately responsible for spread if infected. This predicts that leaders who sin spread sin exponentially faster than laypeople who sin. The consistent biblical emphasis on stricter judgment for teachers and leaders (James 3:1, Luke 12:48) is not arbitrary — it reflects correct network epidemiology.

 Implication 4 — Quantitative Hamartiology

ISO-027 opens the possibility of a quantitative hamartiology: estimating R&sub0; for specific sins in specific communities from observable data (rates of spread, recovery, relapse). This is not currently done in either church practice or academic theology, but the SIR framework makes it mathematically tractable. Different sins in different cultural contexts would have different R&sub0; values — and different intervention strategies would be optimal for each.

 Weakest Link

The quantitative R&sub0; for specific sins in specific communities has not been empirically estimated. The isomorphism is structurally confirmed but the quantitative parameters remain unmeasured. It is not yet possible to say, for instance, what the R&sub0; for pornography addiction is in a given congregation, or what the herd immunity threshold is for a community of 200. Estimating these would require longitudinal social network data on sin transmission patterns — methodologically difficult and ethically complex. Until such estimates exist, the isomorphism operates at the qualitative-structural level rather than the full quantitative level that the mathematics supports.

 Strongest Link

The Jeroboam super-spreader case and the Sodom herd-immunity-threshold negotiation are both extensively documented in Scripture, both independently predicted by epidemiological theory, and both show the precise quantitative signatures that the SIR model predicts — exponential cascade in the Jeroboam case (100% transmission to all 19 subsequent northern kings), and threshold-sensitivity analysis in the Sodom case (Abraham's step-by-step negotiation of the minimum righteous-remnant size). These two examples are sufficient to establish the structural validity of the isomorphism at high confidence.

 Open Research Questions

(1) Can R&sub0;(sin) be empirically estimated for specific sins in historically documented communities (e.g., the spread of specific heresies through early church records)?

(2) Does the SIR model's prediction of damped oscillations around an endemic equilibrium match observed revival/apostasy cycle frequencies in documented church history?

(3) Do the SEIR, SIRV, or network-SIR extensions (which add Exposed, Vaccinated, or heterogeneous network topology) map onto additional theological mechanisms not captured in the basic 10-element table above?

(4) Does accountability group structure (small groups, confession, spiritual direction) empirically increase γ (recovery rate) in measurable ways, consistent with the isomorphism's predictions?

 Registry Note
