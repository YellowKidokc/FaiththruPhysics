# ISO-015: Moral Physics — Coherence as Virtue Metric

## Domain A — Information Theory
Coherence C[χ] is a measurable quantity in information-theoretic terms. A system's coherence increases when its internal states become more mutually consistent, and decreases when contradictions, noise, or disorder accumulate.

Shannon entropy H = −Σ p_i log p_i measures disorder. Coherence moves inversely to entropy in structured systems: higher coherence corresponds to lower informational entropy within the relevant degrees of freedom.

The key quantity is **ΔC** — the change in coherence resulting from an action or process.

## Domain B — Theology
Natural law tradition holds that:
- Moral truths are embedded in the structure of reality (Romans 2:14-15)
- Virtue is alignment with that structure; vice is deviation from it
- Conscience functions as a detector of moral alignment — an internal sense of whether an action coheres with the moral order

## Shared Structure
| Information Theory | Theology |
|---|---|
| ΔC > 0 (coherence increases) | Virtue — action aligns with moral order |
| ΔC < 0 (coherence decreases) | Sin — action introduces disorder |
| C[χ] is measurable | Moral consequences are observable over time |
| Coherence gradient | Conscience — detects local ΔC direction |
| Entropy accumulation | Corruption, decay, death |

The claim: **virtue is that which increases coherence; sin is that which decreases it.** Conscience functions as a local coherence gradient detector — it senses whether an action moves the system toward or away from coherence.

## Evidence
- **World Values Survey trust data**: societies with higher interpersonal trust (a coherence proxy) show better outcomes across health, economic, and social metrics. Trust erosion (decoherence) correlates with institutional decay.
- This is suggestive, not conclusive. Correlation between trust-as-coherence and flourishing is real but the causal mechanism is not established.

## Swap Test: PARTIAL
The mapping works in the primary direction: virtue → coherence increase, vice → coherence decrease. However, **sin can temporarily increase local coherence** — a well-organized criminal syndicate has high internal coherence. The mapping requires specifying the scope: sin decreases *global* or *long-term* coherence even when it increases local/short-term coherence.

This is a genuine strain point, not a fatal flaw, but it requires the additional constraint that the coherence metric must be evaluated at the appropriate scale.

## Falsification Condition
If moral improvement showed **no correlation with measurable coherence metrics** at any scale — that is, if virtuous societies were no more coherent (by any information-theoretic measure) than vicious ones — the mapping breaks.
