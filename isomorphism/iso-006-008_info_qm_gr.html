ISO-006
Information Theory ↔ Logos
Law 06 · Confirmed

Information requires a channel, a source, and a receiver. The Logos is the channel through which divine information structures reality. Shannon's channel capacity theorem maps to the maximum rate at which truth can be communicated through a noisy (sin-corrupted) channel. Kolmogorov complexity measures the minimum description length — the Logos is the minimum sufficient description of reality itself. The Logos is the shortest program that generates all of observable creation; Shannon entropy is the measure of meaning-degradation through sin; channel capacity bounds the rate at which divine truth propagates through fallen creation without distortion.

 Kill Condition (ISO-006)

If reality could exist without information structure, the mapping breaks. If the universe were maximally random (K(x) = |x|, zero compressibility), no Logos-like compression principle exists and ISO-006 is falsified.

ISO-007
Quantum Mechanics ↔ Faith
Law 07 · Parallel

Superposition = holding multiple possibilities before measurement. Faith = holding commitment before evidence resolves. The observer effect establishes that consciousness participates in collapsing reality from possibility to actuality. Prayer operates as consciousness directed at specific outcomes — a directed collapse of the probability wavefunction at the macro-experiential level. PEAR Lab demonstrated consciousness affects random event generators at 6.35σ; the Global Consciousness Project showed correlated anomalies at 6σ across 325+ replicas. Faith is the structural posture of remaining in superposition while directing conscious intention toward a specific collapse.

 Kill Condition (ISO-007)

If consciousness is demonstrated to have zero effect on physical systems (PEAR/GCP fully debunked, replications return null) the mapping weakens significantly. If faith required zero uncertainty (pure propositional knowledge), it would not structurally map to superposition, which requires uncertainty over multiple eigenstates.

ISO-008
General Relativity ↔ Grace
Law 08 · Confirmed

Mass curves spacetime — objects follow geodesics (paths of least action) through curved space. Grace curves moral-spiritual spacetime — agents follow the path of least resistance through a grace-curved landscape. The equivalence principle (gravity = acceleration in a local frame) maps to the theological claim that grace = the natural trajectory of a being when properly aligned with its Source. The Cross is identified as the extremal path: the one trajectory through the moral-spiritual manifold satisfying all boundary conditions simultaneously (Creation, Fall, Redemption, Consummation) — just as a geodesic uniquely satisfies given boundary conditions in curved spacetime. Grace bends the manifold such that what was previously the steepest ascent becomes the natural descent.

 Kill Condition (ISO-008)

If grace operated instantaneously (point interaction) rather than through curvature-field effects (manifold deformation), the mapping breaks. If the Cross were shown to not satisfy δS = 0 over the theological manifold, or if no variational functional exists over that manifold, ISO-008 is falsified.
