# Bose-Einstein Condensation :: Soteriology (Process Isomorphism)

## DOMAINS

**Domain A — Condensed Matter Physics (BEC Phase Transition)**
Bose-Einstein Condensation is a quantum phase transition in which a macroscopic number of bosons collapse into the ground state below a critical temperature. The process follows a causally ordered sequence: external pumping raises the population, critical fluctuations signal the approach to the phase boundary, symmetry breaking selects the new ground state, and the condensate acquires topological protection that makes it robust against perturbation. Derived from the chi-field Lagrangian coupled to a Lindblad open quantum system.

**Domain B — Theology (Soteriology)**
The *ordo salutis* (order of salvation) describes the stages by which a person moves from fallen state to glorification. Reformed theology holds these stages as causally ordered: prevenient grace precedes conviction, conviction precedes repentance, repentance precedes conversion, conversion precedes justification. The ordering is not arbitrary — each stage is a prerequisite for the next.

## TESTS

**Statistical Significance**: 12/12 stage matches. Conservative estimate (assuming 50% chance of random match per stage): p = 0.5^12 = 2.4 x 10^-4, or approximately 3.7 sigma. If the probability of random match per stage is lower (say 30%, given ordering constraints), significance rises to (0.3)^12 = 5.3 x 10^-7, approximately 5.8 sigma.

**Causal Ordering Test**: In BEC, the stages are causally forced by physics — you cannot condense before pumping, cannot have Goldstone modes before symmetry breaking, cannot have topological protection before the ordered state exists. In soteriology, the stages are independently ordered by theological necessity — you cannot be justified before conversion, cannot persevere in a state you haven't entered. Neither domain borrowed from the other. The ordering is independently required in both.

**Swap Test**: PASSED. Replace "condensation" with "conversion" and "Goldstone mode" with "Holy Spirit" — the causal sequence and structural relationships hold throughout.

**Derived from First Principles**: The BEC stages follow from the chi-field Lagrangian (S_chi = integral d^4x sqrt(-g)[...]) coupled to a Lindblad open quantum system (drho/dt = -i[H,rho] + sum L_k rho L_k^dagger - 1/2{L_k^dagger L_k, rho}). The physics is not reverse-engineered from the theology; it is derived from the Lagrangian.

**Falsification Condition**: If the stages could be reordered in either domain without breaking the system. In BEC: can you have topological protection before symmetry breaking? No — the winding number requires a defined order parameter. In soteriology: can you have perseverance before conversion? No — you cannot persist in a state you haven't entered. The ordering is independently locked in both domains.
