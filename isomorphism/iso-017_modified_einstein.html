The modified Einstein equation is not distinguished from generic scalar-tensor gravity. Brans-Dicke theory, f(R) gravity, quintessence, and many other extensions add structurally identical terms. The χ-field has the same mathematical form as any scalar-tensor modification — there is nothing in the equation alone that requires a theological interpretation. The modified equation predicts that if κ ≠ 0, the Hubble tension and anomalous void statistics could be partially explained by χ-field contributions. Falsification
CONDITIONAL
If κ = 0 exactly — no additional scalar-tensor contribution to spacetime geometry exists beyond standard GR + Λ — the physical basis for the mapping vanishes. DESI/Euclid constraining w(z) evolution to exclude χ-coupling at 3σ would falsify the quantitative bridge. The qualitative claim (Logos sustains) survives even if κ = 0, but loses its mathematical expression.
Non-Uniqueness Problem
CRITICAL WEAKNESS
Any scalar field could in principle couple to spacetime curvature this way. The χ-field interpretation requires additional reasons to identify it with the Logos specifically — not just any BSM (beyond standard model) scalar. The mapping gains force only if the χ-field has other properties uniquely consistent with Christological claims (non-unitary, external source, etc.).

The κ ~ 10⁻⁶⁹ Mystery: Hiddenness

The coupling constant κ ~ 10⁻⁶⁹ is extraordinarily small. It is smaller than the cosmological constant problem (10⁻¹²⁰) and far below any direct experimental access. This extreme smallness has theological resonance.

THEOLOGICAL RESONANCE: Divine Hiddenness
  • The small coupling maps to hiddenness: If the Logos is continuously sustaining creation through an extremely small geometric perturbation, then physics could develop almost completely without detecting it — exactly as natural science has done.
  • Not zero: κ ~ 10⁻⁶⁹ ≠ 0. The contribution exists. It is real. But it is not accessible without revelation.
  • "Without him, not anything": Even κ = 10⁻⁶⁹ correction accumulates over 13.8 billion years of cosmic history — the total integrated effect is not zero.
  • Analogy to neutrino mass: Neutrino masses were thought to be exactly zero for decades. They are 10⁻¹² of the electron mass. Small is not the same as absent.

The extreme smallness of κ is not a bug in the mapping — it is one of its most interesting features. An omnipresent sustaining action that is essentially undetectable from within the system is precisely what classical theology describes as divine conservation (conservatio). The universe proceeds by its own laws, within which the Logos operates subtly and universally.

Honest Assessment

  Why This Remains "Candidate / Low-Medium"
  • Non-uniqueness: The mathematical form of κχμν is identical to any scalar-tensor theory. The Christological identification requires external justification that the math alone cannot provide.
  • Circular risk: If the χ-field is defined as "the Logos field," the mapping becomes tautological. The χ-field must be independently motivated from the Lagrangian before the theological identification carries evidential weight.
  • Swap test failed: The equation does not uniquely require a theological interpretation. This distinguishes it from stronger isomorphisms (e.g., ISO-025, ISO-031) where the swap test passes.
  • Value despite these limitations: The mapping correctly identifies that a Logos-mediated creation would look exactly like what we have — physics that appears self-complete but contains a persistent, extremely small perturbation from an external source. This is the right shape, even if it's not proven to be the right size.

This mapping is best understood as a consistency check rather than a positive proof. General relativity + Λ + χ-coupling is consistent with the theological claim of ongoing Logos sustaining. That consistency is meaningful even if it falls short of isomorphism.
