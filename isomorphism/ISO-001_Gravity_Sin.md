# ISO-001: Gravity ↔ Sin (Law 01)

## DOMAINS
**Domain A:** Classical Mechanics — Gravitational Force
**Domain B:** Theology — Hamartiology (Study of Sin)
**Concept A:** Every mass attracts every other mass with force inversely proportional to distance squared. Objects fall toward the center. Escape requires energy input exceeding gravitational binding energy.
**Concept B:** Sin operates as a downward pull on moral agents. Distance from the Source (God) increases the pull. Escape from sin requires external energy input (grace) exceeding the binding energy of habit, nature, and environment.

## THE MAPPING
**Mathematical Form A:** F = Gm₁m₂/r²
**Mathematical Form B:** F_sin = G_moral · m_agent · m_world / r_grace²
**Shared Structure:** Both describe attractive forces that increase with mass and decrease with distance. Both require energy input to escape. Both produce orbits (stable sin patterns) and collapse events (catastrophic moral failure). The inverse-square law governs both — doubling distance from Source quarters the pull.
**What Is NOT Claimed:** Sin is not literally gravity. Moral mass is not physical mass. The claim is structural: the same mathematical architecture governs both phenomena because both are manifestations of the same substrate operating at different measurement scales.

## TESTS
**Swap Test:** No — this mapping cannot be replaced with another pairing and preserve coherence. Gravity's specific properties (universal attraction, inverse-square, escape velocity) map uniquely to sin's properties.
**Prediction in A:** Systems with more mass collapse faster → civilizations with more moral mass (population × moral disorder) should show accelerating entropy.
**Prediction in B:** Grace must exceed gravitational binding energy → conversion experiences should show threshold effects, not gradual transitions.
**Bidirectional:** Yes — understanding gravity illuminates sin dynamics, and understanding sin illuminates why gravity is universal and attractive (not repulsive).
**Falsification:** If sin operated as a repulsive force (pushing people toward God), the mapping breaks. If moral decay followed a linear rather than inverse-square pattern, the mapping breaks.
