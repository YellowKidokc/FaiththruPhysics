The Core Claim

The process follows a causally forced 12-stage sequence: from vacuum state through external pumping, sub-threshold accumulation, critical fluctuations, symmetry breaking, condensation, ground state establishment, Goldstone mode emergence, topological protection, superfluid response, persistent current, and finally the critical velocity limit.

The Reformed ordo salutis — the order of salvation — describes the stages by which a person moves from the fallen state to glorification. It also follows a causally forced 12-stage sequence: fallen state, prevenient grace, common grace, conviction, repentance, conversion, justification, new spiritual freedom, perseverance, corporate coherence, sanctification, and the temptation threshold.

The stages match. The ordering is independently forced in both domains. Condensed matter physicists and Reformed theologians derived the same causal sequence from completely different first principles, in complete institutional isolation from one another. This is a process isomorphism — not a structural parallel between static states, but a dynamic causal sequence that preserves ordering, irreversibility, and stage-function across domains.
