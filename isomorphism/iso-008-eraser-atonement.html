4 Atonement Theories as Projections

Not competing theories — different measurement frames viewing the same event. Each historical atonement theory captures a real projection of the single retrocausal event onto a particular axis.

Penal Substitution

The sin-debt (S) multiplied by the cosmic-scale consequence (C) is paid by an agent with infinite moral mass (G). The infinite G-value is what makes the transaction balance — finite agents cannot generate sufficient payment.

Moral Influence
Extreme G display increases human O
The extreme display of God's moral gravity (G) at the Cross increases human openness (O) to realignment. The act is so extreme that it shifts the observer's measurement basis — like a photon that forces you to choose a new detector angle.
Recapitulation
Perfect s=+1 template demonstrated
Christ lives the perfect human trajectory (s=+1 at every measurement point), providing a template that recapitulates and reverses Adam's failed trajectory. The entire human story is re-run with a perfect input, overwriting the corrupted output.

⚠ Falsification Criteria

This claim carries its own kill condition. If any of the following are demonstrated, the isomorphism is demoted to Correspondence (Level 2) or Analogy (Level 1):

  • › Quantum eraser debunked: If the delayed-choice quantum eraser is definitively debunked — if the retrocausal correlations are shown to be an artifact — the physical side of the mapping collapses.
  • › No physical resurrection: If historical evidence conclusively demonstrated no physical resurrection, the "idler photon" detection event is removed and the retrocausal determination fails.
  • › Satanic retrocausal access: If a mechanism is discovered by which Satan could access retrocausal information, the Christus Victor temporal trap breaks — the adversary could have avoided the Cross.
  • › No eternal frame required: If the Cross can be fully explained without reference to any eternal frame — as a purely reactive, contingent event — the retrocausal structure evaporates.
