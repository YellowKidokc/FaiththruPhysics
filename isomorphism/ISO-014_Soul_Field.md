# ISO-014: Soul Field → Imago Dei / Immortality

## Domain A — Quantum Field Theory
Posit a scalar field Ψ_S governed by the Klein-Gordon equation:

**(□ + m_S²)Ψ_S = ρ_soul**

Parameters:
- **m_S ~ 0** (massless or near-massless): a massless field propagates indefinitely — encoding immortality.
- **g_sm ~ 10⁻¹⁸**: the soul-matter coupling constant is extraordinarily weak, making the field experimentally undetectable by current instruments.

Death is modeled as:
- **g_sm → 0** (decoupling of soul field from matter field)
- **NOT** Ψ_S → 0 (destruction of the soul field)

The field persists; only the coupling vanishes.

Resurrection would then be:
- **g_sm restored** (re-coupling of soul field to a material substrate)

## Domain B — Theology
- **Imago Dei** — humans bear a non-material image of God (Genesis 1:27)
- **Immortality** — the soul does not cease to exist at death (Matthew 10:28, "do not fear those who kill the body but cannot kill the soul")
- **Death as separation** — death is the separation of soul from body, not the annihilation of the soul (James 2:26, "the body apart from the spirit is dead")
- **Resurrection** — re-embodiment, soul re-coupled to a glorified body (1 Corinthians 15:42-44)

## Mapping
| QFT Feature | Theological Feature |
|---|---|
| m_S ~ 0 (massless) | Immortality of the soul |
| g_sm → 0 at death | Death as decoupling, not destruction |
| Ψ_S persists after decoupling | Soul survives bodily death |
| g_sm restored | Resurrection / re-embodiment |
| g_sm ~ 10⁻¹⁸ | Undetectable by material instruments |

## Honest Verdict
**This is mathematical theology, not physics.** It uses QFT formalism to express theological claims, but it does not derive those claims from physics. The Klein-Gordon equation is the most generic relativistic wave equation — any scalar field satisfies it. The parameters (m_S ~ 0, g_sm ~ 10⁻¹⁸) appear selected specifically to reproduce theological conclusions while remaining experimentally undetectable.

This is formalization, not discovery. The math adds precision to the theology but does not independently predict it.

## Swap Test: FAILED
The KG equation is generic; the parameters appear selected to reproduce theology while remaining undetectable. Swapping domains does not yield independent predictions — the physics side has no empirical content beyond what was put in by hand.

## Falsification Condition
If consciousness were shown to be **entirely eliminable at death with zero residual field** — that is, if a complete physicalist account of consciousness were established with no remainder — the soul-field posit becomes unnecessary and the mapping collapses.
