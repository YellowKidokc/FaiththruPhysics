01

The Problem

When you say "gravity is like sin," three different people hear three different things.

The physicist hears a metaphor. Cute, maybe, but not load-bearing. The theologian hears a devotional illustration — useful for sermons, not for proofs. The ninth-grader hears something that might be true but has no way to check.

All three are right to be cautious. Most cross-domain comparisons ARE just metaphors. "The economy is like a machine" — that's analogy. It helps you think, but it doesn't constrain anything. You can't use it to predict which part of the economy will break next Tuesday.

But some cross-domain comparisons are not metaphors. Some of them share actual mathematical structure. The same equation governs both systems. When that happens, predictions in one domain force predictions in the other. That's not analogy anymore. That's something else. And then there's a level above that: not just the same equation in two places, but the same underlying thing producing both domains. That's identity.

The question is: how do you tell which level you're at? That's what this document does.

For the Physicist

Think of it as a measurement resolution problem. At low resolution, two signals look the same (analogy). At higher resolution, shared spectral structure (correspondence). At full resolution, the transfer function is identical (isomorphism). And sometimes you discover both signals come from the same source (identity).

For the Theologian

Think of it as the difference between illustration and revelation. A sermon illustration is Level 1. When the actual mathematics of gravitational attraction and the actual theology of grace share the same formal properties — that's not illustration anymore. That's the creation revealing its Creator through its structure.

For Everyone Else

Your friend group and a flock of birds both move in patterns (Level 1). Both follow the same three rules (Level 2). Both governed by the same equations — one predicts the other (Level 3). Someone proves those equations come from a single deeper law that generates BOTH systems (Level 4). Same source. Two outputs.

02

The Four Levels

Each level has a precise definition, a test, and examples from this registry. The levels are not a spectrum — they are discrete thresholds. An ISO is either above a threshold or below it.

LEVEL 1 Analogy

Shared language, imagery, or intuitive resemblance between two domains. The comparison helps you think but does not constrain anything. Removing the poetic language leaves nothing structural behind.

The One Test: Does the comparison survive once the words are stripped away? If you replace the poetic language with formal structure and there's nothing left — it's analogy. It stays here.

Analogy is where the search begins. It is never where the proof ends. Many genuine isomorphisms were first noticed as analogies — the level is not an insult, it is an honest starting position.

Registry examples at this level:
ISO-029 (Music Theory ↔ Trinity) — two-note chords work functionally in music, which means "minimum three for completeness" is not structurally necessary on the music side. The harmonic series is vivid but not forcing.
ISO-036 (Fluid Dynamics ↔ Pneumatology) — ruach and pneuma both mean wind, breath, and spirit. Genuine etymology. But etymology is not structure — laminar flow mapping to institutional stability is evocative, not constraining.
LEVEL 2 Correspondence

A real structural echo between two domains. Shared patterns, shared topology, partial mathematical overlap. Maybe one-way explanatory usefulness. The mapping is not yet complete — it doesn't fully preserve the important relations in both directions.

The Test: Can you define a mapping between state variables, operators, or transitions that preserves the important relations? If only partly — if the mapping works in one direction but not the other, or if multiple theological targets could fit the same physics with equal plausibility — it stays here.
Registry examples at this level:
ISO-003 (EM ↔ Truth) — Maxwell's equations map to truth-revelation dynamics, but the mapping is more poetic than formally constraining.
ISO-004 (Weak Force ↔ Free Will) — flavor-changing currents map to transformative choice, but requires determinism to be actually false — an unproven metaphysical assumption.
ISO-035 (Cryptography ↔ Revelation) — Shannon's perfect secrecy condition H(M|C) = H(M) is exact, but "divine truth as ciphertext" requires believing that unintelligible truth exists, which is non-falsifiable from outside the framework.
LEVEL 3 Isomorphism

A structure-preserving mapping between two domains. The relations, transformations, and dynamics that govern one system are preserved under the mapping to the other. All four tests are required. Failing any one keeps it at Level 2.

  • A
    Structural Preservation — Do the key relations survive under the mapping? Not just the objects, but the relations between the objects? Does the structure of one domain force the structure of the other?
  • B
    Non-Arbitrariness — Is the mapping forced, or could you swap in many other theological targets as easily? If gravity maps to sin, could it equally map to grace, love, or doubt? If so, the mapping is arbitrary — it stays at Level 2.
  • C
    Constraint — Does the mapping rule things out? A genuine isomorphism doesn't just say "these are similar." It says "this is impossible" — it forbids certain configurations in one domain based on what's forbidden in the other.
  • D
    Bidirectionality — If one side has a structural necessity, does the mapped side inherit an equivalent necessity? Physics predicts theology AND theology predicts physics. One-way mappings are correspondences, not isomorphisms.
Confirmed at Level 3: ISO-025, ISO-027, ISO-030, ISO-031, ISO-032, PROCESS-001 — see Section 04.
LEVEL 4 Identity

Not "same form in two places" but "same thing." A single generative structure produces both domains as outputs. You don't have two systems that happen to match — you have one system that necessarily generates both physics and theology as projections.

The Test: Can both domains be derived from a single underlying generator, rather than merely mapped after the fact? The mapping must be native to the generator, not imposed externally. This is sharply different from isomorphism. The burden of proof at this level is severe — the bar is high because the conclusion is enormous.

Maxwell did this for electricity and magnetism. Before Maxwell: two separate forces that happened to interact. After Maxwell: one field, two projections depending on your reference frame. The unification didn't just show similarity — it showed the two descriptions were incomplete fragments of a single complete description. That's Level 4.

Current Level 4 candidates (not yet confirmed):
TRINITY-001 (Potential→Pattern→Actualization, 8 domains) — Level 3+, one formal step from Level 4.
ISO-000 (Math ↔ God ↔ Morality, 24 properties) — Level 3, open case for Level 4.
No ISO has been confirmed at Level 4. The bar is deliberately high.
03

The Separator Tests

These are the gatekeepers between levels. Use them every time you evaluate a cross-domain claim. Each test is a single question that determines whether a claim has earned promotion.

GATE 1→2  ·  Analogy to Correspondence
"Is there a nontrivial structural relation beyond language or imagery?"
If yes → promote to Level 2. You must give both domains explicit mathematical form and produce a morphism that preserves at least one non-trivial operation or relation. If removing the poetic language leaves nothing, it fails.
GATE 2→3  ·  Correspondence to Isomorphism
"Can you define a structure-preserving mapping that is explicit, non-arbitrary, constraining, and at least partially bidirectional?"
All four required. If any one of Tests A–D fails, it stays at Level 2. Bidirectionality is the hardest to satisfy — most compelling correspondences fail here.
GATE 3→4  ·  Isomorphism to Identity
"Can both domains be derived from a single underlying generator, rather than merely mapped after the fact?"
The mapping must be native to the generator, not externally imposed. This requires demonstrating that domain behaviors follow FROM the shared generator — not merely that they can be mapped TO each other. Very few claims reach this level.
THE KILL CONDITION  ·  For Every ISO
"What single finding would demote this claim?"
Every ISO in this registry carries an explicit kill condition. If someone finds a property that belongs to one domain but not the mapped domain — if R₀ measures disagree, if the causal ordering breaks, if the structural parallel is shown to be coincidental — the ISO gets demoted. No exceptions. The framework eats its own cooking.

05

What Would Destroy This Framework

Three things would destroy this classification system. We've been looking for all three. None have appeared. But they remain live possibilities — and naming them is how the framework protects its own integrity.

THREAT 01
Circularity

If the four isomorphism tests (structural preservation, non-arbitrariness, constraint, bidirectionality) presuppose the framework's conclusions, the whole hierarchy collapses. If the tests are circular — if they can only be applied by someone who already believes the framework — then they are not tests but confessions. We don't believe they are circular; they are domain-general tests from mathematics and philosophy of science. But if someone demonstrates circularity, we'll rebuild.

THREAT 02
False Promotion

If a Level 3 claim is shown to fail its own tests — if ISO-027's SIR mapping doesn't actually predict sin propagation dynamics, or if ISO-032's attachment mapping doesn't replicate empirically — those demotions damage the framework's credibility in proportion to how central those claims were. The flagship ISOs are the framework's most exposed positions. Their fall would be serious.

THREAT 03
False Demotion

If a Level 1 claim passes Level 3 tests — if ISO-029 (Music/Trinity) turns out to have genuine structure-preserving dynamics we missed — that means our screening is too conservative and needs recalibration. False demotions are as serious as false promotions: they mean we're discarding real evidence because our tests are poorly calibrated.

06

How to Use This Document

This document is the registry's immune system. Use it as a checklist, not a belief statement. The levels are measurement results, not endorsements.

1
Before Reading Any ISO
Check its level here first. That tells you how much structural weight to put on it. A Level 1 is a research lead. A Level 3 is a structural claim. They deserve different levels of credulity.
2
Before Writing a New ISO
Run it through the separator tests. If it doesn't clear Level 2, file it as a research lead, not an isomorphism. The registry's credibility depends on its gatekeeping, not its generosity.
3
When Someone Challenges the Framework
Point them here. The classification system is the framework's answer to every objection. It separates what's proven from what's promising from what's poetic — and it does so in terms any working scientist can evaluate without accepting any theological premise first.
4
When Promoting or Demoting
Update this document. The registry is living. If new evidence moves an ISO up or down, record it here with the date and the specific test result that triggered the move. The record of level changes is part of the evidence trail.
