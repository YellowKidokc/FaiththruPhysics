# ISO-011: Superposition → Mystery/Revelation

## Domain A — Quantum Mechanics
A quantum system exists in superposition — a coherent sum of eigenstates — until measurement forces irreversible collapse into a single eigenvalue. The collapse is:
- **Instantaneous** at the measurement event
- **Irreversible** — the pre-measurement state cannot be recovered
- **Non-local** — entangled partners update simultaneously

Modified Schrodinger equation (E6.2): collapse rate depends on Φ (intent/coherence field).

## Domain B — Theology
Divine mysteries follow the same three-phase arc:
1. **Mysterion** (μυστήριον) — hidden in God before revelation (Colossians 1:26)
2. **Apokalypsis** (ἀποκάλυψις) — revelation at the appointed time, "the fullness of time" (Galatians 4:4)
3. **Ephapax** (ἐφάπαξ) — once-for-all, irreversible completion (Hebrews 7:27, 9:12, 10:10)

## Shared Structure
| Feature | QM | Theology |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-event state | Superposition (multiple eigenstates) | Mysterion (hidden, veiled) |
| Triggering event | Measurement / apparatus interaction | Kairos / fullness of time |
| Post-event state | Collapsed eigenstate | Revealed, completed truth |
| Reversibility | Irreversible | Irreversible (ephapax) |
| Propagation | Non-local (entanglement) | Non-local (all nations, all time) |

Pre-measurement multiplicity → apparatus interaction → irreversible state change → non-local propagation.

## Strain Point
- **QM multiplicity is ontological** — the system genuinely exists in multiple states simultaneously.
- **Theological multiplicity may be epistemological** — the mystery is hidden from human knowledge, but God already knows the outcome.

This is the core tension: is the "superposition" of mystery a real ontological state, or merely an epistemic limitation of the creature?

## Swap Test: PASSED
Replacing QM terms with theological terms and vice versa preserves the structural logic of the three-phase arc.

## Falsification Condition
If theological mysteries were **reversible** — that is, if a revealed truth could be re-hidden after revelation, returning to the mysterion state — the mapping breaks. The irreversibility of ephapax is load-bearing for the isomorphism.

## Scripture
- Colossians 1:26 — "the mystery hidden for ages and generations but now revealed"
- Galatians 4:4 — "when the fullness of time had come"
- Hebrews 7:27, 9:12, 10:10 — ephapax (once for all)
