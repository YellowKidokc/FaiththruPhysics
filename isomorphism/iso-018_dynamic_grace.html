Domain A — Cosmology

Standard Cosmology · Modified Friedmann · Dark Energy

The universe is expanding at an accelerating rate. The standard model describes this with a cosmological constant Λ — a static energy density built into the fabric of spacetime. But if dark energy is not static, the equation of state departs from w = −1, and the physics opens a window onto the theology.

Standard Friedmann Equation
H² = (8πG/3)ρ + Λ/3

H = Hubble parameter (expansion rate), ρ = matter/radiation density, Λ = cosmological constant (static). This is the deist equation: Λ is a fixed number, impersonal, set once and forgotten.

Modified Friedmann — Grace Function
H² = (8πG/3)ρ + G(t,Ψ)/3

G(t,Ψ) replaces the static Λ with a time-dependent, potentially consciousness-coupled function. G(t,Ψ) is dynamic — it responds. This is the theist equation.

Dark Energy Budget

Dark energy constitutes approximately 68% of the total energy content of the universe. Matter (dark + baryonic) makes up the remaining ~32%. The dominant component of reality is not matter or radiation — it is the mysterious accelerating term. Whatever dark energy is, it rules the cosmos.

Equation of State

For a cosmological constant: w = −1 exactly. If dark energy is dynamic, then w ≠ −1 and w varies with redshift. The CPL parameterization: w(a) = w₀ + w_a(1 − a). DESI DR2 (2025) finds 4.2σ preference for evolving dark energy — w₀ ≠ −1, w_a ≠ 0.

02

Domain B — Doctrine of Providence

Sustaining Grace · Continuous Creation · Theism vs. Deism

The universe does not persist by inertia. Scripture describes ongoing, moment-by-moment divine sustaining — not a God who wound up the clock and walked away, but one who actively holds creation in existence every instant.

He upholds the universe by the word of his power. Hebrews 1:3
In him we live and move and have our being. Acts 17:28
In him all things hold together. Colossians 1:17
Theism vs. Deism — The Critical Distinction

Deism: God created the universe, established its laws, and then withdrew. The universe runs by those laws without ongoing divine input. God is the clockmaker, not the sustainer.

Theism: God actively sustains creation at every moment. Remove God's sustaining action and the universe does not simply "run down" — it ceases to exist. The difference is not subtle; it is the difference between a God who is absent and one who is present.

The theological claim: if dark energy is Λ — a fixed, passive, impersonal constant — then cosmology supports the deist picture. But if dark energy is a dynamic function G(t,Ψ) that evolves, responds, and is purposeful, cosmology becomes consonant with active sustaining grace. The physics makes the theological distinction testable.

Prediction P14.1

Live Cosmological Test · DESI DR2 · Euclid 2026

Prediction P14.1 — Partial Confirmation

Prediction: w ≠ −1 — the dark energy equation of state is not exactly −1 but varies with cosmic time. This distinguishes dynamic G(t,Ψ) from static Λ and is a necessary condition for the theist cosmological interpretation.

DESI DR2 (April 2025): 4.2σ preference for evolving dark energy. w₀ and w_a both depart from the ΛCDM prediction at >4σ significance when combined with CMB and Type Ia supernovae data. This is the strongest evidence for w ≠ −1 to date.

Euclid Space Telescope (full results expected October 2026): Independent dark energy measurement that will either confirm or challenge DESI. If Euclid corroborates dynamic dark energy, the evidence base for G(t,Ψ) vs. Λ becomes compelling.

Scope of the Prediction

The prediction is necessary but not sufficient. Evolving dark energy (w ≠ −1) is consistent with the theist interpretation but does not prove it. The mapping claims: if theism is true, we should expect w ≠ −1. It does not claim: w ≠ −1 proves theism. The direction of inference matters.

Swap Test · Bidirectional Prediction · Falsification

Swap Test — Passed
Replace "dynamic dark energy" with "sustaining grace" and "w ≠ −1" with "responsive, not mechanical" — the structural relationships hold in both directions. Static Λ maps onto deism with equal precision; both descriptions of the same structural choice between active and passive cosmic sustaining.
Bidirectional Prediction — Passed
Forward (cosmology → theology): dynamic dark energy predicts a responsive, non-mechanical God. Reverse (theology → cosmology): theist God who actively sustains moment-by-moment predicts w ≠ −1. Neither domain borrowed from the other; the prediction was derived from the theological structure independently of DESI.
Caution — Level L2 Honest Assessment
This mapping is currently Level 2 (Structural Analogy). While the structural parallel is genuine, we do not yet have a formal substitution map (Σ: physics variables → theological constructs) or operation-preserving maps in both directions. DESI evidence is consistent with but does not confirm the isomorphism. Awaiting Euclid and formal mathematical development.
Falsification Condition

If dark energy is confirmed as exactly static Λ with zero time variation — that is, if w = −1 exactly, to high precision, with no detected evolution from the CMB epoch to the present — the dynamic grace interpretation loses its physical basis. The mapping would reduce to static sustaining (closer to deism), and the distinctive theist claim of responsive, dynamic grace would not be supported by cosmological data. Euclid and DESI-Y5 are the decisive experiments.

06

Scripture

Sustaining Grace in the Biblical Record

Hebrews 1:3
"He upholds the universe by the word of his power." Active, ongoing, moment-by-moment sustaining — not a past act but a present one. The word is pherōn (present participle, continuous action). The universe is not self-sustaining.
Acts 17:28
"In him we live and move and have our being." Paul in Athens addresses the Epicureans (deists) and Stoics. "Live and move and have our being" is the most direct biblical claim that existence itself — including its dynamics — depends on ongoing divine action.
Colossians 1:17
"In him all things hold together." Synestēken — the universe coheres in him. Remove the sustaining action; the cosmos does not merely drift; it loses coherence. This maps onto what happens if dark energy suddenly vanished: gravitational collapse followed by cosmological death.
Nehemiah 9:6
"You alone are the Lord. You have made heaven, the heaven of heavens, with all their host, the earth and all that is on it, the seas and all that is in them; and you preserve all of them." The word preserve (mḥyh) is active, continuous — not a past creative act but an ongoing one.
