# Pharmacology :: Grace

## DOMAINS

**Domain A — Pharmacology**
The Hill equation describes dose-response relationships: E = E_max * [D]^n / (EC50^n + [D]^n), where E_max is maximum effect, [D] is drug concentration, EC50 is the concentration producing 50% effect, and n (Hill coefficient) describes cooperativity. Agonists activate receptors; competitive antagonists block the same binding site. The therapeutic window defines the range between minimum effective dose and toxic dose. Tolerance develops with chronic exposure.

**Domain B — Theology**
Grace operates through faith (Ephesians 2:8). Sin competes for the same "binding site" — the human will/attention. Community amplifies individual faith ("where two or three are gathered," Matt 18:20). There is a threshold effect in belief. Maximum sanctification (E_max) is the upper bound. The relationship between grace received and spiritual effect is nonlinear.

## THE MAPPING

| # | Pharmacology (Domain A) | Theology (Domain B) | Notes |
|---|------------------------|---------------------|-------|
| 1 | Agonist (drug that activates receptor) | Grace | The active agent that produces the effect |
| 2 | Receptor (binding site) | Faith | The mechanism by which the agent acts |
| 3 | Competitive antagonist | Sin | Competes for the same binding site, blocks the agonist |
| 4 | EC50 (half-maximal effective concentration) | Threshold of belief | The "tipping point" where grace begins to produce visible effect |
| 5 | E_max (maximum possible effect) | Maximum sanctification | Upper bound on the response — glorification as asymptotic limit |
| 6 | Hill coefficient n (cooperativity) | Community faith density | n > 1 = positive cooperativity = community amplification of faith |
| 7 | Tolerance (reduced response over time) | Spiritual complacency (link to ISO-026) | Chronic exposure without engagement reduces responsiveness |
| 8 | Therapeutic window | Balanced Christian life | Between insufficient engagement and destructive zeal |
| 9 | Side effects | Suffering in sanctification | Grace produces the intended effect but also secondary effects |
| 10 | Drug interactions | Competing worldviews | Multiple agents at the same receptor produce complex dynamics |
| 11 | Pharmacokinetics (absorption, distribution, metabolism, excretion) | Grace distribution (preaching, sacraments, prayer, community) | How the agent reaches and is processed by the target |

**Connection to ISO-012**: The Sign Operator is the limiting case of the Hill equation as n → infinity. When n → ∞, the Hill curve becomes a step function: zero effect below EC50, maximum effect above. The Sign Operator S(x) = {-1, 0, +1} is exactly this discontinuous limit.

## TESTS

**Swap Test**: PASSED. Replace "drug" with "grace" and "receptor" with "faith" — the dose-response logic holds. Grace without faith produces no effect (unbound agonist). Faith without grace has nothing to bind (empty receptor). Sin at the same binding site competitively inhibits the effect. Community (n > 1) amplifies the response cooperatively.

**Bidirectional Prediction**: Pharmacology predicts that increasing antagonist concentration should require more agonist for the same effect (rightward shift) → theology confirms that greater sin requires greater grace (Romans 5:20). Theology predicts community amplification → the Hill coefficient n > 1 precisely models positive cooperativity.

**Falsification Condition**: If grace effects showed no dose-response relationship — i.e., if spiritual growth were entirely binary (all-or-nothing with no gradation) or entirely linear (no threshold, no saturation) — the Hill equation mapping would fail. Scripture describes gradual sanctification with threshold effects, consistent with sigmoidal dose-response.

## CLASSIFICATION

**Type**: Structural isomorphism. The Hill equation's mathematical form (sigmoidal, cooperative, competitive) maps onto grace-faith-sin dynamics with quantitative precision. The ISO-012 connection (Sign Operator as n → ∞ limit) strengthens the structural claim.

**Connections**: ISO-012 (Sign Operator as limiting case), ISO-026 (Tolerance), ISO-002 (Grace/Strong Force), ISO-003 (Sin/Entropy).
