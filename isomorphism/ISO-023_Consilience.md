# ISO-023: Consilience ↔ Cosmic Expansion / Logos

## DOMAINS

**Domain A — Philosophy of Science (Whewell)**
Consilience of inductions: when multiple independent lines of evidence converge on the same conclusion, the conclusion is strongly supported. Whewell coined the term to describe how Newtonian gravity unified celestial and terrestrial mechanics. The power of consilience is that independent convergence is unlikely to be coincidental.

**Domain B — Theology**
Two claimed correspondences:
1. "Stretched heavens" (Isaiah 40:22, 44:24, 45:12; Jeremiah 10:12; etc.) — Hebrew verb *natah* applied to the heavens, claimed to anticipate cosmic expansion.
2. Logos (John 1:1) as informational substrate — claimed to anticipate Shannon information theory / the "it from bit" program.

## THE MAPPING

| Scientific Concept | Theological Concept |
|---|---|
| Consilience (convergence of evidence) | Multiple scriptural witnesses pointing to one truth |
| Cosmic expansion (Hubble, DESI) | "Stretched heavens" (*natah*) |
| Information as fundamental (Wheeler, Shannon) | Logos as creative word / structuring principle |

## TESTS

**Swap Test**: FAILED.
- "Stretched heavens" → cosmic expansion: The Hebrew verb *natah* means "to spread out, stretch, extend" — used for pitching a tent (Exodus 33:7), spreading a garment (Ruth 3:9), extending a hand (Exodus 7:19). The cosmological usage is poetic, describing God spreading the sky like a canopy. Reading Hubble expansion into this requires ignoring the semantic range of the word and the literary genre. This is almost certainly retrospective eisegesis.
- Logos → information theory: The overlap is genuine but limited. Logos in John 1 is a person (the pre-incarnate Christ), not a mathematical quantity. Shannon information is substrate-neutral and content-free. The connection requires stripping Logos of its personal and covenantal dimensions.

**Bidirectional Prediction**: Weak. "Stretched heavens" does not predict the Friedmann equations. Shannon entropy does not predict the Incarnation.

**Falsification**: Already partially self-falsified. The "stretched heavens" correspondence fails basic philological scrutiny. The Logos/information overlap survives but only as a loose analogy, not a structural isomorphism.

## CLASSIFICATION

**Rating**: Candidate / Low confidence / Analogy only.

**Honest verdict**: The "stretched heavens" correspondence is almost certainly retrospective eisegesis. The Hebrew verb *natah* means "spread out like a tent" — a common ancient Near Eastern cosmological image, not a prediction of metric expansion. Claiming otherwise damages credibility.

The Logos/information overlap is genuine but limited. It is suggestive, not demonstrative. John's Logos draws on both Hebrew *dabar* (creative word) and Greek philosophical usage — neither of which maps cleanly onto Shannon's mathematical framework.

**Recommendation**: Do not lead with this isomorphism. If used at all, present the Logos/information connection with heavy caveats and drop the "stretched heavens" claim entirely.
