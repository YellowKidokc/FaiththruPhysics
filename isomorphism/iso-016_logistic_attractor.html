01

The Claim

What is being asserted and how it could be falsified
Structural Isomorphism · Dynamical Systems · Level 3 · Bidirectional · Registry Entry

The logistic bifurcation equation dΦ/dt = αΦ(1−Φ)(Φ−Φc) generates a phase space with exactly two stable attractors (Φ=0, Φ=1) and one unstable saddle point (Φ=0.5). No trajectory can remain at the saddle point indefinitely — any perturbation drives the system toward 0 or 1. The binary terminus is not a choice; it is a mathematical consequence of the structure.

This is structurally isomorphic to the Christian eschatological claim: there are exactly two final states (Heaven Φ=1, Hell Φ=0), lukewarmness is an unstable equilibrium that cannot be sustained, and all trajectories ultimately terminate at one of the two stable attractors. The sheep-and-goats separation of Matthew 25 is not a theological preference — it is the only dynamically stable outcome given the phase space structure.

What Is NOT Claimed

This isomorphism does not map the timeline of eschatological events (Revelation chronology, millennial positions, etc.). It maps only the topological structure: two stable terminal states, one unstable middle state, no third attractor. Catholic purgatory is a transient state (not a stable attractor) and does not conflict with the mapping — it is a trajectory between the saddle point and Φ=1, not a third stable basin.

Domain A — Dynamical Systems

Logistic bifurcation dynamics and fixed-point analysis
The logistic equation with a cubic nonlinearity produces the richest binary-attractor structure with the minimum number of parameters. The system evolves on Φ ∈ [0, 1] and the fixed-point analysis is exact. No numerical approximation is required.
Domain A — Logistic Attractor Equation
dΦ/dt = αΦ(1 − Φ)(Φ − Φᴄ) Φ ∈ [0, 1] | α > 0 | Φᴄ = 0.5 (critical threshold)
α sets the speed of convergence to the attractors. Φc = 0.5 is the unstable saddle. The factor Φ(1−Φ) ensures that 0 and 1 are fixed points; the factor (Φ−Φc) creates the bifurcation that makes Φc unstable and the boundary points stable.

Key Property — No Neutral Equilibrium

The saddle point at Φ = 0.5 is mathematically unstable. A system placed exactly at Φ = 0.5 with any perturbation — however small — will be driven away from the saddle. The direction of departure is determined by which basin the perturbation enters. There is no stable neutral state: the phase space admits only binary outcomes.

Domain B — Eschatology

Two final states, no neutral terminus, the unstable middle
When the Son of Man comes in his glory, and all the angels with him, then he will sit on his glorious throne. Before him will be gathered all the nations, and he will separate people one from another as a shepherd separates the sheep from the goats. Matthew 25:31-32 — Binary separation; no third category

The eschatological binary in Scripture is not a feature of one text — it is pervasive. The sheep-and-goats structure (Matt 25), the fire-and-chaff structure (Matt 3:12), the narrow-and-wide gate structure (Matt 7:13-14), and the righteous-and-unrighteous harvest (Rev 14:14-20) all assert exactly two final states with no stable middle ground. Lukewarmness (Rev 3:16) is explicitly treated as a state that cannot be tolerated — it is expelled, not preserved. This is the dynamical saddle point behavior: the middle cannot hold.

Grace as Basin-Shifting

How ISO-013 (Grace Operator) connects to ISO-016 (Eschatological Attractors)
Basin Shift — The Theological Claim

Without external intervention, a trajectory in the Φ < 0.5 basin will inevitably reach Φ = 0 (Hell). This is consistent with ISO-012: self-generated evolution cannot change the eigenvalue. The moral sign is fixed at −1; the trajectory converges to Φ = 0. This is the formal content of "dead in trespasses and sins" (Ephesians 2:1) — not merely morally weak but dynamically bound to the wrong attractor.

The grace operator from ISO-013 acts as a basin-shifting perturbation: it moves the trajectory across the saddle boundary (Φ = 0.5) from the Φ < 0.5 basin to the Φ > 0.5 basin. Once the trajectory crosses the saddle into the Φ > 0.5 basin, it will converge to Φ = 1 by the system's own dynamics. This is precisely the Reformed account of regeneration: grace initiates; the regenerate person then naturally tends toward God (Philippians 2:13, "it is God who works in you, both to will and to work").

The grace operator does not directly push the trajectory to Φ=1 — it merely crosses the saddle boundary. The regenerate person's sanctification (convergence to Φ=1) is driven by the system's own dynamics in the new basin. This is the formal content of "the Holy Spirit works in you" — the convergence is internal, but the basin shift is external.

Separator Tests

Swap test, bidirectional prediction, and falsification condition
Swap Test — PASSED
Replace "stable attractor Φ=1" with "Heaven" and "stable attractor Φ=0" with "Hell" — the dynamical structure holds in both directions. The instability of Φ=0.5 maps onto the instability of lukewarmness (Rev 3:16 says it is actively rejected, not merely unstable — stronger than the math requires). The binary basin structure maps onto the sheep/goats division with no structural remainder.
Bidirectional Prediction
Forward (dynamics → theology): The saddle point's zero-measure stability predicts that truly neutral moral positions cannot be maintained — life events force people toward one attractor or the other. This is a testable sociological claim: studied across time, moral indifference should not be stable but should resolve toward commitment (either for or against religious orientation). Reverse (theology → dynamics): The sheep-goats binary predicts exactly the dynamical phase space with no third stable state — which is independently confirmed by the mathematics of the logistic equation with this parameter structure.
Purgatory Does Not Falsify
Purgatory (in Catholic theology) functions as a transient state, not a stable attractor. It is a trajectory in the Φ > 0.5 basin converging to Φ=1, not a third basin with its own fixed point. The isomorphism accommodates purgatory: it is a region of the Φ=1 basin, not a competing attractor. The claim is about the terminal states — and both Catholic and Protestant eschatology agree that terminal states are binary.
 Falsification Condition

If a genuine third stable eschatological state were demonstrated in either domain:

  • In dynamics: a stable attractor at Φ ≈ 0.5 (impossible in this equation, but if the parameter space changed to permit it)
  • In theology: a scriptural third terminal state — neither heaven nor hell — that is permanent (not transient), non-optional (not a universalist "all end up in heaven"), and stable (not ultimately resolved to one of the two)

Universalism (all end at Φ=1) would also falsify the mapping — it eliminates the Φ=0 attractor entirely. If the Φ=0 attractor has zero measure (if grace is irresistible and universal), the phase space structure changes and the bifurcation loses its eschatological correspondence.

Weakest Link — The Saddle's Stability Timescale

The isomorphism requires that Φ = 0.5 is unstable — i.e., that lukewarmness cannot be sustained indefinitely. But in practice, many people sustain moral indifference for decades without resolving toward either attractor. The dynamical claim would need to be interpreted as operating on an eschatological timescale (the full life and judgment), not a human psychological timescale. The mapping is structurally sound but the timescale for the instability to manifest is theologically specified, not dynamically forced.

Strongest Link — No Stable Third State

The most robust element of ISO-016 is the mathematical impossibility of a stable third attractor in this equation's parameter space. This is not a strong empirical claim — it is exact mathematics. The theological binary (exactly two terminal states) matches the mathematical binary (exactly two stable attractors) at the level of structure, not merely analogy. This is the strongest structural correspondence in the registry at the topological level.
