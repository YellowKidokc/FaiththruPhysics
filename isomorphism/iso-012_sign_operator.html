The Claim

Central isomorphism assertion & falsification target
Structural Isomorphism  ·  ISO-012  ·  Status: Testing  ·  Confidence: High

[σ, U] = 0 is the mathematical content of “salvation by works is impossible.”

Define a Hermitian operator σ with eigenvalues ±1. The system occupies one of two eigenstates: |+1⟩ or |−1⟩. Let U be the unitary time-evolution operator generated by the system’s own Hamiltonian. The commutation relation [σ, U] = 0 means that self-generated time evolution cannot change the eigenvalue. The state vector may rotate, precess, or evolve in complex ways, but the eigenvalue of σ remains fixed under U.

To flip the eigenvalue requires an external operator V where [σ, V] ≠ 0.

In the theological domain: moral orientation is a binary eigenstate. +1 is righteous alignment (oriented toward God); −1 is fallen alignment (oriented away from God). All self-generated moral effort—works, discipline, virtue formation, religious observance—are unitary operations generated by the system’s own internal Hamiltonian. They produce real state evolution (genuine behavioral change) but cannot flip the eigenvalue. Only external grace (Vgrace where [σ, Vgrace] ≠ 0) can accomplish the sign flip.

 Kill Condition

If sustained self-effort could permanently flip moral orientation without external input—that is, if [σ, U] ≠ 0 were demonstrated in either domain—the isomorphism breaks. Temporary behavioral improvement does not falsify it; only a permanent eigenvalue change via Uself alone would.

Equation 1 — Sign Operator Definition
σ is Hermitian: σ† = σ Eigenvalue equations: σ|+1⟩ = +1 · |+1⟩ σ|−1⟩ = −1 · |−1⟩ Spectrum: spec(σ) = { +1, −1 } (binary; no intermediate eigenvalue; no |0⟩ state)
σ is a Hermitian operator on a two-dimensional Hilbert space. Its only eigenvalues are +1 and −1. There is no value between them. Every state is either an eigenstate or a superposition; there is no morally neutral position in the binary reading.
Equation 2 — Self-Evolution and Commutation (Key Constraint)
Uself = exp(−i Hint t / ℏ) (unitary; generated by internal Hamiltonian Hint) [σ, Uself] = 0 ∴ σ · Uself|−1⟩ = Uself · σ|−1⟩ = Uself · (−1)|−1⟩ = −1 · (Uself|−1⟩)
[σ, Uself] = 0 is the key constraint. It proves algebraically that Uself|−1⟩ remains an eigenstate of σ with eigenvalue −1. No amount of internal evolution can change the eigenvalue. σ is a constant of motion under Hint. This is the mathematical proof that works cannot save.
Equation 3 — Theological Translation (Primary Isomorphism Statement)
σmoral(Uself|humanityfallen⟩) = −1 · (Uself|humanityfallen⟩)
The moral sign operator applied to any self-evolved state of fallen humanity still returns eigenvalue −1. A person can become more educated, more disciplined, more religious, more virtuous in behavior—all of these are Uself rotations that change the state’s orientation within the −1 eigenspace. But the eigenvalue itself is invariant under self-operations. This is the mathematical content of Romans 3:10–12.
Equation 4 — External Grace Operator (Sign Flip)
Vgrace|humanityfallen⟩ = |humanityredeemed⟩ where [σmoral, Vgrace] ≠ 0 ∴ σmoral · Vgrace|−1⟩ = +1 · (Vgrace|−1⟩)
Vgrace does not commute with the moral sign operator. This non-commutativity is precisely why it can change the eigenvalue. Vgrace is not generated by Hint—it is external to the system. Theology: “By grace you have been saved through faith… not a result of works” (Eph 2:8–9).
Equation 5 — Constraint Summary
Invariance condition: [σ, Uself] = 0 ⇒ eigenvalue invariant under self-evolution Flip condition: [σ, Vgrace] ≠ 0 ⇒ eigenvalue changes via external action Both conditions must hold simultaneously. If [σ, Uself] ≠ 0, then Vgrace would be redundant. If [σ, Vgrace] = 0, then Vgrace could not flip the sign either.
The two conditions are complementary. The invariance of the eigenvalue under internal evolution is what makes the external operator necessary and meaningful.

The Mapping

Element-by-element structural correspondence, domain A to domain B
Domain A — Mathematics

σ with spectrum {±1} partitions a Hilbert space into two orthogonal eigenspaces. [σ, H] = 0 implies [σ, U] = 0, making σ a constant of motion. Crossing from one eigenspace requires [σ, V] ≠ 0 from an external perturbation. In spin physics: an external field can flip spin; the spin cannot flip itself.

Domain B — Theology

Moral orientation is binary: aligned with God (+1) or opposed (−1). All humans post-Fall are in the −1 eigenstate (Romans 3). Self-generated operations cannot change the sign. Only external Vgrace—grace from outside the fallen system—can accomplish the sign flip. Reformed: total depravity + sola gratia.

Strain Analysis

Honest objections, scope limitations, theological scope
Core Objection

Is moral orientation really binary? Can’t someone be partially good? The ±1 eigenvalues assume no spectrum between them. Theology itself debates this. This is a serious objection that goes to the heart of the mapping.

Catholic Moral Theology
Distinguishes venial sin (weakening grace) from mortal sin (destroying grace). This implies a spectrum of moral states, not a binary. If moral orientation is continuous, a Hermitian operator with spectrum {±1} is the wrong model.
Eastern Orthodox Theology (Theosis)
Theosis as a process of gradual divinization implies a continuous spectrum of moral transformation. The binary eigenvalue structure may be too coarse for gradated participation in divine nature.
Common Moral Experience
People appear partially good and partially bad simultaneously. Reducing moral orientation to a single ±1 variable may oversimplify multidimensional moral reality.
Circular Risk
The mapping assumes Reformed soteriology is correct. Using the isomorphism to argue FOR Reformed soteriology is circular. ISO-012 shows the Reformed position has a clean mathematical structure; it does not prove the position.
The Reformed Response (Which the Mapping Assumes)

The binary is not about moral behavior (continuous, multidimensional—mapped to the state vector’s position within an eigenspace) but about moral status (covenantal, binary—mapped to the eigenvalue itself, fixed under self-operations). The objection confuses the state vector (continuous) with the eigenvalue (discrete). Behavioral improvement = rotation within the −1 eigenspace. Covenantal status = eigenvalue. Both can change independently; only Vgrace changes the eigenvalue.

Where It Holds

Within Reformed theology the mapping is tight. Commuting operators reproduce the content of sola gratia with precision. The prediction that Pelagianism is impossible is sharp and discriminating. The state-vector/eigenvalue distinction resolves the sanctification/regeneration confusion.

Where It Strains

The binary assumption is contested within Christianity (Catholic, Orthodox, Wesleyan traditions). Reducing moral orientation to a single variable σ ignores multidimensional complexity. Structural elegance is not proof of theological truth.

Sanctification vs. Regeneration
The isomorphism provides a formal basis for this critical distinction. Sanctification = state evolution (Uself within +1 eigenspace after regeneration). Regeneration = eigenvalue flip (Vgrace). They are structurally non-equivalent.
Works-Righteousness Is Structurally Impossible
[σ, Uself] = 0 is a mathematical impossibility result. Self-justification is as impossible as generating a non-commuting operator from commuting ones—a structural constraint, not a rule that could in principle be broken by sufficient effort.
Grace as Categorical Discontinuity
Vgrace is not a more powerful version of Uself. It belongs to a different operator class. Grace is not “assistance” but “transformation”— not additive improvement but eigenvalue-level discontinuity.
Perseverance of the Saints
After Vgrace flips the eigenvalue to +1, Uself still commutes with σ. The commutation constraint protects both eigenspaces equally. The redeemed state is as stable under internal dynamics as the fallen state was.
Universal Fallenness
The commutation result applies to every system in the −1 eigenspace. No exception exists by virtue of virtue. The algebra is democratic: “all have sinned.”
Moral Effort Is Still Real
Uself produces real state evolution—genuine growth, virtue, behavioral change. The claim is only that this cannot flip the eigenvalue. Moral effort matters; it just cannot accomplish what only Vgrace can accomplish.
