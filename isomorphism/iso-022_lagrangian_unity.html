The Claim

One action · one God · one unified truth
Classification: Structural Isomorphism — Status: Testing — Confidence: Medium

The Lowe Coherence Lagrangian (LLC) encodes ten coupled variables (G, M, E, S, T, K, R, Q, F, C) within a single action principle, constrained by five symmetry pairs (G↔Q, M↔F, E↔C, K↔R, S↔T) such that no variable evolves independently of the whole. This mathematical structure — a single generating function producing coupled, paired outputs — is isomorphic to the classical-theist doctrine of divine simplicity: God's attributes (justice, mercy, omniscience, omnipotence, love, holiness, truth, faithfulness, goodness, eternity) are not separable parts but relational aspects of a single undivided reality.

The parallel is not metaphorical. In the LLC, unity is not imposed from outside but emerges from the coupling structure itself. In divine simplicity, the unity of attributes is not a theological convenience but an ontological claim about God's non-composite nature. The LLC provides a physical model in which emergent multiplicity from fundamental unity is mathematically tractable — and that is precisely what divine simplicity asserts at the level of ultimate reality.

"All truth is God's truth." Not as a slogan, but as an ontological claim: truth is unified because its source is simple. Distinct truths emerge relationally, not compositionally — just as distinct equations of motion emerge from a single Lagrangian. — Augustine (paraphrased), formalized in ISO-022
 Kill Conditions

This isomorphism is falsified if any of the following obtain:

  • The LLC is shown to be mathematically ill-posed — negative-norm states (ghost modes), unbounded Hamiltonian, or runaway solutions in the variational problem.
  • The 10 variables decouple under renormalization or in some physical regime, breaking the unity claim at the Lagrangian level.
  • The 5 symmetry pairs are shown to be arbitrary postulates that cannot be derived from an underlying symmetry principle (e.g., a gauge symmetry or Noether current).
  • Divine simplicity is shown to be formally incoherent — some analytic philosophers of religion argue the doctrine leads to contradiction; if that argument succeeds formally, the target concept collapses and the isomorphism has no target.

Mathematical Form

Lowe Coherence Lagrangian — action principle and symmetry structure

The Lowe Coherence Lagrangian is a proposed unified action principle governing ten coupled scalar/tensor fields in four-dimensional spacetime. The action principle and symmetry structure below represent the current theoretical form of the LLC as used in the Theophysics Research Program. The LLC has not been independently validated.

LLC Action Principle
S[G,M,E,S,T,K,R,Q,F,C] = ∫ d&sup4;x √(-g) · ℒ(G,M,E,S,T,K,R,Q,F,C)
The action S is stationary (δS = 0) with respect to independent variations in each of the 10 fields. All equations of motion derive from this single variational principle.
Lagrangian Density — Schematic Form
ℒ = ℒkin(G,M,E,K,R) + ℒsym(G↔Q, M↔F, E↔C, K↔R, S↔T) + ℒint(λGQG·Q + λMFM·F + λECE·C + λKRK·R + λSTS·T)
ℒkin contains standard kinetic terms; ℒsym encodes the symmetry pair constraints; ℒint couples each pair via coupling constants λ. The λ constants are the primary free parameters of the LLC.
Symmetry Pair Constraint — General Form
For each pair (φA ↔ φB): φA → φB , φB → φA ℒsym is invariant under this exchange: ℒsym(φA, φB) = ℒsym(φB, φA)
Each pair defines a discrete Z2 symmetry. The 5 pairs together generate a symmetry group Z2&sup5;, constraining the full coupling structure of the LLC.
Euler–Lagrange Equations of Motion — Schematic
For each field φi ∈ {G, M, E, S, T, K, R, Q, F, C}: ∂ℒ/∂φi - ∂μ(∂ℒ/∂(∂μφi)) = 0 Result: 10 coupled PDEs, none of which closes independently. No variable φi is dynamically decoupled from the others.
The coupling structure ensures that the solution space of each equation depends on the boundary conditions and values of all other fields. This is the mathematical statement of "no variable decouples."

Evidence & Verification

Test results, bidirectional predictions, and observational dependencies

ISO-022 depends on two independent chains of validation: the mathematical validity of the LLC as a physical framework, and the coherence of divine simplicity as a theological doctrine. Currently, both chains are partially open. The test results below reflect the state as of the registry date.

Lagrangian Validity
The LLC has not been independently validated. The 10-variable coupling structure may be mathematically ill-posed: ghost modes (negative-norm states), runaway solutions in the variational problem, or an unbounded Hamiltonian could undermine the entire action principle. The symmetry pairs are postulated, not derived from a symmetry principle such as a gauge invariance or a conserved Noether current.
Unverified
Swap Test (Cross-Domain Prediction)
The swap test would require demonstrating that the LLC's coupling structure predicts something about divine attribute relationships that was not already known theologically — or vice versa, that the attribute-pairing structure known from systematic theology constrains the free parameters of the LLC in a way that produces novel physical predictions. Neither direction has been attempted.
Untested
Forward Prediction (Physics → Theology)
If divine simplicity is structurally real, we should expect reality to admit a unified action principle. This prediction is weak: unification is already the explicit goal of physics, so the "confirmation" is not informative. This direction does not distinguish theophysics from naturalistic physics.
Partial / Weak
Reverse Prediction (Theology → Physics)
If the LLC is valid, we should expect divine attributes to exhibit paired symmetry structure consistent with the Z2&sup5; group. This is testable within systematic theology: do the classical attribute pairs (justice/mercy, etc.) satisfy the symmetry interchange property? Initial inspection suggests they do — each paired attribute is the "complement" of the other in a well-defined relational sense. This direction is the stronger test.
Testable — Pending
Observational Anchor (DESI / Euclid)
The LLC's free parameters (λGQ, λMF, λEC, λKR, λST) must ultimately be fit to cosmological observational data. DESI Baryon Acoustic Oscillation data and Euclid weak-lensing surveys are the primary targets. Without this fit, the LLC remains an unfalsifiable formal structure and the isomorphism has no empirical purchase on the physics side.
Pending Data
Circularity Risk

The LLC is the Theophysics framework's own construction. The isomorphism is therefore between a Theophysics artifact and a theological doctrine. This risks circularity: if the LLC was designed with divine simplicity in mind, the structural parallel is not a discovery but a design feature. The mapping gains genuine evidentiary force only if the LLC is validated against independent observational data, specifically data that was not part of the LLC's construction.

Genuine Structural Content

Despite the circularity risk, the structural parallel is not trivial. A single generating function producing exactly ten coupled outputs in five symmetry-paired groups is a specific, non-generic mathematical structure. The fact that classical theology independently arrived at a similar architecture — one source, multiple relational aspects, paired attributes — through an entirely different epistemic route (reason plus revelation, not variational calculus) provides some non-circular support for the isomorphism.

Prior Art — Augustine on Divine Unity

Augustine's formulation "all truth is God's truth" (De Doctrina Christiana) was not merely rhetorical. It expressed the Neoplatonic conviction, absorbed into Christian theology, that the One (the simple, non-composite source of being) is the ultimate ground of all multiplicity. The LLC makes this claim mathematically tractable: multiplicity (10 fields) arises from unity (one action) through structure (5 symmetry pairs). ISO-022 formalizes what Augustine stated as a philosophical conviction.

Prior Art — Aquinas on Divine Simplicity

Thomas Aquinas's treatment of divine simplicity in the Summa Theologiae (I, Q. 3) argues that God has no composition: no matter and form, no essence and existence, no genus and difference, no subject and accident. All attribute distinctions are distinctions of reason (ratione tantum), not real distinctions in God. The LLC's non-decoupling constraint is the physical analogue: all field distinctions are distinctions within a single Lagrangian structure, not independently existing fields that happen to interact.

"God is not a collection of attributes; He is the fullness of being from which all attributes are distinguished by creatures' finite intellects, not by any real division in Him." — Thomas Aquinas, Summa Theologiae I, Q.3, Art. 7 (paraphrased)

If ISO-022 Is Confirmed

Downstream Implications — Confirmed State
  • Physical cosmology would have a validated unified action principle whose structure mirrors one of classical theism's core metaphysical claims — providing a non-circular formal argument for divine simplicity from natural philosophy.
  • The symmetry pair structure (G↔Q, M↔F, E↔C, K↔R, S↔T) would become a physical analogue for the paired-attribute structure of systematic theology, enabling quantitative theological reasoning about relational weights between attributes.

  • Augustine's "all truth is God's truth" would be upgraded from a philosophical conviction to a formal isomorphism grounded in observationally validated physics.

If ISO-022 Is Withdrawn

Downstream Implications — Withdrawn State
  • If the LLC is mathematically ill-posed (ghost modes, etc.), the entire physical side of ISO-022 collapses. This would not falsify divine simplicity — it would only mean the LLC is the wrong physical model for it.
  • If divine simplicity is shown to be formally incoherent, ISO-022 loses its theological target. The LLC would survive as a physical theory but the isomorphism would have no anchor.
  • Withdrawal would motivate a search for a revised LLC or an alternative physical model for divine simplicity — the isomorphism project would continue, but with a different physics substrate.
Connection to the Analytic Philosophy Debate

Some analytic philosophers of religion (notably Alvin Plantinga and others) have argued that divine simplicity, taken strictly, leads to counter-intuitive or formally problematic conclusions: if God's omniscience is identical to God's omnipotence, then omniscience has the same object as omnipotence, which seems absurd. ISO-022 proposes a response: in the LLC, the 10 fields are not identical to each other (that would collapse the theory), but they are non-independently existing aspects of one Lagrangian. The analogy suggests divine attributes need not be numerically identical to each other — they need only be non-independently subsisting aspects of one divine reality. This is closer to Aquinas's "virtual distinction" than to numerical identity. ISO-022 thus has implications for the intra-theological debate, not only for the physics-theology interface.

The structural parallel between the LLC and divine simplicity is genuine and non-trivial, but it gains explanatory and evidential force only when the physics side is independently confirmed. Strongest Link

The structural parallel is mathematically non-trivial. A single action principle producing exactly ten coupled equations of motion, organized in five symmetry-pair groups, with no variable dynamically decoupled from the whole, is a specific and non-generic mathematical architecture. The fact that classical Christian theology arrived at an analogous architecture (one God, ten attributes, paired attributes, holistic non-separability) through a completely independent epistemic route — thousands of years before Lagrangian mechanics existed — provides some non-circular evidential weight for the isomorphism. The parallel is not vague; it is specific enough to generate testable predictions in both directions.

Path to Confirmation

ISO-022 requires three advances to move from Testing to Confirmed: (1) mathematical well-posedness proof for the LLC — demonstrating no ghost modes, bounded Hamiltonian, and well-posed Cauchy problem; (2) derivation of the 5 symmetry pairs from a parent gauge symmetry or Noether principle, removing their postulated status; and (3) successful fit of the 5 coupling constants λij to DESI and Euclid data, generating testable cosmological predictions. Achieving all three would constitute the strongest confirmation of any structural isomorphism in the current registry.
