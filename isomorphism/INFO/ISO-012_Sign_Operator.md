# ISO-012: Sign Operator → Moral Orientation

## Domain A — Mathematics
Define a Hermitian operator σ with eigenvalues ±1. The system occupies one of two eigenstates: |+1⟩ or |−1⟩.

Let U be the unitary time-evolution operator generated by the system's own Hamiltonian. The commutation relation:

**[σ, U] = 0**

means that self-generated time evolution **cannot** change the eigenvalue. The state vector may rotate, precess, or evolve in complex ways, but the eigenvalue of σ remains fixed under U.

To flip the eigenvalue requires an **external** operator V where **[σ, V] ≠ 0**.

## Domain B — Theology
Moral orientation as a binary eigenstate:
- **+1** = righteous alignment (oriented toward God)
- **−1** = fallen alignment (oriented away from God)

Key texts:
- Romans 3:10-12 — "None is righteous, no, not one... no one does good, not even one."
- Matthew 12:30 — "Whoever is not with me is against me" (binary, no neutral state).
- John 3:3 — "Unless one is born again he cannot see the kingdom of God" (external transformation required).

## Shared Structure
| Feature | Math | Theology |
|---|---|---|
| Spectrum | Binary: ±1 | Binary: righteous/fallen |
| Self-evolution | [σ, U] = 0 — eigenvalue invariant | Works cannot change moral orientation |
| State dynamics | State rotates but eigenvalue doesn't | Behavior improves but nature doesn't flip |
| Flip mechanism | External operator V with [σ, V] ≠ 0 | External grace (regeneration) |

## Key Claim
**[σ, U] = 0 is the mathematical content of "salvation by works is impossible."**

Formally:

σ_moral(U_self|humanity_fallen⟩) = −(U_self|humanity_fallen⟩)

The moral sign remains −1 regardless of self-operation. The state may evolve — moral effort produces real behavioral change — but the eigenvalue of σ_moral is invariant under U_self.

To flip the eigenvalue:

V_grace|humanity_fallen⟩ = |humanity_redeemed⟩

where **[σ_moral, V_grace] ≠ 0**. The grace operator does not commute with the moral sign operator, which is precisely why it can change the eigenvalue.

## Swap Test: PASSED
The mathematical structure (binary spectrum, commutation invariance, external perturbation requirement) maps cleanly onto the theological structure in both directions.

## Falsification Condition
If sustained self-effort could **permanently flip** moral orientation without any external input — that is, if [σ, U] ≠ 0 were demonstrated in either domain — the isomorphism breaks. Any system where internal dynamics alone can cross the eigenvalue boundary falsifies the mapping.
