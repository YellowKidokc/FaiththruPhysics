# ISO-018: Cosmological Grace — Dynamic Dark Energy as Sustaining Grace

## Domain A — Cosmology
The standard Friedmann equation with cosmological constant Λ:

H² = (8πG/3)ρ + Λ/3

is modified by replacing the static Λ with a dynamic Grace Function G(t, Ψ):

**H² = (8πG/3)ρ + G(t, Ψ)/3**

where G(t, Ψ) is a time-dependent (and potentially consciousness-coupled) function that drives cosmic acceleration.

Key features:
- Dark energy constitutes ~68% of the universe's energy budget
- If G(t, Ψ) ≠ Λ (constant), the equation of state parameter w ≠ −1
- **Prediction P14.1**: w ≠ −1 — the dark energy equation of state varies with time

This is consistent with DESI DR2 results (4.2σ evidence for evolving dark energy).

## Domain B — Theology
- "He upholds the universe by the word of his power" (Hebrews 1:3) — active, ongoing sustaining
- "In him we live and move and have our being" (Acts 17:28) — existence itself depends on continuous divine action
- Dark energy (~68% of the universe) reinterpreted as **dynamic sustaining grace** — the dominant component of reality is not matter or radiation but the active sustaining action of God

The theological claim: the universe does not persist by inertia. It is actively held in existence moment by moment. This sustaining action is not static but dynamic — responsive and purposeful.

## Prediction
**P14.1**: If dark energy is truly dynamic (w ≠ −1), this is consistent with the grace-function interpretation. DESI DR2 shows 4.2σ evidence in this direction. Euclid (October 2026) will be a decisive test.

## Swap Test: PASSED
The structural mapping between dynamic sustaining and time-varying dark energy holds bidirectionally. A static Λ maps onto deism; a dynamic G(t, Ψ) maps onto theism. The physics makes a testable prediction that distinguishes the two.

## Falsification Condition
If dark energy is confirmed as **exactly static Λ with zero time variation** — that is, if w = −1 exactly, with no evolution — the dynamic grace interpretation loses its physical basis. The mapping would reduce to a static sustaining (closer to deism), and the distinctive theistic claim of responsive, dynamic grace would not be supported by cosmological data.
