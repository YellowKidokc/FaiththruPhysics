# ISO-000: The 24-Property Structural Identity

## Mathematical Truth ↔ God ↔ Moral Truth

**Classification:** FOUNDATIONAL META-ISOMORPHISM — all other ISOs derive from this one.

**Claim:** Mathematical truth, moral truth, and the classical attributes of God share 24 independently verifiable ontological properties with zero exceptions. This is not analogy. It is structural identity, testable by finding a single property that belongs to one domain but not the others.

**Kill condition:** Find one property that genuinely belongs to mathematical truth but NOT moral truth (or NOT a divine attribute), or vice versa, that is not reducible to measurement domain. One genuine ontological mismatch kills the identity claim.

## Swap Test

**If you swap column headers (relabel "Mathematical Truth" as "Divine Attribute" and vice versa), does the table still read correctly?**

YES. Every row survives the swap. This is the definition of structural identity — the labels are interchangeable because the underlying structure is the same.

**If you remove one column entirely, do the remaining two still hold?**

YES. Math ↔ God holds without the moral column. Math ↔ Morality holds without the God column. God ↔ Morality holds without the math column. Each pair is independently valid. The three-way convergence is stronger than any pair alone.

## Compression Argument (Kolmogorov)

- **God explanation:** 1 entity with these 24 properties. Compression ratio 24:1.
- **Alternative explanation:** 24 separate, unrelated coincidences across three independently derived domains. Compression ratio 1:1.
- **By Kolmogorov complexity:** the explanation with shorter description length is more likely correct.
- **Three moves to defeat this:** (1) disqualify the method (show Kolmogorov doesn't apply to ontological claims), (2) defeat the data (find a property that doesn't match), (3) produce better compression (alternative candidate matching all 24).

Nobody has made any of these three moves.
