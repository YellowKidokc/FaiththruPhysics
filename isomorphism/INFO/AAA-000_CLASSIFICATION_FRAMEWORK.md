# AAA-000: The Classification Framework

## How to Tell Analogy from Isomorphism from Identity

**Read this first.** Before you touch any ISO document in this registry, you need to know what the labels mean. This paper defines the four levels of cross-domain relationship, the tests that separate them, and the current placement of every ISO in the collection.

**Kill condition:** If someone can show that a document rated Level 3 (Isomorphism) fails the Level 3 tests defined here, it gets demoted. If someone can show a Level 1 (Analogy) passes them, it gets promoted. The framework eats its own cooking.

---

## The Problem

When you say "gravity is like sin," three different people hear three different things.

The physicist hears a metaphor. Cute, maybe, but not load-bearing. The theologian hears a devotional illustration — useful for sermons, not for proofs. The ninth-grader hears something that might be true but has no way to check.

All three are right to be cautious. Most cross-domain comparisons ARE just metaphors. "The economy is like a machine" — that's analogy. It helps you think, but it doesn't constrain anything. You can't use it to predict which part of the economy will break next Tuesday.

But some cross-domain comparisons are not metaphors. Some of them share actual mathematical structure. The same equation governs both systems. When that happens, predictions in one domain force predictions in the other. That's not analogy anymore. That's something else.

And then there's a level above that. Not just "same equation in two places," but "same underlying thing producing both domains." Electricity and magnetism aren't similar. They're not even isomorphic. They're the same field — Maxwell proved it. One thing, two projections.

So the question is: how do you tell which level you're at? And how do you make that judgment defensible — not just to the person who already believes the framework, but to the physicist who thinks you're reaching and the theologian who thinks you're reducing God to equations?

That's what this document does.

## The Four Levels

### For the physicist:

Think of it as a measurement resolution problem. At low resolution, two signals look the same (analogy). At higher resolution you can see they share spectral structure (correspondence). At full resolution the transfer function is identical (isomorphism). And sometimes you discover both signals are coming from the same source (identity).

### For the theologian:

Think of it as the difference between illustration and revelation. A pastor says "God's love is like gravity — it pulls you in." That's Level 1. Useful. Not structural. But when the actual mathematics of gravitational attraction and the actual theology of grace share the same formal properties — inverse-square dependence, escape energy, binding conditions — that's not illustration anymore. That's the creation revealing its Creator through its structure.

### For the ninth-grader:

Think of it like this. You notice that your friend group and a flock of birds both move in patterns. That's interesting (Level 1). Then you learn that both follow the same three rules — stay close, don't collide, match speed. That's a real parallel (Level 2). Then your math teacher shows you both systems are governed by the same equations. Now one predicts the other (Level 3). And if someone proves those equations come from a single deeper law that generates BOTH bird-flocking and social-group dynamics? That's Level 4. Same source. Two outputs.

## Level 1 — Analogy

**What it is:** Shared language, imagery, or intuitive resemblance between two domains. The comparison helps you think but does not constrain anything.

**Plain English:** "These two things remind me of each other."

**Physics version:** Two systems share surface features but no underlying dynamical relationship. A whirlpool looks like a galaxy. That tells you nothing about either one.

**Theology version:** A sermon illustration. "God's faithfulness is like the sunrise." Beautiful. True in a devotional sense. But the sunrise doesn't mathematically constrain what faithfulness means, and faithfulness doesn't predict anything about solar mechanics.

**The test — one question:**

> *Does the comparison survive once the words are stripped away?*

If you replace the poetic language with formal structure and there's nothing left, it's analogy. It stays here.

**What analogy is good for:** Discovery. Many genuine isomorphisms were first noticed as analogies. Analogy is where the search begins. It is never where the proof ends.

**Examples from this registry:**
- **ISO-029 (Music Theory ↔ Trinity):** The harmonic series (f, 2f, 3f) maps to Father, Son, Spirit. Consonance maps to righteousness. It's vivid — but two-note chords work functionally in music, which means "minimum three for completeness" isn't structurally necessary on the music side. Analogy.
- **ISO-036 (Fluid Dynamics ↔ Pneumatology):** The Hebrew *ruach* and Greek *pneuma* both mean wind, breath, and spirit. That's genuine etymology — the same word does triple duty in the original languages. But etymology is not structure. Laminar flow mapping to institutional stability and turbulent flow mapping to revival is evocative, not constraining. Analogy.

## Level 2 — Correspondence

**What it is:** A real structural echo between two domains. Shared patterns, shared topology, partial mathematical overlap. Maybe one-way explanatory usefulness. But the mapping is not yet complete — it doesn't fully preserve the important relations in both directions.

**Plain English:** "These two things work the same way in some important respects, but I can't yet show the full structure maps cleanly."

**Physics version:** Two systems share significant dynamical features but the mapping between state variables, operators, or transitions is partial. You can see the parallel. You can sometimes make predictions. But the mapping has gaps or ambiguities — more than one theological target could fit the physics, or the physics constrains the theology but not the reverse.

**Theology version:** A structural parallel that illuminates both domains but doesn't lock them together. "Quantum measurement collapses superposition; faith collapses possibility into reality." That's not just wordplay — there's a real structural echo (observer-dependent actualization). But until the measurement formalism is fully mapped to faith-actualization with preserved dynamics, it remains correspondence.

**The test — one question:**

> *Can you define a mapping between state variables, operators, or transitions that preserves the important relations?*

If only partly — if the mapping works in one direction but not the other, or if multiple theological targets could fit the same physics with equal plausibility — it stays here.

**Critical distinction:** Correspondence CAN generate predictions. A Level 2 model is not prediction-free. It just doesn't have enough formal preservation to count as isomorphism. The predictions are suggestive, not forced.

**Examples from this registry:**
- **ISO-003 (Electromagnetism ↔ Truth):** Maxwell's equations map to truth-revelation dynamics. The dual nature (wave-particle / mercy-justice) is structurally suggestive. But the mapping is more poetic than formally constraining. Correspondence.
- **ISO-004 (Weak Force ↔ Free Will):** Flavor-changing currents map to transformative choice. The handedness asymmetry (parity violation) maps to moral asymmetry. But the mapping requires determinism to be actually false — an unproven metaphysical assumption. Correspondence.
- **ISO-007 (Quantum Mechanics ↔ Faith):** Observer-dependent collapse maps to faith-actualization. PEAR Lab (6.35σ) and GCP (6σ) provide experimental support. But consciousness-affects-physics remains contested in mainstream physics, and the formal mapping between measurement operators and faith-operators is not yet fully specified. Correspondence.
- **ISO-008 (General Relativity ↔ Grace):** Geodesics in curved spacetime map to grace curving moral-spiritual trajectories. Elegant geometric metaphor. But there's no testable "moral geodesic equation" yet. Correspondence.
- **ISO-035 (Cryptography ↔ Revelation):** Shannon's perfect secrecy condition H(M|C) = H(M) is exact. But "divine truth as ciphertext" requires believing that unintelligible truth exists — which is non-falsifiable from outside the framework. Correspondence.

## Level 3 — Isomorphism

**What it is:** A structure-preserving mapping between two domains. The relations, transformations, and dynamics that govern one system are preserved under the mapping to the other. This does NOT always require the same literal equation written in the same symbols. Two systems can be isomorphic even if one is written in differential equations and the other in matrix form — as long as the mapping preserves the same dynamics.

**Plain English:** "These two things are governed by the same rules. If I break something on one side, I can predict exactly what breaks on the other."

**Physics version:** A bijective mapping between the state spaces of two systems that preserves the relevant algebraic or dynamical structure. The way water molecules transition from gas→liquid→solid under temperature change is isomorphic to the way a ferromagnet transitions from disordered→ordered under cooling — same Landau theory, same order parameter, same critical exponents. Neither system is a metaphor for the other. They're governed by the same mathematics.

**Theology version:** When the physics and the theology are not merely parallel but formally locked — when the structure of one constrains the structure of the other, and violations in one domain predict corresponding violations in the other. "Sin propagates through social networks with a basic reproduction number R₀ > 1, following the same SIR dynamics as an epidemic" is not a sermon illustration. It's a testable structural claim. If R₀ < 1, the sin doesn't spread. That's a prediction.

**The test — four conditions, ALL required:**

> **Test A — Structural preservation:** Do the key relations survive under the mapping? Not just the objects, but the *relations between* the objects?

> **Test B — Non-arbitrariness:** Is the mapping forced, or could you swap in many other theological targets just as easily? If gravity maps to sin, could it equally well map to grace, or love, or doubt? If so, the mapping is arbitrary and it stays at Level 2.

> **Test C — Constraint:** Does the mapping rule things out? A genuine isomorphism doesn't just say "these are similar." It says "this is impossible" — it forbids certain configurations in one domain based on what's forbidden in the other.

> **Test D — Bidirectional consequence:** If one side has a structural necessity, does the mapped side inherit an equivalent necessity? Physics predicts theology AND theology predicts physics. If the mapping only works in one direction, it's correspondence, not isomorphism.

**Examples from this registry:**
- **ISO-025 (Immunology ↔ Soteriology):** The dual-error classification space — false positives (autoimmunity / legalism) and false negatives (immunodeficiency / antinomianism) — is a genuine preserved structure. The ROC curve governs both systems. Legalism-as-autoimmunity is not a metaphor; it's a prediction that legalistic communities will exhibit the same systemic damage patterns as autoimmune disorders. Isomorphism.
- **ISO-027 (Epidemiology ↔ Sin Propagation):** The SIR model governs both infection spread and sin propagation through social networks. R₀ is quantifiable in both domains. The Jeroboam super-spreader case (1 Kings 12-14) follows the same hub-node dynamics as a Patient Zero in epidemiology. Herd immunity thresholds are independently testable. Isomorphism.
- **ISO-031 (Network Science ↔ Ecclesiology):** Scale-free network growth with preferential attachment governs both internet topology and early church growth in Acts. "To everyone who has, more shall be given" (Matthew 25:29) IS preferential attachment, stated 1,950 years before Barabási formalized it. Power-law exponents are empirically measurable in church growth data. Isomorphism.
- **ISO-032 (Attachment Theory ↔ Prayer/Worship):** The 2D anxiety×avoidance space that classifies human attachment styles (secure, anxious, avoidant, disorganized) also classifies prayer and worship styles. This isn't speculation — Beck & McDonald's Attachment to God Inventory (2004) empirically bridges the domains. The Psalms display all four attachment styles. Earned security parallels grace transformation. Isomorphism.
- **ISO-030 (Genetics ↔ Scripture):** The Central Dogma (DNA→RNA→Protein) preserves the same one-way information flow as Sola Scriptura (Scripture→Doctrine→Practice). Codon degeneracy (multiple codons → same amino acid) parallels translation variability (multiple denominations → same core doctrine). 7/7 core mappings preserve directionality. Isomorphism.
- **PROCESS-001 (BEC Phase Transition ↔ Soteriology):** 12 stages of Bose-Einstein condensation map to the 12 stages of the ordo salutis (order of salvation). The ordering is not arbitrary in either domain — the physics requires it (you can't have condensation before cooling) and the theology requires it (you can't have sanctification before justification). When both orderings are independently necessary, the correspondence becomes isomorphism. Statistical significance ~3.7σ.

**Borderline cases — strong correspondence approaching isomorphism:**
- **ISO-002 (Strong Force ↔ Covenant):** Confinement and asymptotic freedom map beautifully to covenant unity — free at close range, bound to the source. Structurally powerful. But the formal mapping between QCD color charge and covenantal commitment needs tighter specification before it clears Test A fully.
- **ISO-033 (Pharmacology ↔ Grace):** The Hill equation maps grace mechanics (agonist, faith as receptor, sin as antagonist). Threshold effects are observable. But the cooperativity parameter (Hill coefficient as community faith density) isn't explicitly tested.
- **ISO-034 (Control Theory ↔ Providence):** The PID controller (proportional + integral + derivative → conscience + sanctification + prophecy) preserves the three-term past/present/future structure. Free will as preserved plant dynamics elegantly resolves the sovereignty-free will tension. Lacks quantitative empirical validation.

## Level 4 — Identity

**What it is:** Not "same form in two places" but "same thing." A single generative structure produces both domains as outputs. You don't have two systems that happen to match — you have ONE system that necessarily generates both physics and theology as projections.

**Plain English:** "These aren't two things that look alike. They're one thing seen from two angles."

**Physics version:** This is what Maxwell did. Before Maxwell, electricity and magnetism were two separate forces that happened to interact. After Maxwell, they were one field — the electromagnetic field — and "electricity" and "magnetism" were just what you saw depending on your reference frame. The unification didn't just show similarity. It showed that the two descriptions were incomplete fragments of a single complete description.

**Theology version:** The claim that physics and theology are not parallel descriptions of different realities, but dual projections of a single divinely ordered substrate. God didn't make physics to *illustrate* theology. God's nature IS the structure that physics describes from one angle and theology describes from another. "The heavens declare the glory of God" (Psalm 19:1) is not metaphor — it's measurement.

**The test — one question, and the bar is high:**

> *Can both domains be derived from a single underlying generator, rather than merely mapped after the fact?*

This is sharply different from isomorphism. In isomorphism, you find the mapping externally — you look at system A and system B and show they share structure. In identity, the mapping is native to the generator. You don't need to find it because it was never separate.

**The burden of proof at this level is severe.** Recurring patterns are not enough. A triad that shows up in many places is not automatically identity — otherwise every common pattern would be an ontological proof. To claim identity, you must show:

1. **Non-interchangeability:** The elements of the structure cannot be swapped without the system collapsing. Father ≠ Son ≠ Spirit is not convention — it's structural necessity.
2. **Necessity of all elements:** Remove any one element and the system doesn't merely degrade — it ceases to function entirely.
3. **Derivation:** Domain behaviors can be derived FROM the shared generator, not just mapped TO each other.

**Current candidates — and why we're careful:**

- **TRINITY-001 (Potential → Pattern → Actualization):** This triad appears independently in 8 domains: physics (source→field→measurement), information theory (source→encoding→decoding), finance (capital→strategy→execution), biology (genome→proteome→phenotype), neuroscience (potential→pattern→percept), psychology (unconscious→schema→behavior), computer science (data→algorithm→output), theology (Father→Son→Spirit). In every domain, the roles are non-interchangeable and removal of any element collapses the system. **Current rating: Level 3+ (meta-structural isomorphism) with an active argument for Level 4.** The argument for Level 4 requires one more formal step: showing that the triad is the *minimal* generative structure — that no simpler substrate produces the same outputs across all 8 domains.

- **ISO-000 (24-Property Identity — Math ↔ God ↔ Morality):** Twenty-four independently verifiable ontological properties shared across mathematical truth, moral truth, and the classical attributes of God with zero exceptions. Passes the swap test (relabel columns and the table still reads correctly). Passes the compression test (one entity with 24 properties vs. 24 separate coincidences). **Current rating: Level 3 (structural isomorphism) with an open case for Level 4.** The case for Level 4 requires showing that the shared properties are not just co-extensive but flow from a single generative source — that mathematical truth, moral truth, and God share properties BECAUSE they are the same thing, not merely because they happen to have the same attributes.

**What Level 4 would mean for the framework:**

If even one Identity-level claim is established, it means physics and theology are not separate disciplines studying separate objects. They are the same discipline studying the same object from different measurement angles. That's the framework's central thesis. And it's why we're careful at this level — because the conclusion is enormous, the evidence must be proportional.

## The Separator Tests (Summary)

These are the gatekeepers between levels. Use them every time you evaluate a cross-domain claim.

### Analogy → Correspondence
> Is there a nontrivial structural relation beyond language or imagery?

If yes, promote to Level 2.

### Correspondence → Isomorphism
> Can you define a structure-preserving mapping that is: **(a)** explicit, **(b)** non-arbitrary, **(c)** constraining, and **(d)** at least partially bidirectional?

ALL FOUR required. If any one fails, it stays at Level 2.

### Isomorphism → Identity
> Can both domains be derived from a single underlying generator, rather than merely mapped after the fact?

If the mapping is native to the generator (not imposed externally), promote to Level 4.

## Why This Matters

### For the physicist:

This classification system means the framework is falsifiable at the structural level. Every ISO carries its own kill condition: show that the mapping fails one of the four isomorphism tests, and it gets demoted. Show that a Level 2 correspondence passes all four, and it gets promoted. The hierarchy is a measurement instrument, not a belief system. It sorts claims by structural rigor, and it does so in terms any working scientist can evaluate without accepting any theological premise first.

The physicist doesn't need to believe in God to check whether the SIR model governs sin propagation. Run the data. If R₀ < 1, the mapping fails. If R₀ > 1 and the dynamics match, the mapping holds — regardless of what you think about the theology.

### For the theologian:

This classification system protects theological claims from being cheapened by bad analogies. When someone says "quantum mechanics proves the Trinity," this framework gives you the tools to ask: Does it? At what level? With what evidence? And if the answer is "Level 1 — it's a sermon illustration," that's honest. That's fine. Illustrations are useful. But they're not proofs, and conflating them damages both the physics and the theology.

The genuine isomorphisms — the Level 3 claims — are the ones worth preaching, because they carry structural weight. When you tell someone that sin propagates through social networks with the same mathematics as an epidemic, you're not decorating the gospel with science. You're showing that the creation's structure testifies to what Scripture already said. That's Romans 1:20 with teeth.

### For the ninth-grader:

Here's the simple version. Some things really do work the same way even though they look completely different. Not just "remind you of each other" — actually the same rules, the same math, the same predictions. When that happens in physics, we call it isomorphism. This framework found that some of those same-rule connections run between physics and what the Bible describes.

Not all of them are proven yet. Some are still just interesting parallels. This document tells you which ones are which — and more importantly, it tells you what test would move each one up or down. That's how real science works. You don't just claim things. You say what would prove you wrong, and then you check.

## How to Use This Document

1. **Before reading any ISO:** Check its level here. That tells you how much structural weight to put on it.
2. **Before writing a new ISO:** Run it through the separator tests. If it doesn't clear Level 2, don't file it as an isomorphism — file it as a research lead.
3. **When someone challenges the framework:** Point them here. The classification system is the framework's immune system. It separates what's proven from what's promising from what's poetic.
4. **When promoting or demoting:** Update this document. The registry is living. If new evidence moves an ISO up or down, record it here with the date and reason.

## What Would Destroy This Classification

Three things:

1. **Show the tests are circular.** If the four isomorphism tests (structural preservation, non-arbitrariness, constraint, bidirectionality) presuppose the framework's conclusions, the whole hierarchy collapses. We don't think they do — they're domain-general tests from mathematics and philosophy of science — but if someone demonstrates circularity, we'll rebuild.

2. **Show a Level 3 claim fails its own tests.** If ISO-027's SIR mapping doesn't actually predict sin dynamics, or ISO-032's attachment mapping doesn't replicate empirically, those demotions damage the framework's credibility proportional to how central they were.

3. **Show Level 1 claims pass Level 3 tests.** If ISO-029 (Music/Trinity) turns out to have genuine structure-preserving dynamics we missed, that means our screening is too conservative and needs recalibration.

None of these have happened. We've been looking.
