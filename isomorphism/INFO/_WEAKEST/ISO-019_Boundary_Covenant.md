# ISO-019: Boundary Conditions → Covenant Constraints

## Domain A — Mathematics
The χ-field equations require 8 boundary conditions (BC1-BC8) to yield a unique, physical solution. These BCs constrain the field to avoid pathologies (ghosts, tachyons, divergences) and to match observational data.

The boundary conditions function as selection criteria: of the infinite family of mathematical solutions to the field equations, only those satisfying all 8 BCs simultaneously are physically admissible.

## Domain B — Theology
Divine covenants impose constraints on the God-creation relationship:
- Covenant structure defines what is and is not possible within the relationship
- The claim: Christianity satisfies all 8 boundary conditions simultaneously, making it the unique 8/8 solution
- Other worldviews are evaluated against the same BC set and found to satisfy fewer conditions

## Mapping
| Mathematics | Theology |
|---|---|
| BCs select unique physical solution | Covenant constraints select unique worldview |
| 8 simultaneous constraints | 8 theological requirements |
| Solutions failing any BC are unphysical | Worldviews failing any constraint are incomplete |
| Unique solution (if exists) | Christianity as unique satisfier |

## Circularity Assessment: ACUTE
This is the most vulnerable isomorphism in the collection.

- **4 of the 8 BCs are independently derived** from physical requirements (ghost-freedom, asymptotic behavior, energy conditions, observational consistency). These stand on their own.
- **3 of the 8 BCs are Christian-presuppositional** — they encode theological commitments that already assume the conclusion. If the BCs are designed so that only Christianity satisfies them, the "uniqueness" result is tautological.
- The remaining BC is ambiguous in origin.

The honest assessment: the boundary-condition framework is useful for organizing theological evaluation, but the **uniqueness claim is circular** to the extent that the BCs themselves encode Christian commitments.

## Swap Test: FAILED
Swapping domains does not produce independent predictions. The BCs on the physics side are legitimate physical constraints. The BCs on the theology side are partly constructed to select Christianity. The mapping is not symmetric.

## Falsification Condition
If **another worldview satisfies all 8 BCs without modification** — that is, without needing to reinterpret or weaken any boundary condition — the uniqueness claim fails. This would require a non-Christian worldview that simultaneously satisfies all 4 independently-derived BCs plus the 3 Christian-presuppositional BCs (or demonstrates that those 3 are derivable from non-Christian premises).

## Recommended Action
Either:
1. **Derive all 8 BCs from physics alone** (removing the circularity), or
2. **Honestly partition** the BCs into "independently derived" and "theologically motivated" categories, and present the uniqueness claim only for the independently-derived subset.
