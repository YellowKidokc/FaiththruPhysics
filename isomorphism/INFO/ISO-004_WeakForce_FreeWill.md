# ISO-004: Weak Force ↔ Free Will (Law 04)
**Shared Structure:** The weak force is the ONLY force that changes particle flavor (type). It transforms one thing into another. Free will is the only mechanism that changes moral orientation — it transforms the agent. Both are irreversible under normal conditions. Both require threshold energy (W/Z boson mass = activation energy of a genuine decision). Both violate parity symmetry — there is a handedness, a preferred direction.
**Falsification:** If moral transformation could occur without choice (pure determinism), the mapping breaks. If the weak force preserved flavor rather than changing it, the mapping breaks.

# ISO-005: Thermodynamics ↔ Judgment (Law 05)
**Shared Structure:** dS/dt ≥ 0 in closed systems. Moral disorder increases without external input. Both are IRREVERSIBLE without energy input from outside the system. Both define an arrow (of time / of moral decay). Both permit local decreases only through coupling to a larger system that absorbs the entropy cost. Grace is the external input that decreases moral entropy — just as a refrigerator decreases local entropy by coupling to the power grid.
**Falsification:** If moral systems self-corrected without external input, the mapping breaks. If entropy decreased spontaneously in closed physical systems, both physics and the mapping break.
