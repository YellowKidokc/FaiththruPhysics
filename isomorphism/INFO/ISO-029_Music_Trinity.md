# Music Theory :: Trinity

## DOMAINS

**Domain A — Music Theory**
The harmonic series generates frequencies f, 2f, 3f, ... from a single vibrating string. Lerdahl's tonal attraction model quantifies how pitches relate hierarchically. Consonance arises from simple frequency ratios; dissonance from complex ones. Three notes (root, octave, fifth) form the minimum structure for harmonic completeness — no two-note combination establishes a full tonal space.

**Domain B — Theology**
The Trinity: Father (source, fundamental), Son (exact imprint of the Father's nature — Hebrews 1:3), Spirit (the "mover" who proceeds and actualizes). Three persons, one God. The Christian claim is that three is the minimum for relational completeness — love requires a lover, a beloved, and the love between them (Augustine, *De Trinitate*).

## THE MAPPING

| # | Music Theory (Domain A) | Theology (Domain B) | Notes |

| 1 | Fundamental frequency f | The Father | Source tone from which all harmonics derive |

**Key Insight**: Three is the minimum number for harmonic completeness in music. Two notes (unison + octave) produce only identity; adding the fifth creates the first genuinely new harmonic relationship. The Trinity is Christianity's claim that three persons are the minimum for relational completeness. This is a structural parallel, not metaphor.

## TESTS

**Swap Test**: PASSED. Replace "Father" with "fundamental" and "Son" with "octave" throughout Trinitarian theology — the structural relationships hold. The Son as "exact imprint" maps precisely to the octave as frequency-doubled identity. The Spirit as "mover" maps to the fifth as the tone that creates harmonic motion.

**Bidirectional Prediction**: Music theory predicts that removing any one of the three destroys completeness → theology independently claims the same (modalism, binitarianism both fail). Theology predicts that the second person must be an "exact image" → the octave is exactly this (2:1 ratio = identical pitch class).

**Falsification Condition**: If two notes were sufficient for harmonic completeness — i.e., if a root and octave alone could generate all tonal relationships without the fifth — the mapping would collapse. This is empirically false: the octave alone produces only identity, not harmonic motion.
