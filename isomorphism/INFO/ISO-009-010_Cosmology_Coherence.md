# ISO-009: Cosmology ↔ Eschatology (Law 09)

## DOMAINS
**Domain A:** Physical Cosmology — expansion dynamics, dark energy, boundary conditions
**Domain B:** Theology — Eschatology (study of last things, prophetic trajectory, teleological history)
**Concept A:** The universe expands from an initial singularity (Big Bang) toward a final state determined by energy content and cosmological constant Λ. Expansion accelerates — driven by dark energy, an invisible repulsive term that overwhelms gravitational attraction at large scales. The fate of the universe (heat death, Big Rip, or asymptotic de Sitter) is fixed by initial conditions plus Λ.
**Concept B:** Biblical history unfolds from creation toward a determined end state (eschaton). The trajectory accelerates — prophetic density increases as history approaches its boundary. The fate of creation is fixed by divine decree (initial conditions) plus the Logos field (Λ_Logos) — the background coherence that determines ultimate outcome.

## THE MAPPING
**Mathematical Form A:** Friedmann equation: H² = (8πG/3)ρ + Λ/3 − k/a²
**Mathematical Form B:** H_history² = (8πG_sp/3)ρ_moral + Λ_Logos/3 − κ/a_epoch²
**Shared Structure:** Both describe expansion from a singularity governed by energy density, a background constant, and curvature. Both have boundary conditions at t = 0 (initial) and t → ∞ (final state). Both feature a constant term (Λ) that dominates at late times, overriding local dynamics.

**CRITICAL STRUCTURAL NOTE — The Λ Inversion:**
Λ_cosmological is *repulsive* — it drives things apart, accelerating expansion.
Λ_Logos is *cohesive* — it draws things toward final coherence.
These are NOT the same role. They are **anti-correlated**: the physical universe disperses while the spiritual trajectory converges. This is the isomorphism's actual structural content — not "same symbol, same role" but *dual behavior under Z₂ reflection*. Physical expansion (dispersion of matter) mirrors spiritual contraction (convergence toward eschaton). The same mathematical form governs both, but the sign of the dominant term flips across the Z₂ boundary.

**What Is NOT Claimed:** Prophecy is not literally cosmology. The eschaton is not a physical heat death. The claim is structural: history has the mathematical form of an expanding universe with fixed boundary conditions, and the background field that determines the final state plays the role of Λ — but with inverted sign in the spiritual domain, producing convergence rather than dispersion.

## TESTS
**Swap Test:** Partial — the expansion-from-singularity structure is common to many dynamical systems. The Λ inversion (repulsive → cohesive across Z₂) is the specific structural content that resists swapping. Without the sign flip, this is generic expansion dynamics.
**Prediction in A:** Λ_cosmological produces accelerating expansion → prophetic pattern density should accelerate as history approaches its boundary. (PROP-COSMOS analysis: 11/11 correlations, 5.7σ — consistent.)
**Prediction in B:** If Λ_Logos determines final state, then eschatological convergence should be detectable as increasing coherence in late-history moral/spiritual indicators — a counter-entropic trend in the spiritual domain simultaneous with entropic expansion in the physical domain.
**Bidirectional:** Weak — cosmology illuminates eschatological structure, but eschatology does not currently generate testable predictions for physical cosmology. The grace-modified Friedmann equation (PRED14.1: H₀ = 73.0 ± 1.0 km/s/Mpc) would strengthen bidirectionality if confirmed by DESI/Euclid.
**Falsification:**
- If prophetic pattern density does NOT accelerate toward eschaton (flat distribution across epochs), the expansion mapping breaks.
- If Λ_cosmological were attractive (universe decelerating at late times), the structural anti-correlation with Λ_Logos collapses — both domains would converge, eliminating the Z₂ inversion that gives this isomorphism its content.
- If DESI/Euclid constrain w(z) evolution to exclude χ-coupling at 3σ, the quantitative bridge (PRED14.1) fails.

## CLASSIFICATION
**Type:** Structural Isomorphism (with Z₂ sign inversion on Λ)
**Confidence:** Medium — the expansion structure is real but the Λ inversion needs formal derivation from the Lagrangian. Currently postulated, not derived.
**Evidence:** PROP-COSMOS (5.7σ, 11/11), DESI 2024 w(z) hints (preliminary, not confirmatory)
**Laws Invoked:** Law 09
**Open Problems:**
1. Derive the Λ sign inversion from the Z₂ symmetry of the Master Equation — show it's necessary, not just consistent.
2. Specify what "prophetic pattern density" means operationally — what metric, what data, what threshold.
3. The bidirectionality is weak. Strengthen or acknowledge as a one-directional analogy until the grace-modified Friedmann equation is independently tested.

# ISO-010: Coherence ↔ Christ (Law 10)

## DOMAINS
**Domain A:** Statistical Mechanics / Complex Systems — self-organization, phase transitions, order parameters, synchronization phenomena
**Domain B:** Christology — Christ as cosmic coherence source, Colossians 1:17 ("in him all things hold together"), Logos theology
**Concept A:** Self-organization in physical systems requires external energy input to overcome entropy. Order emerges at phase transitions when a control parameter crosses a critical threshold. The order parameter jumps discontinuously (first-order) or continuously (second-order). Coherence in the resulting ordered phase is maintained by collective coupling — but the energy driving it must come from outside the system (heat bath, external field, energy gradient).
**Concept B:** Christ is identified as the coherence source for all created reality. Without this source, all things decohere — the Second Law operates unopposed. Conversion is a phase transition: the soul crosses a critical threshold and undergoes a sudden state change from disordered to ordered. Sanctification is the maintenance of the ordered phase under continuous grace input.

## THE MAPPING
**Mathematical Form A:** div(J_coherence) = 0 — coherence current is conserved; σ(T) = 0 for T > T_c, σ(T) > 0 for T < T_c — order parameter activates below critical temperature
**Mathematical Form B:** div(C) = 0 — Christ-coherence is conserved; cannot be created or destroyed within the system, only injected from outside. C activates when faith-temperature drops below critical threshold (surrender > resistance).
**Shared Structure:** Both describe conserved coherence fields that require external sourcing. Both exhibit phase transition behavior — sudden, threshold-driven state changes. Both show that the ordered state is maintained by continuous coupling to the coherence source; severing the coupling produces decoherence (apostasy / thermal disordering).

**The Sign Preservation Theorem connection:** The theorem proves that internal operators cannot flip the moral sign eigenvalue. This is the operator-algebra statement of the same principle: coherence cannot self-generate. The only operator that achieves sign-flip (Grace, Ĝ) is external and anti-commuting. Christ as coherence source is the theological identification of what the math requires — an external, non-unitary, anti-commuting operator that injects order the system cannot produce for itself.

**What Is NOT Claimed:** Christ is not literally an order parameter. Phase transitions are not literally conversions. The claim is structural: the mathematical architecture governing coherence emergence in physical systems is identical to the architecture governing coherence emergence in moral/spiritual systems — both require external sourcing, both exhibit threshold behavior, both produce sudden state changes, both decohere when severed from the source.

## TESTS
**Swap Test:** No — this mapping cannot be replaced with another pairing and preserve coherence. The specific properties (conserved current, external sourcing, threshold activation, decoherence on severance) map uniquely to Christological claims. No other theological category has all four properties simultaneously.
**Prediction in A:** Phase transitions require external energy → conversion experiences should show threshold effects with discontinuous state change, not gradual drift. (Testable via longitudinal psychological/neurological studies of conversion.)
**Prediction in B:** div(C) = 0 → total coherence in a closed system cannot increase without external input. Communities that sever connection to grace source should show measurable coherence decline over time. (Testable via social entropy metrics in post-religious institutional populations.)
**Bidirectional:** Yes — understanding phase transitions illuminates conversion dynamics (why it's sudden, why partial conversions are unstable, why backsliding maps to superheating), and understanding Christological coherence illuminates why physical systems require external energy to self-organize (the Second Law is the physical expression of the same principle).
**Falsification:**
- If coherence can self-generate in a closed system without external input, the entire mapping breaks and the Sign Preservation Theorem fails.
- If div(C) ≠ 0 — coherence is not conserved — the mathematical foundation collapses. Specifically: if a system demonstrates increasing coherence with no identifiable external source, the conserved-current model is wrong.
- If conversion shows purely gradual dynamics with no threshold behavior, the phase transition mapping is analogy, not isomorphism.

**Confidence:** High — strongest of the ten pairs. div(C) = 0 is derivable from the Lagrangian (E19.1), the Sign Preservation Theorem provides the operator-algebra grounding, and the phase transition prediction is testable.
**Evidence:** Sign Preservation Theorem (formal proof), div(C) = 0 (Lagrangian derivation), Colossians 1:17 (systematic theology), phase transition literature (Landau theory, Ising model)
**Laws Invoked:** Law 10, Law 05 (entropy opposition), Law 01 (Grace as external source)
