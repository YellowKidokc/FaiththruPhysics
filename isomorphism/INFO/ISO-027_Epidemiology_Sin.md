# ISO-027: Epidemiology ↔ Sin Propagation — The SIR Model of Hamartiology

## DOMAINS

**Domain A — Epidemiology**
The SIR (Susceptible-Infected-Recovered) model describes disease propagation through a population:
- dS/dt = -βSI (susceptible individuals decrease as they contact infected)
- dI/dt = βSI - γI (infected individuals increase by transmission, decrease by recovery)
- dR/dt = γI (recovered individuals increase as infected recover)
- R₀ = β/γ (basic reproduction number: average secondary infections per case)
- If R₀ > 1, the disease spreads exponentially. If R₀ < 1, it dies out.

**Domain B — Theology (Sin Propagation)**
Scripture describes sin as contagious: "A little leaven leavens the whole lump" (1 Cor 5:6, Gal 5:9). Sin spreads through social networks via contact (influence, imitation, normalization). Jeroboam son of Nebat is the paradigmatic super-spreader: his idolatry infected every subsequent northern king (referenced 21 times in Kings as "the sins of Jeroboam who caused Israel to sin").

## TESTS

**Swap Test**: PASSED. The substitutions generate non-trivial, testable claims:
- "Jeroboam was a super-spreader with R₀ >> 1" — predicts that his influence should show exponential, not linear, propagation through the northern kingdom. The biblical record confirms: every single northern king is described as continuing his sins.
- "Church discipline is quarantine" — predicts that failure to practice discipline should result in infection spread, which Paul explicitly argues (1 Cor 5:6-7: "Don't you know that a little yeast leavens the whole batch?").
- "The righteous remnant functions as herd immunity" — predicts a critical threshold below which societal moral collapse becomes inevitable. Abraham's negotiation for Sodom (Gen 18:22-33) explicitly explores this threshold.

**Bidirectional Prediction**:
- Forward (epidemiology → theology): SIR dynamics predict that sin propagation should follow contact-network topology, not random distribution. This is testable: sin patterns in communities should correlate with social network structure.
- Reverse (theology → epidemiology): The Judges cycle (sin → oppression → cry → deliverance → sin) predicts epidemic oscillation — which is exactly what SIR models produce (damped oscillations in endemic disease).

**Falsification**: If sin propagation followed random rather than contact-network patterns. If moral collapse showed no threshold effects (no "herd immunity" phenomenon). If isolated individuals were equally likely to sin as socially connected ones.

## CLASSIFICATION

**Rating**: Testing / High confidence.

**Strength**: The SIR model provides quantitative structure. R₀ for sin is not merely metaphorical — it can in principle be estimated from historical data (rate of apostasy spread in communities). The super-spreader phenomenon (Jeroboam) and herd immunity phenomenon (righteous remnant / Sodom negotiation) are independently attested in Scripture and independently predicted by epidemiological theory.

**Jeroboam as case study**: The phrase "the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, who caused Israel to sin" appears 21 times in 1-2 Kings. This is the most extensively documented transmission chain in Scripture. The epidemiological framework explains why: Jeroboam institutionalized idolatry (set up golden calves at Dan and Bethel, appointed non-Levitical priests, changed the festival calendar). He did not merely sin — he created infrastructure for transmission. He lowered the barrier to infection (β↑) and removed recovery mechanisms (γ↓), maximizing R₀.
