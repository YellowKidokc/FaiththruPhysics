# ISO-016: Eschatological Attractors

## Domain A — Dynamical Systems
Consider logistic-type dynamics on the variable Φ ∈ [0, 1]:

**dΦ/dt = αΦ(1 − Φ)(Φ − Φ_c)**

where Φ_c = 0.5 is the critical threshold.

This system has three fixed points:
- **Φ = 0** — stable attractor (basin of attraction: Φ < 0.5)
- **Φ = 1** — stable attractor (basin of attraction: Φ > 0.5)
- **Φ = 0.5** — unstable saddle point (any perturbation drives the system toward 0 or 1)

Key properties:
- **No neutral equilibrium**: the saddle is unstable. The system cannot remain at Φ = 0.5 indefinitely.
- **Binary terminus**: all trajectories end at 0 or 1.
- **Basin boundary**: the saddle divides phase space into two basins of attraction.

## Domain B — Theology
Eschatological binary:
- **Φ = 1** — Heaven, full communion with God, complete coherence
- **Φ = 0** — Hell, total separation from God, complete decoherence
- **No neutral terminus** — "He will separate the sheep from the goats" (Matthew 25:31-46). There is no third category.

The instability of the middle state maps onto the theological claim that lukewarmness is unsustainable: "because you are lukewarm, and neither hot nor cold, I will spit you out of my mouth" (Revelation 3:16).

## Shared Structure
| Dynamical Systems | Theology |
|---|---|
| Φ = 1 (stable attractor) | Heaven / glorification |
| Φ = 0 (stable attractor) | Hell / final separation |
| Φ = 0.5 (unstable saddle) | Lukewarmness / indecision |
| Binary basin structure | Sheep and goats — no third option |
| Perturbation drives away from saddle | Life events force toward one attractor or the other |

## Grace as Basin-Shifting
The grace operator from ISO-013 acts as a **basin shift**: it moves the system's trajectory across the saddle boundary from the Φ = 0 basin to the Φ = 1 basin. Without the external operator, a trajectory in the Φ < 0.5 basin will inevitably reach Φ = 0 (consistent with ISO-012: self-evolution cannot change the eigenvalue).

## Swap Test: PASSED
The structural mapping — two stable attractors, one unstable saddle, binary terminus, no neutral equilibrium — holds in both directions. The dynamical systems framework predicts exactly the eschatological structure found in theology.

## Falsification Condition
If a genuine **third stable state** were discovered in either domain — a stable neutral equilibrium in the dynamical system, or a scriptural third eschatological category (neither heaven nor hell) — the mapping breaks. Purgatory (in Catholic theology) functions as a transient state, not a stable attractor, so it does not falsify the mapping.
