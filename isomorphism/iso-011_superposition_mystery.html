The Claim

Process isomorphism — three-phase arc in both domains
Process Isomorphism · ISO-011 · Status: Testing

The three-phase arc of quantum measurement — superposition → irreversible collapse → non-local propagation — maps structurally onto the theological arc of divine mystery — mysterion → apokalypsis → ephapax.

In quantum mechanics, a system exists in a coherent superposition of eigenstates until a measurement event forces instantaneous, irreversible collapse into a single definite eigenvalue. The result then propagates non-locally to all entangled partners simultaneously.

In theology, divine truth is veiled (hidden in God before the appointed time), then revealed at the kairos moment — "the fullness of time" — in a manner described as once-for-all (ephapax), irreversible, and universal in scope across all nations and all time.

The structural parallel is not analogical decoration. It holds across the key invariants: pre-event multiplicity, a singular irreversible triggering event, post-event determinacy, and non-local reach.

 Kill Condition

If theological mysteries were reversible — that is, if a revealed truth could be re-hidden after revelation, returning to the mysterion state — the mapping breaks. The irreversibility of ephapax is load-bearing for this isomorphism. Any credible demonstration that New Testament revelation is provisional, retractable, or subject to re-concealment would falsify ISO-011.

Mathematical Form

Wavefunction formalism & modified collapse equation
Standard Superposition State
|Ψ⟩ = Σi ci |ψi⟩
A quantum system |Ψ⟩ is a coherent sum of basis eigenstates |ψi⟩ with complex probability amplitudes ci. Before measurement, all eigenstates coexist simultaneously — none is more "real" than another.
Born Rule — Measurement Probability
P(ψi) = |ci|²
Upon measurement, the system collapses to eigenstate |ψi⟩ with probability equal to the square of the amplitude. The collapse is instantaneous and irreversible under standard formalism.
Irreversibility Condition — No Uncollapse
|Ψpost⟩ = |ψk⟩ ≠ Σi ci |ψi⟩
Post-measurement, the superposition cannot be reconstructed without full quantum erasure of the measurement record. In macroscopic systems, decoherence makes this effectively impossible — paralleling ephapax.
Modified Schrödinger Equation (E6.2) — Collapse Rate
d|Ψ⟩/dt = -iÅ|Ψ⟩ + Φ(t) · Λ[|Ψ⟩]
Per the POF 2828 framework, the collapse rate is modulated by Φ(t), the intent/coherence field. Λ is the collapse operator. This extends the standard formalism to incorporate purposeful measurement events — relevant to the theological parallel where the kairos event is not random but intentional.
Non-Local Entanglement Propagation
|ΨAB⟩ = (1/√2)(|0⟩A|1⟩B − |1⟩A|0⟩B) → measurement on A ⇒ instantaneous state update on B
Bell-state entanglement: collapse of subsystem A instantaneously determines the state of spatially separated subsystem B. This non-locality maps to the universal, simultaneous scope of apokalypsis across all nations and all time.

Quantum-Mechanical Evidence

 Decoherence and Macroscopic Irreversibility

In macroscopic systems, quantum decoherence causes the superposition to become irreversibly entangled with environmental degrees of freedom. The measurement record is distributed across ~1023 particles. Erasure is thermodynamically impossible. This is not merely a practical limitation but an effective structural irreversibility that maps precisely to ephapax.

 Bell's Theorem and Non-Locality

Experimental violation of Bell inequalities (Aspect 1982; Hensen et al. 2015 loophole-free test) confirms that quantum correlations are genuinely non-local. Collapse of one subsystem instantaneously determines the state of spatially separated partners. This non-locality maps to the universal, boundary-crossing scope of apokalypsis.

 Measurement Timing as Non-Random in Structured Systems

In engineered quantum systems (cavity QED, ion traps), measurement timing is precisely controlled by the experimental apparatus rather than occurring randomly. This supports the kairos mapping: the triggering event is intentionally ordered, not stochastic. The POF 2828 extension (E6.2) formalizes this via the intent/coherence field Φ(t).

Strain Point: Ontological vs. Epistemological

 The Core Tension

This is the genuine strain point of ISO-011. The isomorphism requires careful handling of what "superposition" means in each domain.

Domain A · Quantum Mechanics
Ontological Multiplicity
QM multiplicity is ontological — the system genuinely exists in multiple states simultaneously. This is not a limitation of the observer's knowledge; it is the actual physical reality of the system before measurement. The wavefunction describes a real physical state, not an epistemic probability distribution over hidden variables (Bell's theorem rules out local hidden variable alternatives).
Domain B · Theology
Epistemological Hiddenness?
Theological multiplicity may be epistemological — the mystery is hidden from human knowledge, but God already knows the outcome. The "superposition" of the divine mystery may not be a genuine ontological state of divine reality, but rather the creature's limited perspective on a truth that was always determined in the mind of God (cf. Eph 1:4 — "chosen in him before the creation of the world").
"The question is whether mysterion represents an ontological state of divine indeterminacy, or merely the creature's epistemic limitation relative to a fully-determined divine plan. If the latter, the superposition analogy is an analogy of appearance rather than structure, and ISO-011 must be re-classified from process isomorphism to epistemic analogy." — ISO-011 Analysis, POF 2828 Research Program, 2026-04-04
 Current Resolution

The isomorphism holds at the structural level regardless of how the ontology/epistemology question is resolved. Whether mysterion is ontological or epistemological, the three-phase arc (hidden → triggered disclosure → irreversible once-for-all) is formally identical to the quantum arc (superposition → measurement → irreversible collapse). The strain point affects the depth of the isomorphism, not its structural validity. Status remains Testing pending further philosophical analysis.
