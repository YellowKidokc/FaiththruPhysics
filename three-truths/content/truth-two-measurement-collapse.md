# Truth Two: The Measurement Collapse

## Good and evil aren't opinions. They're measurements.

Physics has a word for when things are aligned, structured, in phase: **coherence**.  
And a word for when that alignment breaks down into noise: **decoherence**.

These aren't metaphors. They're measurable. Shannon quantified it. Boltzmann formalized it. Every lab in the world uses these measurements daily.

## The Inescapable Gradient

The physicist's entropy, the doctor's diagnosis, the ethicist's judgment, and the theologian's discernment are all reading the **same dial**.

This is why:
- **Sociopaths still know right from wrong** (they feel the gradient)
- **Beauty is recognizable across cultures** (same signal, different languages)

## The Coherence Signature (χ)

Every domain maps to a single underlying variable: **coherence (χ)**.

```
χ ∈ [-1, +1]

χ = +1 → Maximum coherence (Good, True, Beautiful, Healthy)
χ = 0  → Mixed state (Ambiguity, Transition)
χ = -1 → Maximum decoherence (Evil, False, Ugly, Diseased)
```

This isn't philosophy. This is **measurement**.

## What This Means

If good and evil are measurements of coherence, then:
1. **Morality is objective** (not cultural preference)
2. **Evil is ontologically real** (not just absence of good)
3. **The ground must be coherent** (or there's no standard to measure against)
