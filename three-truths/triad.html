Why This Works

Most arguments for moral truth start from theology and work backward to logic. This one starts from the one thing every human being on earth already agrees on — 2+2=4 — and shows that everything else is already locked inside it.

No scripture required. No faith required. No philosophy required. Just one question: do you accept basic arithmetic? If yes, you've already conceded the conclusion. The rest is just showing you why.

Line by Line

2+2 can't equal 5. Truth can't produce a lie.
This isn't a claim. It's a logical primitive. The number 2 is distinct from 5. A state of 4 is distinct from a state of 5. Within the informational structure of reality, mathematical operations have defined, non-arbitrary outcomes. Truth here isn't a feeling — it's a measure of correspondence to the actual structure of reality. The statement 2+2≠5 is a direct consequence of reality being real. Basis: Logical necessity. Axiom of distinction. Information theory.
Coherence can't produce decoherence. Same sentence, different instrument.
This is the Data Processing Inequality — proved by Claude Shannon in 1948. Processing can only destroy information, never create it. A high-fidelity source cannot spontaneously generate noise. This isn't a framework axiom waiting to be proved. It's settled physics, 78 years old. Signal cannot produce static. Order cannot generate disorder. The framework just names what information theory already demonstrated. Basis: Shannon (1948), Data Processing Inequality. Universal law of information theory.
Therefore right and wrong aren't opinions. They're arithmetic.
If truth is absolute and informational (Line 1), and coherent sources can't produce incoherent signals (Line 2), then any system's alignment with coherence is an objective, measurable state — not a viewpoint. Deviation from coherence is error, the same way 2+2=5 is error. Not a perspective. Not a cultural preference. A miscalculation. And this isn't just philosophy. Your body already confirms it — HRV rises with gratitude, cortisol spikes with resentment, EEG coherence increases during prayer, immune function collapses with isolation. Every biological instrument that measures human states reports coherence as "good" and decoherence as "bad." The definition follows the data. Basis: Deductive from Lines 1+2. Empirically confirmed across biology, neuroscience, immunology.

Try to Escape

Every attempted exit from this argument fails at the same point: you'd have to deny Line 1. And nobody does.

"Morality is subjective — it varies by culture."
Does 2+2 vary by culture? Coherence and decoherence are measured with instruments, not surveys. HRV doesn't care about your culture. Cortisol doesn't care about your opinions. The biology is universal because the math is universal.
"You can't derive 'ought' from 'is' — that's the naturalistic fallacy."
We're not deriving ought from is. We're showing that what people call "ought" and what physics measures as "coherent" are the same variable read by different instruments. The moral-physical dictionary maps every "good" word to σ=+1 and every "evil" word to σ=−1 across all domains. This isn't deriving values from facts. It's discovering they were never separate.
"This is just a word game — you redefined 'morality' as 'coherence.'"
We didn't define it. We measured it. Gratitude increases heart rate variability. Resentment spikes cortisol. Prayer produces EEG coherence. Social isolation degrades immune function as much as 15 cigarettes a day. Every cell in your body votes "coherence = good" without reading a single moral argument. The label came after the data, not before it.
"Coherence is just order — you're smuggling in value."
Try this: name one thing every human being calls "good" that corresponds to decoherence. Name one thing every culture calls "evil" that corresponds to coherence. You can't. Not because you haven't thought hard enough, but because the mapping is total. The "value" isn't smuggled in. It's already there. We just read it.
