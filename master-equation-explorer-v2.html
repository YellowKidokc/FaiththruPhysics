READ IT IN ENGLISH

Total coherence equals the integrated product of grace, moral force, truth, love, judgment, Logos, relational frame, faith, moral conservation, and the Christ factor — measured continuously across the entire domain.

If any single variable goes to zero, the entire product collapses. Grace times love times truth times zero equals zero. Every variable is load-bearing. Nothing is optional.

WHAT THIS MEANS

The first eight laws are internally symmetric — each one contains both the constructive and destructive case within the same equation. Grace and sin live in the same math. Love and captivity are the same force with the sign flipped. Free will determines which regime you're in.

Law 9 breaks symmetry. Sin is directional — you can't undo it by running the equation backward. But the energy is conserved. It doesn't disappear. That's why atonement requires an external source term.

Law 10 is not dual at all. C is χ. Christ is not one side of a pair — Christ is the coherence itself. Decoherence is what happens in His absence.
