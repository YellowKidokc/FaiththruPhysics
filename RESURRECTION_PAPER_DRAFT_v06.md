## Abstract

We present a formal model of irreversible coupling-architecture modification between two substrates in a system where one substrate is fundamental (high-symmetry, temporally unbounded) and the other is derivative (broken-symmetry, temporally bounded). The model demonstrates that a distributed field in the fundamental substrate can undergo spontaneous symmetry breaking by localizing into the derivative substrate, and that this localization — if sustained for a duration exceeding the vacuum-stabilization threshold — permanently modifies the coupling architecture between substrates without altering the internal physics of either substrate independently. We show that energy conservation holds across the full arc of the transition (distribution → localization → release → re-distribution), that the observable consequences of the coupling modification appear in the inter-substrate coupling domain rather than in the internal physics of either substrate, and that the modification is structurally irreversible by any operator within the system. The model is then shown to exhibit a candidate structural correspondence with a specific sequence of historical-theological events — the Incarnation, Crucifixion, Resurrection, and Pentecost — where the mapping preserves the physics at every stage and the theological identification emerges from constraint satisfaction rather than imposition. We argue that the failure of any single standard physics phenomenon (bistable switching, first-order phase transition) to capture the full sequence is itself a prediction of the model: the sequence is a composition of operations, each individually analogous to known physics at the structural level, whose composition has no current single-phenomenon analog. The paper concludes by identifying the specific falsifiable predictions the model generates and the domain in which those predictions should be tested.

## 2. Mathematical Preliminaries

### 2.1 Spontaneous Symmetry Breaking

We adopt the standard formalism. Consider a scalar field φ with Lagrangian density possessing a continuous symmetry group G. The potential V(φ) admits a set of degenerate minima — the vacuum manifold M = G/H, where H is the residual symmetry group of the chosen vacuum. For the canonical U(1) case:

V(φ) = −μ²|φ|² + λ|φ|⁴

with μ² > 0 and λ > 0. The minimum is not at φ = 0 but at the vacuum expectation value |φ| = v = √(μ²/λ).

Three properties critical for our model:

**Property 1: Permanence in the infinite-volume limit.** The energy cost of transitioning between degenerate vacua is proportional to the spatial volume. In the infinite-volume limit, this cost diverges. The system is locked into its chosen vacuum. Tunneling back requires infinite energy and is structurally forbidden.

**Property 2: The vacuum remembers.** Once symmetry is broken, the new vacuum state persists even after the conditions that caused the break have changed. The break is recorded in the vacuum structure itself.

**Property 3: New physics emerges.** The broken-symmetry vacuum permits physical states that did not exist in the symmetric phase. In the electroweak case, the Higgs mechanism generates mass terms for gauge bosons that were massless in the symmetric phase.

### 2.2 Coupling Architecture

We define coupling architecture as the set of interaction channels between two subsystems, together with the coupling constants, selection rules, and conservation laws governing those channels. Coupling-architecture modification is distinct from substrate modification. Modifying the substrate changes internal physics. Modifying the coupling architecture changes which interactions are permitted between systems without altering the internal physics of either. The modification is visible only to processes that span both substrates.

### 2.3 Vacuum Stabilization and the Locking Threshold

A symmetry-breaking event does not instantaneously produce a stable new vacuum. The new state must persist for a duration sufficient to: (a) overcome quantum tunneling back, (b) propagate through relevant degrees of freedom, (c) dissipate latent energy.

We define the locking threshold τ_lock as the minimum duration required for permanence. For t < τ_lock, the break can decay. For t ≥ τ_lock, the break is irreversible.

**Status of τ_lock:** The locking threshold is treated as a parameter accepted from the theological-historical source data (approximately 33 years), not derived from first principles. Deriving τ_lock from the coupling constants and entropy landscape remains an open problem and constitutes one of the strongest internal consistency tests (see Prediction 3, Section 8).

## 3. The Dual-Substrate Model

### 3.1 The Structural Necessity of a Fundamental Substrate

Three results from mathematical logic constrain any viable model:

- **Gödel's Incompleteness Theorems (1931):** Any consistent formal system complex enough to describe arithmetic contains true statements unprovable within the system.
- **Tarski's Undefinability Theorem (1936):** No sufficiently powerful formal language can define its own truth predicate.
- **Church's Undecidability Theorem (1936):** No general algorithm can determine truth or falsity for every statement in a formal system.

Taken together: internal closure is structurally impossible for any system of sufficient complexity. The system requires a role — a ground of consistency, a source of coherence-sustaining input — that cannot be filled internally. We denote whatever fills this role as Σ_F. This is a functional definition.

### 3.2 Substrate Specification

**Σ_F (Fundamental Substrate):**
- Temporally unbounded (no intrinsic arrow of time)
- High symmetry (full symmetry group G acts without breaking)
- Contains a distributed field Ψ_F
- Energy content: E_F (conserved in absence of coupling)

**Σ_D (Derivative Substrate):**
- Temporally bounded (intrinsic arrow of time)
- Broken symmetry
- Contains localized excitations with finite extent and lifetime
- Energy content: E_D (conserved in absence of coupling)

**Coupling:** Initially C₀ — limited, externally-maintained, fragile interaction channels. Requires continuous maintenance to persist.

### 3.3 Energy Conservation

E_total = E_F + E_D + E_coupling = constant

### 3.4 The Initial Condition: Coupling Severance

C_orig → (severance) → C₀

The severance is a coupling-breaking event: substrates remain intact, channels between them are reduced. Post-severance, coupling C₀ is maintained only by externally-imposed mechanisms — specific locations (temples), specific mediators (prophets, priests), specific protocols (sacrifice, purification). These are fragile, geographically localized, and fail under perturbation.

## 4. The Symmetry-Breaking Sequence

### 4.1 Stage 1: Localization (The Symmetry-Breaking Event)

Ψ_F(distributed) → [SSB] → ψ_D(localized)

Structurally analogous to the Higgs mechanism: a field distributed across the full substrate acquires a non-zero VEV at a specific point. The localized excitation retains its origin in Σ_F — it is Ψ_F expressed in Σ_D coordinates. Full information content compressed, not reduced.

**Theological correspondence:** The Incarnation.

### 4.2 Stage 2: The Locking Period (Vacuum Stabilization)

The localized excitation must persist for t ≥ τ_lock to permanently modify the coupling architecture. During this period: (a) perfect coherence must be maintained — any decoherence collapses the break before it locks; (b) the vacuum state of inter-substrate coupling is being rewritten; (c) the localization is subject to all local physics of Σ_D.

**Theological correspondence:** The 33-year life — the coherence-purity requirement (sinlessness) is a structural necessity, not a moral preference.

### 4.3 Stage 3: Localization Release (Energy Redistribution)

ψ_D(localized) → [release] → Ψ_F(returned)

Energy redistributes from Σ_D back to Σ_F. The thermodynamic cost of maintaining perfect coherence in a high-entropy environment for the full locking period is paid. No operator within Σ_D can provide the energy.

**Theological correspondence:** The Crucifixion.

### 4.4 Stage 4: Vacuum Confirmation (The Irreversible Modification)

C₀ → [irreversible] → C₁

C₁ differs from C₀: coupling channels become distributed, self-sustaining, internally maintained, geographically universal, available without mediator hierarchy. The modification is irreversible — reversing it requires infinite energy in the infinite-volume limit.

**Theological correspondence:** The Resurrection — not a return to pre-Incarnation state, not a new Incarnation, but confirmation that the vacuum has been permanently modified. The field returns to Σ_F with the physical localization retained as permanent property.

### 4.5 Stage 5: Coupling Redistribution

C₁ → [distribution] → C₁ ⊗ Σ_D

Energy previously concentrated in ψ_D redistributes across the full derivative substrate through new coupling channels. Structure of a phase front propagating through a medium.

**Theological correspondence:** Pentecost — energy manifestation (fire), cross-cultural communication (tongues), distributed coupling (Spirit available to all rather than concentrated in prophets).
