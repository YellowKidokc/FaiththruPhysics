The Coherence Factor

The Coherence Factor (chi) measures how well a civilization's parts work together. Families, institutions, trust networks, moral frameworks, and information flows are the parts. When they align and reinforce each other, the society can act as one.

chi = 1.0 means perfect alignment. chi = 0.0 means total fragmentation.

Current U.S. chi: ≈ 0.24
Critical Threshold: 0.35
Status: Threshold Crossed
END:COMPONENT:executive-summary:executive-summary BEGIN:COMPONENT:section:section-opening

In Part 1, we introduced the Coherence Factor (chi).

Now let's open the hood. What exactly are we measuring?

chi is calculated from nine domains. Each domain captures a different part of social health. Together, they tell us whether a civilization is holding together or flying apart.

The Nine Domains

01 Family Structure (F)

Question: Are families forming and staying together?

Indicators
  • Marriage rate per 1,000 adults
  • Divorce rate
  • Percentage of children living with both biological parents
  • Single-parent household rate
  • Birth rate within marriage vs. outside
Why it matters

The family is the smallest unit that builds coherence. When families break apart, every other domain follows.

1900 score0.92
2024 score0.31
02 Social Trust (T)

Question: Do people trust each other and institutions?

Indicators
  • "Can most people be trusted?" survey responses
  • Institutional confidence (government, media, churches, courts)
  • Contract enforcement needs
  • Neighbor-to-neighbor trust
Why it matters

Trust is what makes cooperation easy. Without it, every deal needs verification. Every promise needs enforcement. Working together becomes very hard.

1900 score0.85
2024 score0.22
03 Safety (S)

Question: Are people safe from violence?

Indicators
  • Murder rate per 100,000
  • Violent crime rate
  • Sexual assault rate
  • Domestic violence rate
  • Child abuse rate
Why it matters

Physical safety is the baseline for everything else. When people fear each other, community becomes impossible.

1900 score0.75(higher crime in cities, but lower overall)
2024 score0.45(varies dramatically by location)
04 Self-Control (C)

Question: Can people resist destructive impulses?

Indicators
  • Addiction rates (alcohol, drugs, gambling, pornography)
  • Obesity rate
  • Consumer debt levels
  • Impulse-related bankruptcy
  • STD transmission rates
Why it matters

Self-control is coherence at the personal level. A society full of people who can't control themselves cannot hold any larger order together.

1900 score0.80
2024 score0.28
05 Mental Health (M)

Question: Are people psychologically healthy?

Indicators
  • Suicide rate
  • Depression rates (clinical diagnosis)
  • Anxiety disorder rates
  • "Deaths of despair" (suicide + overdose + alcoholic liver disease)
  • Psychiatric medication usage
Why it matters

Mental health is the individual's coherence with themselves. When masses of people break down psychologically, it signals — and worsens — social breakdown.

1900 score0.82
2024 score0.25
06 Economic Stability (E)

Question: Can people build and maintain wealth?

Indicators
  • Household savings rate
  • Home ownership rate
  • Bankruptcy rate
  • Generational wealth transfer
  • Economic mobility
Why it matters

Economic stability makes long-term planning possible. Long-term planning makes commitment possible. Commitment makes coherence possible.

1900 score0.78
2024 score0.35
07 Civic Participation (P)

Question: Do people engage in community?

Indicators
  • Volunteering rate
  • Membership in civic organizations
  • Voter participation
  • Community event attendance
  • Neighbor interaction frequency
Why it matters

Civic participation is coherence made visible. People choosing to work with strangers toward shared goals — that is a healthy society showing up.

1900 score0.88
2024 score0.22
08 Shared Meaning (Sigma)

Question: Do people share a common framework?

Indicators
  • Religious participation rate
  • Agreement on basic moral questions
  • Shared cultural knowledge
  • Common information sources
  • Consensus on historical narrative
Why it matters

Shared meaning lets people coordinate without arguing about everything first. When it disappears, every decision has to be fought over from scratch.

1900 score0.90
2024 score0.18
09 Intergenerational Transfer (I)

Question: Is wisdom passing to the next generation?

Indicators
  • Children learning parents' trade/profession rate
  • Family business survival across generations
  • Religious/cultural continuity child-to-parent
  • Elder care by family vs. institution
  • Grandparent involvement in child-rearing
Why it matters

A society that can't pass on its wisdom dies in one generation — no matter how wise it was.

1900 score0.92
2024 score0.20

The Coherence Factor Formula

chi is calculated as the weighted average of all nine domains:

Coherence Factor
chi = (F + T + S + C + M + E + P + Sigma + I) / 9

Each domain is normalized to a 0–1 scale using historical benchmarks.

What Jumps Out

Look at those numbers again.

  • Trust: Down 74%
  • Shared Meaning: Down 80%
  • Intergenerational Transfer: Down 78%
  • Civic Participation: Down 75%

These aren't gradual declines. These are collapses.

And here's what's critical: they're all connected.

When shared meaning drops, trust drops. When trust drops, civic participation drops. When civic participation drops, families fragment. When families fragment, intergenerational transfer fails.

It's a cascade. Each domain pulls down the others.

That's why chi isn't just an average—it's a measure of systemic failure.

END:COMPONENT:section:section-what-jumps-out
BEGIN:COMPONENT:section:section-threshold

The Threshold Question

Why do we say chi = 0.35 is the threshold?

Because that's the level below which no civilization in recorded history has self-corrected.

Below 0.35, the feedback loops go in the wrong direction. Decline speeds up. The system can't build enough internal coherence to turn things around.

Above 0.35, self-correction is possible. Below it, only outside help changes the direction.

We crossed that line.

chi = 0.27. Threshold = 0.35. We're below it.

Nine domains. All declining. All connected.

This is the shape of collapse.
