Method

A Human-Led Epistemological Framework with AI as Instrumental Collaborator

David Lowe • Theophysics Institute

This protocol does not constitute AI-driven inquiry.

This methodology is structured around human-epistemic primacy, with AI functioning as an instrumental collaborator that accelerates terrain-mapping without substituting for the researcher's synthetic judgment.

AI is employed here to rapidly map the intellectual landscape across multiple disciplines, surface interpretive contradictions, organize competing theoretical frameworks, and identify domains requiring deeper primary-source investigation. AI is not used to adjudicate truth claims. Incomplete or erroneous outputs are an anticipated feature of this methodology, not a defect.

The purpose of this method is to achieve temporal efficiency without sacrificing epistemological depth — by forcing disagreement, structural tension, and coherence failures to surface early in the inquiry cycle. It is the researcher conducting the research, with greater speed and greater methodological honesty, using AI instrumentally at the boundaries of established knowledge.

"Truth is rarely found where everyone agrees. It is found where disagreement is sharpest and still survivable."

Why This Question Matters

I entered this inquiry with a strong prior belief that the United States is on a trajectory toward systemic economic, social, and institutional collapse. The research question is not abstractly academic; it emerges from a personal sense of obligation to understand and, if possible, respond to observable civilizational trajectories.

I experience a degree of moral urgency regarding these findings that I acknowledge may appear epistemically suspect; I include it here as required by the Biaxiosum disclosure standard. This sense of obligation is the motivating force behind this work.

This is not a commitment to having the answers. It is a starting point for asking the right questions.

Personal Note — David Lowe

The research question driving this project: Why is there a ubiquitous, measurable change in moral decline in America from the early 1900s to present day?

Prior to attempting an answer, one must interrogate the epistemological category of the question itself. This question spans sociology, theology, economics, family structure, institutional trust, and moral philosophy. It cannot be resolved within the scope of a single discipline. It requires a methodology capable of holding that cross-domain complexity without collapsing it into a single explanatory register.

The Five-Page System

Each unit of inquiry within this project is structured around five documents, each performing a distinct and non-redundant cognitive function in the research protocol. The five-document structure must be replicated for each inquiry unit.

01

Inquiry Seed

Question & Orientation

Formalizes the research question and documents the researcher's pre-AI epistemic commitments. This step is methodologically critical: the researcher's uncontaminated prior reasoning constitutes the load-bearing epistemological foundation. Ceding question-framing to AI renders the researcher an auditor of AI outputs rather than the author of an inquiry.

File: Inquiry_Seed_[Question].md

02

AI Prompt Engine

How AI Is Used

Documents the exact prompts, AI models consulted, and raw outputs without preliminary evaluation. At this stage the AI functions as a terrain-mapping instrument: it surfaces competing interpretive frameworks, names relevant scholars and fault lines, and identifies domains requiring deeper investigation. Epistemic adjudication is explicitly deferred.

File: AI_Prompt_Engine_[Question].md

03

Research Workspace

Evidence & Links

The primary evidentiary repository where verified sources accumulate. AI-generated claims are stress-tested against primary literature at this stage; the attrition rate of AI claims upon contact with primary sources is itself methodologically informative, indicating where consensus has been manufactured or where the empirical record is genuinely contested.

File: Research_Workspace_[Question].md

04

Contradictions & Resolution

Sense-Making

This document constitutes the methodological core of the system. Source disagreements, AI-primary literature divergences, and intuition-data conflicts are surfaced and documented here. Premature resolution is proscribed; the researcher must inhabit irresolution long enough to comprehend it structurally rather than selecting confirmatory evidence.

Truth is most often found in the contradiction that refuses to resolve cleanly, not in the consensus that resolves too easily.

File: Contradictions_Resolution_[Question].md

05

Quotes, Domains, & Links

Shared Library

A cumulative reference library that persists across all inquiry units, consolidating attributed quotations, domain taxonomies surfaced during research, and annotated external links. This document grows continuously and constitutes the raw material from which publications are subsequently derived.

File: Quotes_Domains_Links_[Question].md

What AI Does — and Does Not Do

AI Is Used To

  • Map intellectual terrain quickly across multiple disciplines
  • Surface contradictions between competing interpretations
  • Organize competing frameworks side by side
  • Suggest where deeper primary-source study is needed
  • Stress-test early conclusions against counterarguments

AI Is Not Used To

  • Decide what is true
  • Replace primary-source verification
  • Frame the initial question before the researcher does
  • Select which evidence counts as significant
  • Resolve contradictions that require human judgment

Incomplete and erroneous AI outputs are an anticipated feature of this methodology, not a defect. AI error functions as a diagnostic signal: it identifies domains where the intellectual terrain is genuinely contested, where scholarly consensus has been manufactured rather than earned through evidence, and where deeper primary-source investigation is warranted.

How to Use This System

Workflow Sequence

1.

Initiate with the Inquiry Seed. Formalize the research question and document initial epistemic commitments prior to any AI consultation.

2.

Execute the AI Prompt Engine phase. Record outputs without evaluation; collection precedes adjudication.

3.

Proceed to the Research Workspace. Trace AI-surfaced leads to primary sources. Cite all evidence with full attribution and record empirical findings as stated in the source literature.

4.

Construct the Contradictions & Resolution document. Surface all source disagreements explicitly. Inhabit irresolution; do not collapse it prematurely.

5.

Maintain the Quotes, Domains, & Links library continuously. Each inquiry unit contributes to the cumulative reference archive.

The five-document structure must be replicated for each inquiry unit. This architecture is not procedural bureaucracy; it is the epistemological scaffolding that prevents AI-augmented research from collapsing into AI-generated opinion presented as independent scholarly judgment.

"Truth is rarely found where everyone agrees. It is found where disagreement is sharpest and still survivable."

This research exists because the perceived weight of civilizational decline demands more than affective response — it demands empirical investigation conducted under disciplined epistemic constraints. The method requires sustained engagement with disconfirming evidence before synthesis is attempted.

That is its function.
