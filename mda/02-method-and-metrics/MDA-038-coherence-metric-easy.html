Abstract

This paper introduces the Coherence Metric (chi), a way to measure structural integrity using physics. We show that the same math governing critical phenomena in physical systems also describes decay patterns across nine independent social domains. Analysis of data from 1900–2025 shows synchronized threshold-crossing events clustered within the 1968–1973 window. This points to a unified underlying mechanism, not separate decay processes. The framework gives falsifiable predictions and measurement criteria that stand independent of political interpretation.

Keywords: coherence • phase transitions • critical phenomena • social physics • structural integrity

1. Introduction

The question this paper addresses is simple: Can physics measure moral decay?

Not metaphorically. Not as analogy. As measurement.

The claim is that structural integrity follows the same math whether it's in bridges, bodies, or civilizations. When constraints are removed, systems don't gradually decline. They hold apparent stability until a critical threshold. Then they collapse rapidly. This is not opinion. This is phase transition physics.

The political interpretation is irrelevant. You can vote for gravity or against gravity. Gravity does not care. It pulls.

The Coherence Master Equation

This equation says: coherence (chi) is determined by how far the control parameter (P) is from the critical threshold. The further from the threshold, the more coherence you have. Cross the threshold, and coherence collapses. The same structure governs superconductors, ferromagnets, and civilizations.

2. The Synchronization Problem

If coherence decay were random, the inflection points would be spread across the 125-year observation window. Divorce rates, crime rates, obesity rates, trust metrics, and educational outcomes would each respond to separate causes. Their turning points would be scattered.

They are not.

Between 1968 and 1973, nine independent metrics crossed their critical thresholds within a 5-year window.

The probability of this occurring by chance is vanishingly small (p < 10⁻⁶). This is the signature of a system failure, not a collection of unrelated problems.

3. Methodology

3.1 Data Sources

Nine domains using longitudinal datasets from 1900–2025. Sources: CDC, Pew, Gallup, NORC GSS, BGSU NCFMR, BLS. See Appendix A for the full source list.

3.2 Normalization

Z-score transformation to a 1940–1949 baseline. This lets us compare across domains on a common coherence scale.

3.3 Curve-Fitting

Nonlinear least squares regression with bootstrap confidence intervals (n = 1,000 iterations) to the power-law form chi ≈ |P − Pc|^beta.

4. Results

Key Findings by Domain

Familial

Divorce rate peaked at 22.6 per 1,000 (1981). Non-marital births rose from 10.7% (1970) to 40% (2023). Marriage covenant as a constraint has been removed from law and culture.

Institutional Trust

Public trust in government fell from 77% (1964) to 16% (2023). That is an 80-percentage-point collapse in six decades. Constitutional constraint removal preceded the decline.

Semantic Coherence

74% of virtue-related words show declining frequency in English-language text after 1960 (Google Ngram analysis, UPenn Language Log). Language is the medium of shared meaning. When definitions become unfixed, communication degrades toward noise.

Economic Coherence

Dollar purchasing power: $1.00 (1971) to $0.14 (2024). Nixon's closure of the gold window cut the constraint anchoring currency to physical reality. The pattern maps perfectly to the coherence equation with the gold standard as Pc.

8. Discussion

The 1968–1973 Constraint Removal Cluster

Synchronized inflection across nine domains demands a common cause. The 1968–1973 window produced a clear cluster of constraint-removal events in law and culture. These are not interpretations. They are dates.

1965

Griswold v. Connecticut — Decoupled contraception from the state's interest in marriage, weakening the familial constraint structure.

1968

Vietnam / Civil Unrest — Institutional authority collapsed publicly. Trust in government began its terminal decline from 62% toward 16%.

1970

No-Fault Divorce — California's Family Law Act removed permanence from the marriage covenant. Divorce rate began its vertical climb.

1971

Gold Window Closure — Nixon severed the dollar's constraint anchor. Currency decoupled from physical reality. $1.00 became $0.14 over 53 years.

1972

Eisenstadt v. Baird — Extended contraceptive access to unmarried individuals. This completed the legal decoupling of sexuality from covenant.

1973

Roe v. Wade — Removed the final institutional constraint on the consequences of decoupled sexuality. Nine domains show synchronized inflection within this window.

Limitations

1.

Correlation is not causation. Synchronization is statistically anomalous. But the causal mechanism needs further formal analysis beyond Granger causality.

2.

Measurement validity. Domain operationalizations involve index construction choices that affect results. Alternative operationalizations should be tested.

3.

beta universality requires replication. The mean beta = 0.41 is consistent with known universality classes. But it requires independent replication across additional longitudinal datasets.

The Coherence Metric gives us a falsifiable, measurement-based framework for evaluating structural integrity across domains. The synchronized collapse pattern in 1968–1973 points to a unified mechanism—constraint removal—not independent decay processes.
