Extended Boundary Model

Where the derived chain becomes the Terminal Observer, Grace Operator, Master Equation, and modeled worldview filter.

Scope of This Page

This page is not the foundation. It is the interpretive and integrative layer built after the axiom hierarchy and derived chain claims. It names the Terminal Observer, defines grace as a repair/sign-flip operator, places the ten laws inside the Master Equation, and explains the Christianity boundary-condition result as a modeled filter.

Terminal Observer

BOUNDARY MODEL
The Observation Chain Terminates
The framework models a terminal observer rather than an infinite regress of observers.
The theological identification is that this terminal observer is God. The formal role is structural: terminate the chain, ground observation, and prevent regress. The claim is strongest when presented as property matching and framework closure, not as Lean proving God by declaration.

Grace Operator

DEFINITION / OPERATOR MODEL
Grace as External Repair
Grace is modeled as an external non-self-generated repair/sign-reversal operator that preserves identity while changing orientation.
This keeps the theological point and the formal point aligned: the system cannot save itself from inside its own decay/sign structure. The repair comes from outside, but the coupling is not coercive.

The Ten-Law Coherence Integral
χ = ∭ (G · M · E · S · T · K · R · Q · F · C) dx dy dt

Worldview Boundary Filter

MODELED FILTER
Christianity 8/8 Boundary Conditions
Within the modeled eight-boundary-condition filter, Christianity is the named candidate satisfying the encoded conditions.
This keeps the claim strong and properly scoped. The page may say the framework's modeled filter identifies Christianity as satisfying all eight encoded conditions.
