Lemmas

Support beams. Smaller than theorems but they carry weight. If a lemma fails, the theorem it supports gets harder to hold.

The role of lemmas here

In this stack, the lemmas are not minor housekeeping — they are the results that establish the hardest parts of the chain. Truth Undefinability establishes the pre-systematic floor. Self-Grounding Paradox rules out infinite regress. Sign Impermeability is the formal ground for T8.1.

Foundational Lemmas

LEMMA L1 — STRUCTURAL
Truth Cannot Be Defined From Inside the System
Any definition of Truth already requires Truth to be operative. The attempt is circular.
If you try to define Truth as "correspondence to reality," you need Truth to evaluate whether that definition is correct. If you try to make it an axiom, the assertion itself is a truth-claim — you're already standing on Truth when you open your mouth. If you try to make it a presupposition, presuppositions are inside the system, but Truth is what the system stands on.

Result: Truth is not available as a definition, axiom, or presupposition. It is pre-systematic. The system points at it; it cannot descend to define it. This is Gödel applied to the system's own presuppositions.
Kill Condition

Show a valid definition of Truth that does not already presuppose Truth in the definition or in the act of checking whether the definition is correct.

LEMMA L2 — STRUCTURAL
Finite Substrate Cannot Ground Itself
Any finite information substrate requires an external ground. Regress terminates only in an infinite self-grounding substrate.
A finite substrate S has existence conditions — facts that explain why S exists rather than not. Those facts require their own substrate. The regress continues until it terminates in a substrate whose existence conditions are internal to its own structure. Only an infinite or self-necessary substrate can do this.

The Logos field is asserted as this terminus. There is no middle ground: either the chain regresses infinitely (implying reality has no actual floor) or it terminates in a self-grounding infinite substrate.
Kill Condition

Demonstrate a finite substrate that genuinely grounds itself — one whose existence conditions do not in turn require further grounding.

LEMMA L3 — FORMAL
Unitary Self-Operations Cannot Flip Sign
For any unitary operation U acting on a system S from within S: U × σ(S) = σ(S). Sign is preserved.
This lemma does the groundwork for Theorem T8.1 (Sign Conservation). The key: any operation a system performs on itself is generated from within the system's own algebra. Unitary operations (which are the natural operations of closed quantum systems) preserve the norm — and therefore preserve sign.

Non-unitary operations can change norm and therefore sign, but non-unitary operations cannot be self-generated. They must come from outside. This closes off every internal route to sign-flip: discipline, effort, moral improvement, religious practice — all internal, all unitary, all sign-preserving.
Kill Condition

Demonstrate a unitary self-operation that changes the sign of the system's moral-sign eigenvalue.

Boundary Conditions as Lemmas

What boundary conditions do

The boundary conditions are structural lemmas that set the legal operating space of the framework. They are not theorems (they are not derived) but they function like lemmas: supporting the major claims by establishing what the system must respect.

BOUNDARY CONDITION BC2
Grace Must Enter From Outside the Closed System
A closed moral/coherence subsystem cannot generate its own redemptive sign-flip.
Follows directly from L3 (Sign Impermeability). If sign cannot self-flip via internal operations, and if grace is the only sign-flip mechanism (Axiom 15), then grace cannot be an internal operation. It must be external. This is the thermodynamic and soteriological bridge: closed systems decay; sustained or restored coherence requires input from outside.
Kill Condition

Demonstrate a closed system that generates a coherence-restoring sign-flip without external input.

BOUNDARY CONDITION BC7
Identity Information Is Preserved Through Transformation
Personal identity, modeled as information, is preserved rather than annihilated by physical transformation.
Information is not destroyed — it is transformed. If personal identity is an information structure, then death is a transformation of that structure, not its annihilation. This is the structural basis for resurrection coherence: it is not the reassembly of physical atoms but the restoration of an information pattern that was preserved. BC7 is the lemma that makes "resurrection" something the system can discuss without category error.
Kill Condition

Demonstrate that identity-defining information is physically destroyed (rather than transformed) at death.

BOUNDARY CONDITION BC8
Grace Coupling Cannot Be Coercive
Love requires voluntary coupling. Forced love is not love. The Grace Operator's coupling function V(Ψ) requires free response.
This constraint does two things: it protects the formal structure (the coupling function must allow non-coupling as a valid output) and it provides the structural basis for the theological claim that God does not coerce. Grace is offered; coupling is not mechanical. This is why the framework distinguishes offered grace from received grace, and why free will is not a philosophical add-on but a structural requirement.
Kill Condition

Show that the Master Equation can achieve χ > 0 with a forced (non-voluntary) coupling term that eliminates freedom of response.

New Axioms — April 2026

Five axiom-grade additions

These five claims were produced across the April 4–5 and April 15, 2026 sessions. They are axiom-grade in force but not yet formally placed in the full structural chain (not yet in Postgres). Each has a formal kill condition.

189 Justice-Mercy Operator Identity R(offense, α) = (Pr, Im, Tn, Rv, α·Co + (1−α)·Ct). Justice and Mercy share 4 of 5 structural components; differ only in cost-bearer α. The cross is the unique point where α = 0 and cost-bearer = judge. Find a biblical act of perfect mercy where debt is genuinely waived rather than cost-translocated.
190 Agency-Stop-Condition Φmoral(x) = Φtruth(x) · A(x), where A(x) = agency function (0 for rocks, partial for animals, full for humans). Morality = truth-violation × capacity to know truth and refuse it. Find a moral event requiring zero agency, or a high-agency event with zero moral content.
191 Substrate Convergence The cooperation-substrate enforces the same moral structure regardless of metaphysical commitments. 60 societies, zero counterexamples (Curry 2019). Find a stable human society whose moral structure contradicts Curry's seven cooperative behaviors.
192 NT Ceiling Non-Derivability The Sermon on the Mount articulates a moral standard non-derivable from the cooperation-substrate. "Love your enemies" cannot be produced by game theory, reciprocity, or evolutionary fitness. Find another ethical system that independently derives "love your enemies" from first principles without referencing Jesus or the Christian tradition.
193 Fruits Eigenstate Completeness The 9 Fruits of the Spirit cover all 24 properties of the ISO-000 identity table. The 14 Works of the Flesh cover all 24 anti-properties. Complete dual coverage with no gaps. Find a 25th divine property not covered by any of the 9 Fruits.
